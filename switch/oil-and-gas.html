Common questions

Can oil and gas workers get renewable energy jobs?

Yes. Field, electrical, construction, operations and engineering experience all carry over, and employers in solar, wind, storage, geothermal and hydrogen hire from oil and gas. Right now 58 employers have 1,326 open roles on Verde Talent in the job families on this page.

Which clean energy jobs pay the most for oil and gas workers?

Of the roles on this page, the highest median advertised pay is in geothermal engineering ($140,000), project development and land ($120,000) and project management ($110,800). These are what employers advertise, not offers made, and postings without a published range aren't counted.

Where are the most openings?

The most open roles today are in solar, wind and storage construction (738), solar, storage and grid technician (428) and wind technician (233).

Do I need new certifications?

Often not to get started - many employers train on their own equipment. The ones that come up most are GWO Basic Safety Training for wind, NABCEP PV Associate for solar and NFPA 70E for electrical safety.
