Before you get out

Use what you've earned

  • SkillBridge - work with a civilian employer for up to your last 180 days of service, still on military pay.
  • GI Bill - can reimburse approved licensing and certification test fees, not just degrees.
  • Translate your job - write your duties in civilian words (“maintained 400 kW generators”, not your MOS code). Employers search by skill.

Common questions

Do renewable energy employers hire veterans?

Yes. Right now 102 postings on Verde Talent from 17 employers name military experience or training as a qualification - the most from NovaSource Power, First Solar and Dominion Energy. Electrical, mechanical, operations, construction and logistics experience all carry over.

Which clean energy jobs pay the most for veterans?

Of the roles on this page, the highest median advertised pay is in project management ($110,800), safety and EHS ($103,400) and supply chain and procurement ($67,500). These are what employers advertise, not offers made.

Where are the most openings?

The most open roles today are in solar, wind and storage construction (738), solar and storage technician (399) and wind technician (233).

Can I use the GI Bill for clean energy certifications?

The GI Bill can reimburse the fees for approved licensing and certification tests, and can pay for approved training programs. Check what's approved for your benefit with the VA before you book.

Can I start before I leave the service?

DoD SkillBridge lets service members spend up to their last 180 days of service working with a civilian employer while still on military pay. Ask employers whether they take SkillBridge participants.
