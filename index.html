A job board built specifically for clean energy

Generic job boards like Indeed and LinkedIn bury clean energy roles under millions of unrelated postings, and their filters aren't built for this industry. Verde Talent only lists jobs from solar, wind, battery storage, grid, EV infrastructure, and hydrogen employers — real open roles from companies like NextEra Energy, Vestas, Sunrun, First Solar, and Bloom Energy — so every search result is actually relevant, no sifting through retail, hospitality, or software listings to find the handful of energy roles mixed in.

Whether you're searching for solar installer jobs, wind turbine technician jobs, battery storage engineer roles, EV charging infrastructure positions, or grid and utility engineering openings, Verde Talent organizes postings by sector and role so you can filter down to exactly what you're qualified for — across all 50 states, with dedicated filters for remote and international roles too.

We also show real salary ranges on postings whenever employers disclose them, and back every listing with our own Intelligence data — compensation benchmarks, hiring trends, and fill-difficulty by role, sourced from the same live postings you're browsing. Create a free candidate profile and employers hiring in solar, wind, storage, and grid can find and reach out to you directly.
