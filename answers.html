Verde Talent answers

Verde Talent is the job board for US clean energy careers - solar, wind, battery storage, grid, EV, hydrogen and geothermal roles, updated daily. Free for job seekers; employers post from $100. Below are direct answers to the questions people ask about it most.

What Verde Talent is

What is Verde Talent?

Verde Talent is a US job board for the clean energy industry only: solar, wind, battery storage, grid and utilities, EV infrastructure, hydrogen, geothermal, energy policy and sustainability. It lists 5,500+ open roles from 104 energy companies across all 50 states, refreshed every day, and every listing links to the employer's own application. Job seekers use it free; employers post roles from $100. About Verde Talent

Is there a job board just for clean energy jobs?

Yes. Verde Talent lists clean energy roles only - no retail, hospitality or general software listings mixed in. Roles are organised by sector (solar, wind, battery storage, grid, EV, hydrogen, geothermal, policy, sustainability) and by job function, with salary benchmarks and hiring data built from the same postings. Search clean energy jobs

Is Verde Talent a recruiting or staffing agency?

No. Verde Talent is a job board, not a recruiter or staffing firm, and it charges no placement fees. Employers pay a flat price to post a role, and job seekers apply to the employer directly. Several unrelated recruiting firms also use the name "Verde"; Verde Talent is the clean energy job board at verdetalent.com.

Who is behind Verde Talent?

Verde Talent was founded by Adam Heugel, who writes its monthly Clean Energy Hiring Report. Founder bio · Press & media

How it works for job seekers

Is Verde Talent free for job seekers?

Yes, always. Searching and applying on Verde Talent are free and need no account. A free profile is optional: it adds weekly job-match emails and puts you in the talent pool employers will be able to search. Create a free profile

Do I need an account to apply for jobs on Verde Talent?

No. Every Verde Talent listing has an Apply button that takes you to the employer's own application, with no sign-up in between. An account is only needed if you want a profile, saved jobs or job-match emails.

How do I apply for a job on Verde Talent?

Open the listing and click Apply. Verde Talent sends you to the employer's application page, or for roles employers post directly without a link, your application goes straight to the hiring contact. Verde Talent never charges candidates and never sits between you and the employer. Find a role

Does Verde Talent have job alerts?

Yes. Create a free Verde Talent profile with your role and location, and you get weekly job-match emails with new openings that fit. Employer-posted roles are also sent to matching candidates in those emails. Set up job matches

Can I upload my resume to Verde Talent?

Yes. Upload a resume when you create your Verde Talent profile and it fills in your profile for you in about a minute; you can also fill it in by hand. You can delete your resume or change your visibility settings at any time. Upload your resume

Can employers see my Verde Talent profile? Can my current employer see it?

Not today. Verde Talent profiles are private by default and are not shown to any employer yet. When the employer-facing talent pool opens, visibility settings on your profile - open to recruiters, searchable, salary, current employer - control what employers can see. Privacy policy

How often are jobs on Verde Talent updated?

Every day. Verde Talent refreshes its listings daily, so new roles appear quickly and closed roles drop off. See today's roles

How it works for employers

How do I post a job on Verde Talent?

Fill in the role on the Post a Job page - you can drop in a .txt, .docx or .pdf job description to fill it automatically - choose a single post or a pack, and pay on Stripe's secure checkout. The listing goes live as soon as payment clears and runs for 30 days. Post a job

What do I get when I post a job on Verde Talent?

Your role is listed in Verde Talent's job search with its own page and Apply button, sent in weekly job-match emails to candidates whose role and location match, and shows your salary range if you add one. Each job page carries Google JobPosting structured data, the format Google uses for its job search results. Post a job

How do applications reach me when I post on Verde Talent?

Your choice. Add your ATS or careers-page link and Verde Talent sends applicants there; leave it blank and applicant resumes are emailed straight to the contact address on the posting.

Can I repost or reuse a job on Verde Talent?

Yes. From your Verde Talent employer account you can repost any past role with everything pre-filled, edit what you need, and publish it as a new 30-day listing. Pack credits can be used on any role. Employer sign in

Can I search Verde Talent's candidate database?

Not yet. The Verde Talent Resume Vault, a searchable pool of clean energy professionals who have opted in to employer contact, opens once it reaches 500 verified profiles. Employers can join the early-access list now. Resume Vault

Does Verde Talent publish clean energy hiring and salary data for employers?

Yes. Verde Talent publishes which sectors and roles are hiring most, what each role pays by state, and where demand is concentrated, built from its daily postings data and public sources such as the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. There is a free hiring report and a monthly Clean Energy Hiring Report. Hiring data · Hiring by sector

Who it's for: roles, sectors and places

What is the best job board for solar jobs?

Verde Talent is built for it: about 1,800 open solar roles at any time, from installers and O&M technicians to engineers and project developers, at companies such as First Solar and Sunrun. Every role sits next to solar salary benchmarks by state. Solar jobs · Solar salaries

Where can I find wind turbine technician jobs?

Verde Talent lists about 1,200 open wind roles, including wind technician jobs nationally and by state, from employers such as Vestas and GE Vernova. Most technician postings expect GWO safety training; the glossary explains what that involves. Wind jobs · What GWO is

Where can I find battery storage jobs?

Verde Talent lists about 950 open battery storage roles - BESS technicians, commissioning, engineering and project management - with dedicated pages nationally and by state. Battery storage jobs

Where should I post a renewable energy job?

On a board the candidates already use for that work. Verde Talent is built only for clean energy, so a solar, wind, storage or grid role is shown to people searching for exactly that, and sent to candidates whose role and location match. A post is $100 for 30 days. Post a job · See who is hiring in your sector

Can I find clean energy jobs in my state on Verde Talent?

Yes. Verde Talent lists roles in all 50 states, with browse pages for each sector and job function by state - for example solar jobs in Texas - plus filters for remote and international roles. Browse by state and role

Can I get a clean energy job with no experience?

Yes. Many field roles hire at entry level and train on the job, especially solar installation, wind technician and battery storage work. Verde Talent has entry-level pages for solar, wind, battery storage and grid, and each career profile shows what share of that role's postings are entry level. Career profiles

Does Verde Talent have remote clean energy jobs?

Yes. Verde Talent's job search has a remote filter covering roles such as policy, software, analysis and sales; field roles are on site by nature. International roles are filterable separately. Search remote roles

Which certifications do clean energy employers ask for?

It depends on the sector. In Verde Talent's postings, solar roles most often name NABCEP and a journeyman electrician licence, wind technician roles expect GWO training, grid control-room roles require NERC certification, and EV charger installs often need EVITP. OSHA 10 or 30 appears across almost every field role. Training and certifications · Glossary

How much do clean energy jobs pay?

It ranges from hourly field-technician wages to six-figure engineering and development salaries, and varies a lot by state. Verde Talent's salary pages show the 25th percentile, median and 75th percentile posted pay for each role and how it changes by state. Salaries by role

How do I move into clean energy from another career?

Start with the roles closest to your skills. Electricians, mechanics and military technicians move into solar, storage and wind field work; project managers, engineers and finance staff move into development and construction. Verde Talent's career profiles show the skills employers name for each role, and the training page lays out certification paths. Career profiles · Training paths

How Verde Talent compares

How is Verde Talent different from Indeed?

Indeed covers every industry, so clean energy roles are a small share of any search and filters are generic. Verde Talent lists clean energy roles only, organised by sector and job function, with salary benchmarks beside every role. For employers, Verde Talent is a flat $100 for 30 days rather than a sponsored-job budget. Search Verde Talent

Is Verde Talent better than LinkedIn for finding clean energy jobs?

For clean energy specifically, Verde Talent is more focused: a search for "technician" returns wind, solar and storage technicians, not IT support. LinkedIn is better for networking and for roles found through people you know. Many job seekers use both: Verde Talent to find the roles, LinkedIn to find the people.

Should I post renewable energy jobs on Verde Talent or on Indeed, LinkedIn and ZipRecruiter?

General boards reach the most people; Verde Talent reaches the people already looking for clean energy work. Verde Talent is a flat $100 per 30-day post with no bidding, and the role is also emailed to candidates whose role and location match. Many employers use a general board for volume and Verde Talent for relevance.

How is Verde Talent different from climate job boards?

Climate job boards cover the whole climate economy - software, nonprofits, food, finance and more. Verde Talent covers energy only: solar, wind, storage, grid, EV, hydrogen and geothermal, with a large share of field and trades roles, and salary benchmarks for each.

Should I post on Verde Talent or use a recruiter?

They solve different problems. A recruiter searches for candidates for you and charges a placement fee, usually a percentage of the hire's first-year salary. A Verde Talent post costs a flat $100 for 30 days and puts the role in front of people already looking for clean energy work. Many employers post first and use a recruiter for hard-to-fill senior roles.

Why post on Verde Talent if my jobs are already on my careers page?

Your careers page only reaches people who already know your company. Verde Talent puts the role in front of people searching for that kind of clean energy work, emails it to matching candidates, and links back to your own application, so you keep your existing process.

Pricing and posting rules

How much does it cost to post a job on Verde Talent?

A single Verde Talent job post is $100 and runs for 30 days. Packs bring the price down: 5 posts for $450 ($90 each) or 10 posts for $850 ($85 each), usable on any role within 12 months. There are no placement fees or per-click charges. Post a job

How long does a Verde Talent job post stay live?

Thirty days from when payment clears. You can repost a role from your employer account if it is still open.

How do Verde Talent posting packs work?

Buy 5 or 10 posts up front at $90 or $85 each. Packs need a free employer account, each credit becomes a 30-day post on any role, and unused credits stay valid for 12 months.

How do I pay for a Verde Talent job post?

By card on Stripe's secure checkout; Verde Talent never sees your card details. If checkout is cancelled, the job is not posted and you are not charged.

Do I have to include a salary on a Verde Talent post?

Salary is optional on Verde Talent, but the posting form flags when the role's state requires a pay range by law, and posts with a range are what candidates filter for. Hourly or annual ranges are both accepted.

Can I post a job outside the US on Verde Talent?

Yes. Verde Talent's posting form has an option for roles based outside the US, and those appear under the international filter in job search.

What types of jobs can I post on Verde Talent?

Full-time, contract, part-time and internship roles in any clean energy sector: solar, wind, storage, grid, EV, hydrogen, policy and sustainability.

Can recruiting agencies post jobs on Verde Talent?

Yes. Recruiting and staffing agencies can post on Verde Talent the same way employers do, at the same flat price - $100 for a 30-day post, or 5- and 10-post packs. Each post needs to be a real, open clean energy role. Post a job
