Common questions

Which clean energy companies sponsor H-1B visas?

The clean energy employers on Verde Talent with the most H-1B petitions approved by USCIS in fiscal year 2026 are NextEra Energy (138); GE Vernova (92); Siemens Gamesa / Siemens Energy (88); Hitachi Energy (77); Exelon (65); PG&E (51). All 44 are listed above, with their open roles.

Does a sponsorship record mean a specific job will sponsor me?

No. It means the company has sponsored H-1B workers before. Each role is decided separately, and field and entry-level roles are sponsored far less often than engineering and specialist roles. Read the posting and ask the employer before you apply.

How many clean energy job postings say they sponsor visas?

Very few. As of November 15, 2026, 0 open postings on Verde Talent say sponsorship is offered and 1,055 say it is not; the rest don't say. That's why this page starts from employers' USCIS records instead.

Where does this data come from?

Employer records are from the USCIS H-1B Employer Data Hub, fiscal year 2026 (approved initial and continuing-employment petitions). Whether a posting offers sponsorship is read from the posting's own wording. Jobs are refreshed daily.
