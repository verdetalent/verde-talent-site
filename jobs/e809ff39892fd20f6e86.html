Position Specific Description

Position Summary

NextEra Energy Resources is seeking a Principal IIoT Platform Engineer to provide technical leadership for the Industrial IoT infrastructure supporting one of the industry's largest renewable energy telemetry platforms. The platform ingests telemetry from hundreds of wind, solar, battery storage, and distributed generation facilities, processing more than 100 billion Industrial IoT events per day while delivering highly available data services for operational technology (OT), enterprise analytics, AI, and business applications.

This role serves as a senior technical leader for the Industrial IoT platform, responsible for the design, operation, and continual improvement of the Ignition, HiveMQ, and AWS infrastructure that powers the Sequoia platform. The Principal Engineer will provide technical leadership, mentor engineers, establish engineering standards, lead architecture decisions within their domain, and solve complex platform challenges while partnering with other Principal Engineers across the Sequoia ecosystem to deliver a secure, resilient, and scalable Industrial IoT platform.

What Success Looks Like

Success in this role is measured by the reliability, scalability, security, and operational maturity of the Industrial IoT platform. The Principal Engineer will help ensure the platform continues to evolve through automation, engineering standards, resilient architecture, and technical mentorship while supporting one of the largest Industrial IoT environments in the utility industry. This individual will play a key role in enabling a platform that reliably processes more than 100 billion Industrial IoT events per day while supporting the continued growth of renewable energy operations and the broader Sequoia ecosystem.

Job Overview

Employees in this position ensure that solutions developed on domain expert platforms conform to application strategy and ensure high availability on multimillion dollar, complex, mission critical and high volume transactional systems for the business unit. This position includes, but is not limited to, writing, coding, testing, analyzing, configuring, debugging and documenting software programs and applications.
