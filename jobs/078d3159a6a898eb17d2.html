Job Description:

Role Overview

Fluence is looking for an IT Service Desk Intern to support and enhance internal

IT support services. This role provides hands-on experience in a professional IT

environment, working with Microsoft 365, ServiceNow, and enterprise

infrastructure.

Responsibilities

  • Provide technical support for hardware and software in a Microsoft 365
  • environment
  • Assist in setting up and deploying desktop computers (Windows and Mac)
  • Support user account management activities in Active Directory
  • Help with procurement and distribution of IT equipment
  • Assist with Office 365, SharePoint, and ServiceNow-related tasks
  • Contribute to and maintain knowledge base documentation in ServiceNow
  • Support hardware and software inventory management
  • Assist in troubleshooting audio/video conferencing systems
  • Support administration of core hosted applications
  • Collaborate with local IT vendors and internal teams

Key Qualifications & Experience

  • Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in IT, Computer Science, or
  • a related field
  • Basic understanding of Microsoft Windows and/or Mac OS
  • Familiarity with Microsoft 365 (Office applications, Teams, Outlook)
  • Interest in IT support, troubleshooting, and end-user services
  • Basic understanding of networking, device management, or identity
  • management is a plus
  • Strong problem-solving skills and willingness to learn
  • Good written and verbal communication skills
  • Ability to work on-site in the Erlangen office

What You’ll Gain

  • Hands-on experience in an enterprise IT environment
  • Exposure to tools like ServiceNow, Active Directory, and Microsoft 365
  • Opportunity to work with experienced IT professionals in a global company
  • Practical knowledge of IT support processes and systems
