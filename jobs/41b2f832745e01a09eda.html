Full description
Eversource will not offer immigration-related sponsorship for this position (e.g., H-1B, O-1, J-1, TN, E-3, etc.). Applicants requiring visa sponsorship to start employment with Eversource will not be considered. The System Reliability and Resiliency Planning Manager provides strategic direction and leadership for continuous System performance, including developing and deploying tools and analytics that support Eversource’s goal of providing exceptional customer service. The Manager will lead a multi-faceted team to optimize distribution reliability investments across the Company’s footprint using historical and predictive analytics. The Manager will also lead assessment of distribution resiliency needs across the system and develop targeted, granular plans and programs to harden the system and reduce customer interruptions, interruption minutes, equipment damage and restoration costs during major and catastrophic storms, as well as during other man-made and natural major events that could disrupt electrical distribution service. The Manager will drive fundamental changes in the way we study and assess distribution reliability and resiliency needs, the way we develop and deploy solutions for resiliency improvement, and how we apply equipment and technology to ensure service continuity during major events. The Manager will lead the team to develop best-in-class analytics and tools to identify system deficiencies, improve system design, efficiently deploy equipment, optimize spending, reduce equipment, operation and customer costs, and develop and report metrics, scorecards and performance incentive mechanisms (PIMs). The Manager takes a leadership role in development of the Company’s Resilience metrics and driving the necessary regulatory changes for adoption in regulatory rate case proceedings.
