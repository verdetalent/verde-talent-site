Full description

At Bloom Energy, our vision for a world powered by clean, reliable, and affordable energy is more than just a dream—we’re making it reality.

For over two decades, we’ve been at the forefront of the global energy transition, pioneering solutions that empower critical industries to thrive in a rapidly digitizing, energy-intensive world. From revolutionizing power for AI-driven data centers to ensuring resilience for hospitals, electric grids, manufacturing facilities, and utilities, our solid oxide fuel cell (SOFC) and solid oxide electrolyzer (SOEC) technologies are redefining what’s possible by delivering energy abundance for all. With more than 30,000 fuel cell modules deployed worldwide, we are the trusted partner for Fortune 100 companies and innovators alike. Our cutting-edge solutions enable unparalleled “time-to-power” capabilities, reliability, and sustainability, ensuring our customers remain ahead in a world where soaring energy demand and intensifying energy scarcity are rapidly becoming the new norm.

At Bloom, we thrive on collaboration, bold thinking, and relentless innovation. We believe that, together, we can create a brighter, more sustainable future while tackling the most pressing challenges of the 21st century.

We are looking for a Senior Manager of Global Securityto join our team in one of today’s most exciting technologies.  This role will report tothe Vice President – Global Facilitiesand will be based in San Jose, California.This is a fully on-site, in office role 5 days a week.

TheSenior Director – Worldwide Facilities and Real Estatewill help develop and implement security, policies, procedures, and programs throughout all Bloom Energy locations. The role will be a key member of Bloom Energy’s Facilities and Real Estate leadership team and will collaborate closely with several disciplines, including Human Resources, Finance, Information Technology, Supply Chain, and Legal.

Role and Responsibilities:

  • Oversee Security operations globally
  • Develop and review global security standards, programs, and SOP’s.
  • Manage all security vendors against SLAs and KPIs.
  • Develop security awareness training programs.
  • Assess update and manage security systems for all new and existing locations globally.
  • Manage physical security requirements 24/7, including development and management of a global access program.
  • Partner with the Facilities and Real Estate team, Finance Operations, Information Technology, Engineering, Supply Chain, and HR to understand security requirements.
  • Select service providers and vendors who can support Bloom Energy’s goals with integrity and cost effectiveness.

Required Skill & Experience:

  • 8+ years experience in security management
  • Bachelor’s degree
  • Experience in a global corporate security role
  • Strong analytical and critical thinking skills
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills with the ability to communicate effectively at all levels of the organization
  • Ability to collaborate with others
  • Ability to source and maintain vendors capable of supporting and servicing a high growth multi-national company.
  • Ability to simultaneously deliver results for a large number of complex projects and programs on very tight timetables.
  • Ability to manage through ambiguity and build a strong internal network.

Desired Skills & Experience:

  • CPP, PSP or APP professional designation
  • Experience with site security assessments and surveys
  • Experience with access control systems
  • Familiarity with FTZ certifications
  • Experience in managing and delivering workplace violence programs in a corporate environment
  • Experience with physical security design specifications and standards
