Position Specific Description The Business Analyst I supports account-level research, case management, reporting, audit support, and process discipline for EverBright Revenue Protection workflows. This role helps the team manage Salesforce case assignments, postponement/deferment and contract modification requests, UCC and ABS reporting, legal account assistance, and recurring analytics used to monitor portfolio performance and operational controls. Summary Employees in this role support the Revenue Protection organization through account research, cross-functional coordination, high-quality documentation, data validation, and recurring reporting. The Business Analyst partners with Legal, Compliance, Data Analytics, Business Management, Customer/Operations partners, and external servicing stakeholders to resolve account issues, support audits, and improve recurring workflow. Job Overview This position blends operational execution with analytical support. The Business Analyst reviews customer and account-level information, prepares accurate stakeholder responses, validates data outputs, maintains process documentation, and supports reporting packages related to delinquency, modifications, write-offs, recoveries, UCC filings, and financing/audit requests. Success in this role requires attention to detail, comfort working across systems, clear written communication, adherence to documented procedures, and the ability to manage both recurring deliverables and time-sensitive ad hoc requests.
