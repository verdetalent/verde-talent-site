About NovaSource

NovaSource Power Services is the world’s #1-ranked solar operations and maintenance (O&M) provider and insight-driven total asset optimization partner for renewables asset owners ready to fuel smart growth. With over 20 years of operating experience and a presence on 5 continents, NovaSource has the global reach and strategic capabilities to achieve our clients’ renewables goals around the world.

NovaSource’s comprehensive approach to total asset optimization in addition to O&M services includes value engineering, performance analysis, strategic supply chain management, and advanced monitoring systems. The company operates in key global markets managing over 30GW of solar power plants. NovaSource’s expertise extends beyond solar and includes battery energy storage systems (BESS), offering a complete suite of services for the evolving renewable energy landscape.

Position Summary

The Substation Services Technician performs specialized testing, diagnostics, maintenance, installation, and commissioning support on medium- and high-voltage electrical equipment supporting utility-scale renewable energy facilities and substations.

This position is responsible for executing advanced electrical testing, performing preventative and corrective maintenance, troubleshooting equipment issues, supporting planned and emergency outages, and providing technical recommendations to improve asset reliability and performance.

The ideal candidate has experience with substation equipment, transformer diagnostics, electrical testing, and utility work practices. Experience with Doble testing equipment, protective relays, and substation commissioning is highly desirable.

This position requires travel throughout North America (approximately 75–80%) in support of customer projects.

Primary Responsibilities

Electrical Testing & Diagnostics

Perform diagnostic testing and condition assessments of substation equipment including:

  • Transformer Turns Ratio (TTR)
  • Sweep Frequency Response Analysis (SFRA)
  • Excitation Current Testing
  • Capacitance Testing
  • Power Factor / Dissipation Factor Testing
  • Bushing Capacitance & Power Factor Testing
  • Insulation Resistance (Megger) Testing
  • Contact Resistance (Ductor) Testing
  • Medium-Voltage Cable Insulation Testing
  • Transformer Condition Assessments
  • Diagnostic Test Analysis & Interpretation

Preventive & Corrective Maintenance

  • Conduct mechanical inspections
  • Torque verification
  • Equipment removal and installation
  • Equipment replacement
  • Electrical troubleshooting
  • Failure investigations
  • Lead and support planned outage
  • Emergency response
  • Perform maintenance activities on:
  • Generator Step-Up (GSU) Transformers
  • Medium-Voltage Transformers
  • Disconnect & Hook Switches
  • Capacitor Bank Switchers
  • Bushings
  • Medium-Voltage Cable Systems
  • Electrical Connections & Terminations

Field Operations

  • Coordinate subcontractors and specialty vendors
  • Support commissioning and return-to-service activities
  • Develop detailed field reports
  • Document test results and maintenance findings
  • Provide technical recommendations for repair or replacement

Safety & Compliance

  • Perform all work in accordance with OSHA 1910.269, NFPA 70E, NESC, and company safety procedures.
  • Conduct Job Hazard Analyses (JHAs) and support a strong electrical safety culture.
  • Follow Lockout/Tagout, grounding, and switching procedures.
  • Maintain company quality and documentation standards.

Experience

  • High School diploma or equivalent
  • 5+ years of medium- and high-voltage electrical experience

  • 2+ years of experience testing and maintaining transformers, disconnect switches, and substation equipment
  • Experience interpreting electrical test results and equipment condition

Preferred:

  • Transformer Testing Training
  • Protective Relay Test Sets NETA certification
  • SEL, ABB, GE, Siemens, or Schweitzer relay training
  • Utility and/or renewable energy substation experience
  • Independent electrical testing company experience
  • Commissioning experience

Technical Knowledge
