Job Description

Position Overview

As a Senior HR Generalist at Invenergy, you will support a multi-state population of 1,000+ employees in all areas of Human Resources such as employee relations, policy development and application, performance management, employment law compliance, and department development.

This is an on-site position at Invenergy's downtown Chicago office.

Responsibilities

  • Conduct investigations on employee, customer and vendor complaints, including, but not limited to, harassment, discrimination and violation of company policies.
  • Liaison between management and employees by handling questions and interpreting and administering policies.
  • Assist with the development and administration of programs, procedures, and guidelines to help align the workforce with the strategic goals of the company.
  • Develop and implement human resource policies and procedures, including maintaining employee handbooks. Provide recommendations to policy or procedure changes accordingly.
  • Maintain compliance with local, state and federal employment law keeping the HR team apprised of any changes.
  • Analyze and evaluate the impact of proposed legislation and rule changes; propose policy/procedural changes as necessary.
  • Assist and counsel managers on human resources and employment issues.

Minimum Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree
  • 5+ years of experience in Human Resources, preferably in an HR Generalist or similar position
  • Experience leading complex investigations
  • Strong working knowledge of multi-state and federal employment laws and practices
  • Excellent communication, interpersonal and coaching skills
  • Evidence of the practice of a high level of confidentiality
  • Proficiency using HRIS systems and Microsoft suite
  • Ability to travel up to 15%
  • Eligible to work in the United States without the need for employer visa sponsorship now or in the future.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Workday experience
  • Experience in a manufacturing, safety sensitive, and/or multi-state, multi-location environment
