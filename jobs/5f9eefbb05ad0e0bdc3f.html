Eversource is looking for an Associate Planner & Scheduler II, Project Controlsin Westwood, MA. As the Associate Planner & Scheduler II, Project Controls, you will plan, schedule, manage and report on the Transmission Organization: Capital Projects, Maintenance Projects, and Distribution Projects managed by Transmission across the Eversource projects portfolio.  You will complete tasks associated with establishing, maintaining, analyzing, and reporting of costs and schedules associated to project activities contained in the overall project schedules.  Perform the following in coordination as needed with more senior team members, obtain, and provide information as well as implement recommendations to meet project goals and positively influence project direction as well as assist project managers, project team stakeholders and Transmission & Engineering Controls & Contracts in determining department resource solutions for meeting project cost and schedule objectives.  Develop an understanding and begin to apply project management theories and methodologies and project control tools.

Essential Functions:

Perform the following with assistance as needed from more senior team members for:

  • Develop, maintain, analyze, and manage project activities and resources to the level necessary to support project and department requirements and needs.
  • Establish, monitor, and report on baselines for cost and schedule.
  • Assist in determining internal and external engineering and construction resource solutions for meeting project objectives.
  • Identify internal and external engineering and construction resources in the schedule and any constraints on current projects and future demands.
  • Communicate project status and resource loading with team members and management.
  • Identify and communicate project plan changes and advise team members and management of potential conflicts and solutions.
  • Expedite project plan changes and communicate internal and external reallocation of engineering and construction resources to accommodate changes and conflicts.
  • Evaluate project forecasts to assure desired results are achieved.
  • Develop alternative solutions for resolving potential project resource constraints, schedule conflicts and cost overruns.
  • Participate in intra and inter departmental activities to assure adherence to schedule and established guidelines.
