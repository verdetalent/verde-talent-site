Job Description

Position Overview:

As a Shop Technician, you'll be part of an exciting opportunity to work in a brand new facility, where you'll play a key role in refurbishing wind turbine components. This position is essential to our operations, giving you the chance to contribute to the success of our team while maintaining the highest standards of quality and delivering exceptional customer satisfaction.

Responsibilities:

  • Safely operate all shop equipment.
  • Capable of operating welding and cutting equipment and completing metal fabrication projects.
  • Read and comprehend technical drawings and bill of materials.
  • Disassemble and reassemble wind turbine components.
  • Ensure accurate and complete quality documentation is maintained and signed off in a timely manner.
  • Maintain company tooling, facilities and equipment and a clean safe work environment.
  • Assure all activities are performed in a safe manner consistent with Invenergy policies and procedures.
  • Assist with flatbed shipment unloading/offloading.
  • Assist with inventory management.

Shop Technician 1 Required Skills

  • High School Diploma or equivalent
  • 6 months to 3 years of technical experience (mechanical, electrical, etc.) and/or technical education.
  • Knowledge of oxy/acetylene cutting processes
  • Computer proficiency with Microsoft Office software.
  • Positive attitude and a strong work ethic.
  • Strong attention to detail, accuracy, and safety
  • Excellent problem-solving skills and the ability to adapt to changes on short notice.
  • Ability to move and manipulate up to 45 kg (100 pounds).
  • Must be able to climb ladders
  • Valid driver’s license and acceptable driving record.
  • Eligible to work in the United States without the need for employer visa sponsorship now or in the future.

Shop Technician 1 Preferred Skills

  • Experience operating overhead cranes.
  • A degree in a technical discipline is preferred.
  • Experience operating forklifts
  • MIG/TIG welding
