Full description

Becoming a more process-driven finance organization is a key part of our finance transformation. In this role, you will work in a high-visibility area where you can make a real impact and drive meaningful change.

The Global Finance Process Specialist (BPS) position has the responsibilities and qualifications defined as follows:

Responsibilities:

  • Ensure finance processes are well‑documented and accurately reflect how Finance operates in practice.
  • Partner with the Global Finance Process Owners (GFPO) to support the implementation of new and amended financial processes globally.
  • Work closely with the finance community to improve processes, making them more efficient, consistent, and simple across the organization.
  • Integrate internal controls, financial guidelines, and global policies into finance processes to ensure compliance and a “right-first-time” approach.
  • Drive improvements in efficiency, consistency, and standardization of financial processes and controls. Incorporate recommendations from audits to strengthen processes and governance.
  • Enhance Finance’s understanding of process requirements by driving awareness and engagement through user forums and other communication channels.
  • Ensure process maps and SOPs are up to date and documented according to Vestas Document Management Systems (DMS), Vestas Process Portal (VPP/ARIS).
  • Ensure proper governance and quality of process documentation, including regular reviews and compliance with the annual update cycle.

Qualifications:

  • Relevant Bachelor’s or Master's degree within Finance, Controlling or similar from a good University
  • Approximately 5 years' work experience from finance in an international company
  • Certified Public Accountant is an advantage
  • “Process excellence”, “Quality assurance”, “Applying Process data analytics”, “Applying Lean and Six sigma to financial processes” is an advantage
  • Experience with Finance Process mapping and improvements is an advantage
  • Strong knowledge and experience within SAP S/4HANA are required
  • Ability to assess risks on a given process and suggest value adding control activities to reduce risks to an acceptable level is a requirement
  • Experience with Business/Financial controlling in complex environments is an advantage
  • Strong communication skills and fluency in English is a requirement
  • Good Stakeholder Management skills
  • Ability to execute end to end process mapping workshops with line of business

The successful candidate has the following characteristics:

  • A structured and organised way of working
  • Ability to interact with colleagues at all levels in and outside the organisation
  • An excellent team player and good at keeping close to stakeholders
  • Ability to keep the overview in a hectic and dynamic environment
  • A cross-functional mindset
  • Pro-active, open-minded and self-motivated
  • Reliable, service-minded and result-oriented
