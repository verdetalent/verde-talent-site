Full description
The Specialist, Service Commercial Contracting, is a hybrid role responsible for project managing and negotiation of complex Vestas service contracts, including new service contracts and renewals, and transactional sales contracts, ensuring compliance with internal Vestas contract requirements on cost control and risk management and incorporating best practices and lessons learned. The Specialist then manages a regional portfolio of service contracts to facilitate operational compliance and guide issue resolution involving input and feedback from multiple operational, technical, commercial, and legal individuals across the organization. Proficient contract interpretation skills, drafting capabilities, and be solution minded. Responsibilities: • Support Service Sales by project managing the negotiation of Vestas service contracts, including direct negotiation with customers from internal kick-off meetings through handover to Operations. • Develop a position on all terms and conditions during negotiation, ensuring that stakeholders' input and concerns are appropriately represented. • Support a Service Operations Region by identifying contractual and commercial approaches and negotiation tactics to avoid or resolve customer disputes; help execute the chosen strategy through drafting and obtaining internal approvals of letters, notices, position papers and amendments throughout the contract lifecycle. • Manage the negotiation process: liaise with customer representative, set the timeline, arrange and manage meetings, and coordinate internal agreement on resolution. • Ensure that all contracts and other legal instruments are consistent with Vestas internal guidelines for acceptable commercial commitments (or other risk policies) and optimize Vestas' ability to perform to the contract and mitigate customer disputes. • Maintain contracts database that captures life-cycle requirements for easy reference and management of operational and commercial obligations. •...
