About Redwood Materials Redwood is localizing a global battery supply chain that seamlessly integrates recovery, reuse, and recycling — keeping critical minerals in circulation and driving the energy transition. Founded in 2017, we’re delivering low-cost and large-scale energy storage and producing battery materials in the U.S. for the first time, all from batteries we already have. ERP Solutions Architect The ERP Solutions Architect will play a central role in the design, implementation, and ongoing support of the company's next-generation ERP platform. The core financial system will be built on Microsoft Dynamics 365, with additional modules developed in-house. This role is primarily focused on configuring financial systems and processes in D365 and serves as a key liaison between internal software development team Responsibilities will include: Participate in the end-to-end implementation of a new ERP platform, with primary ownership of financial modules including requirements gathering, configuration, testing, and go-live support. Partner with Finance and Accounting SMEs to design and document workflows, business processes, and system configurations that support accurate and efficient financial operations. Collaborate closely with internal software development teams to translate business requirements into technical solutions for in-house ERP modules. Support the configuration and administration of Microsoft Dynamics 365 financials, including period-end close, accounting distributions, AP/AR processes, and related financial workflows. Develop and maintain custom reports and data extracts to support financial reporting and period-end validation. Administer ERP security access controls including role design, user provisioning, and segregation of duties compliance.
