Job Description:

This is an onsite position, and we are seeking candidates who live within a reasonable commuting distance of the Fluence office and are available to work onsite four days per week.

Role Overview

The Product Manager – Gridstack Pro (GSP) Product Lines role is responsible for the success and support of Fluence’s legacy GSP and Gridstack product lines. This role requires cross functional management of the needs and requirements across our major markets.

Key Responsibilities

As the Product Manager – Gridstack Pro Product Lines you will:

  • Ownership:Own Fluence’s product offerings to include identification and evaluation of ideas from a business and technical feasibility standpoint with cross-functional support from Fluence’s Growth team, Regional Service and Technology teams, as well as development of commercial offerings.
  • Product Strategy: Develop and execute a comprehensive product strategy for Platform Products, addressing the unique needs of the Fluence’s different regions (Americas, EMEA, APAC) and other customers. Drive initiatives to improve project availability and expand the product portfolio.
  • Market Analysis: Support and consume market research by working with teams worldwide to identify trends, customer needs, and competitor activities, translating insights into actionable product enhancements that span all markets.
  • Stakeholder Collaboration: Work closely with cross-functional teams, including Engineering, Supply Chain, Program, Growth, and Customer Success, to communicate product changes and ensure successful implementation. Support the Customer Success teams by supporting upgrades and improvements.
  • Product Lifecycle Management: Oversee the entire product lifecycle for new features, in field and factory changes from conception through development to end-of-life strategies, ensuring timely delivery of enhancements and updates.
  • Communication: Responsible for communicating the strategy, roadmap, and value propositions of Fluence’s after-market capabilities to both external and internal stakeholder.
  • Regulatory Compliance: Work with the compliance team to ensure that all product modifications comply with relevant industry standards and regulations, maintaining our commitment to safety and sustainability.

Required Qualifications & Experience

  • The ideal candidate will have a strong understanding of the unique needs of deployed products and the teams that support them as well as an understanding of how the needs of these products can be converted into customer fulfillment opportunities.
  • Bachelor’s Degree or equivalent in engineering, physics, or other technical discipline. Most critical is an educational and work history background demonstrating competencies in analytical thinking encompassing both technical and commercial strategy.
  • Possesses strong interpersonal communications skills to coordinate with diverse internal and external teams worldwide.
  • Takes individual initiative and works with minimal supervision while being a collaborative team player.
  • Works dynamically, across multiple teams and projects concurrently, in a technical business environment.
  • Possesses an entrepreneurial drive for getting things done and a “whatever it takes” attitude.
  • Experience in workflow tools such as JIRA.
  • Experience in and knowledge of Agile development methods.
  • Experience working with Supply Chain and procurement teams.
  • Ability to utilize data and statistical analyses to inform decisions.
  • Periodic time zone flexibility to support customers and colleagues across the world
  • A desire to make the world a better place through technology.
