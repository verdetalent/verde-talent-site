SUMMARY

Mortenson Engineering Services is currently seeking an Engineer III – Protection & Controlto join our team.  This position is responsible for the technical control design on a broad range of detailed engineering designs for high voltage substation projects through 500 kV. Candidate will be responsible for working closely with other engineering and design team members to coordinate overall design package and requirements. They will be responsible for performing a broad scope of engineering assignments of moderate difficulty requiring knowledge in engineering-related disciplines.  With some technical oversite, the engineer will learn to evaluate, select, and apply standard engineering techniques, procedures, and criteria by using judgment in making adaptations and modifications and providing interpretations and conclusions with support from other Engineers.

Hiring Locations: Minneapolis, MN, Fargo, ND, Denver, CO, or San Antonio, TX offices

RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Responsible for the relay protection & control engineering aspects on substation and transmission design projects.
  • Duties include one line design, schematic design, panel elevations, wiring diagrams, and system troubleshooting.
  • Review and approve equipment vendor drawings on major equipment from manufacturers with Lead Engineer or Manager review and approval.
  • Intermediate understanding of discipline specific technical designs, engineering principles, business standards, practices, procedures, and product / program requirements.
  • Works independently with high level direction from Engineering Lead, Principal Engineer, or Engineering Manager.
  • Provide engineering support for proposals, estimating, procurement and construction.
  • Coordinate with other engineering disciplines and Design Phase Managers to successfully deliver design projects.
  • Provides technical support to personnel/cross functional teams supporting the assigned project by performing moderate level engineering calculations, data analysis, and engineering tasks.
  • Assist field personnel in resolving unanticipated site conditions.
  • Strong analytical problem-solving skills.
  • Assure proper documentation of technical data generated for the assigned projects and/or tasks consistent with engineering policies and Mortenson QA/QC procedures.
  • Creates detailed designs aligned with our customer requirements and Mortenson’s best practice standards.
  • Plans and conducts work requiring judgment in the evaluation, selection, and adaptation or modification of standard techniques, procedures, and criteria.
  • Review of engineering work produces a first-time review rate of documents at 75% correct.
  • Performs data analysis and engineering tasks, calculations and studies to develop preliminary and detailed designs for completion of large and small projects.
  • Develops, under supervision, the technical requirements and standards for fabrication and construction tender packages.
  • Has basic knowledge of software packages: ASPEN, ETAP, PSSE, CDEGS, is desirable but not required.
  • Previous experience with relay settings desirable but not required
  • Moderate Project Management and analytical problem-solving skills.

QUALIFICATIONS:

  • Bachelor’s or graduate degree in Electrical Engineering or related Engineering program
  • 4+ years of related work experience in the Transmission & Distribution (T&D) industry
  • Experienced in power system protection & control design including one line, schematic, and wiring diagram design. Substation-specific experience preferred
  • Experienced in electrical power engineering theories and principles
  • Demonstrated experience in practices of researching engineering and design issues, evaluating alternatives, making sound recommendations, and preparing and presenting recommendations
  • Excellent written and oral communication skills
  • Engineering-in-Training (EIT) certification, required -or- Professional Engineering (PE) license, preferred
