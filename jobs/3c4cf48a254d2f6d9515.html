Mobile Wind Site Manager - Route 66, TX

Vestas
Panhandle, TX Full-time Manager+
WindSalesforce
Applying takes you to Vestas's own careers site to complete your application.
Full description

About Us:Vestas is a global leader in wind energy, committed to sustainable energy solutions. We pride ourselves on our innovative and safety-focused approach to renewable energy.

Job Overview:The Mobile Site Manager will be tasked with the management of several service sites across a specific region, aiming to maintain smooth operations, high levels of customer fulfillment, and compliance with safety standards. This role requires significant travel, as you will be assigned to various sites as needed.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Safety and Compliance: Establish and oversee the implementation of safety practices and protocols. Ensure compliance with Vestas’s organizational standards and safety regulations.
  • Operational Effectiveness: Cultivate a culture of safety, structure, and continuous improvement. Communicate change management effectively to stakeholders.
  • Team Leadership: Lead and develop a team of Service Technicians. Ensure team members are compliant with safety training and certifications. Develop internal and external partnerships.
  • Customer Relations: Interface with customers, address inquiries, and collaborate with internal departments to resolve issues. Ensure all contractual obligations are met.
  • Financial Management: Manage site budgets, cost allocations, and operational revenues. Input and ensure accurate data in systems like Salesforce and SAP.
  • Continuous Improvement: Develop and deliver continuous improvement plans. Implement LEAN practices at the site.
  • Technical Assistance: Provide technical support to Service Technicians for troubleshooting and resolving issues. Ensure team adherence to work instructions and propose improvements.
  • Travel Requirements: This is an 80% travel position. You may be assigned to any service site within your designated region and occasionally to sites in other regions based on business needs.

Qualifications:

  • Minimum 3 years of wind experience.
  • Valid driver’s license required; ability to obtain a passport is beneficial.
  • Experience in P&L management.
  • Experience managing safety programs.
  • Advanced computer operation and internet-based program navigation skills.
  • Commitment to Vestas’ core values of diversity, equity, inclusion, and belonging.

Physical Demands

The physical demands described here are representative of those that are met by an employee, to effectively perform the essential functions of this role. Reasonable accommodations may be made to enable team members with disabilities to perform the essential functions.

  • Ability to stand and walk for prolonged periods of time.
  • Hearing ability to use telephones, close range radios or related device.
  • Visual acuity including depth perception, field of vision and the ability to distinguish between colors.
  • Ability to stoop, kneel, crouch or crawl as needed, as well as hand-eye coordination and manual dexterity.
  • Ability to climb stairs & vertical ladders to heights of 60 to 100 meters and at times supervise work in confined space environments.
  • Ability to lift, push, pull, carry items up to, but not more than 50 lbs. in weight.
  • Ability to observe training and obtain certification in required safety programs that include LOTO, Electrical, Fall Arrest & High Angle Emergency Decent, and Confined Space.
  • Ability to participate in all training courses, including controlled decent practical exercises.
  • Due to safety parameters of fall protection equipment, the total weight of required PPE and gear shall weigh 300 lbs or less while being worn. Required PPE and gear can weigh up to 40 lbs.

About Vestas

Vestas is the energy industry’s global partner on sustainable energy solutions. We are specialised in designing, manufacturing, installing, and servicing wind turbines, both onshore and offshore.

Across the globe, we have installed more wind power than anyone else. We consider ourselves pioneers within the industry, as we continuously aim to design new solutions and technologies to create a more sustainable future for all of us. With more than 185 GW of wind power installed worldwide and 40+ years of experience in wind energy, we have an unmatched track record demonstrating our expertise within the field.

With 30,000 employees globally, we are a diverse team united by a common goal: to power the solution – today, tomorrow, and far into the future.
