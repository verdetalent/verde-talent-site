Join Vestas and oversee the risk management framework that supports some of the world's most important renewable energy projects. In this strategic role, you will manage the standardization, governance, and continuous improvement of risk processes across a global organization, enabling smarter decision-making and sustainable growth. If you are committed to risk management, stakeholder engagement, and creating lasting business value, this is your opportunity to help improve risk practices.
