Position Specific Description We are building a next-generation People Analytics function focused on delivering predictive, AI-enabled workforce intelligence at scale. This role will be foundational in architecting and building the data infrastructure that powers advanced analytics, AI use cases, and strategic workforce decision-making across the enterprise. This position designs, develops, and maintains workforce data solutions that support People Analytics, workforce intelligence, and AI-enabled decision-making capabilities. Employees in this role are responsible for building and optimizing data pipelines, integrated workforce data models, semantic layers, and analytics-ready datasets that enable trusted reporting, self-service analytics, predictive insights, and future AI applications. The role partners with HR, analytics, technology, and business teams to deliver scalable data products and ensure the quality, accessibility, and reliability of workforce data. Responsibilities: Designs, develops, and maintains workforce data pipelines and integrations across HR, talent, operational, and enterprise systems Builds and enhances analytics-ready workforce data models, semantic layers, calculation views, and governed datasets Develops and maintains workforce data solutions within an SAP ecosystem (SuccessFactors, SAP HANA, S/4HANA, Power BI), while partnering with broader IT and technology teams to evaluate and leverage additional data platforms in support of AI-enabled use cases.
