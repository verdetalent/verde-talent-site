Full description

Description and requirements

If you want to shape the future of energy, we are looking for a Regional HSEQ Operational Manager to join our HSEQ team!

Who you will work with

The position, while coaching and mentoring less senior unit members, will define and The HSEQ Operations Regional Manager is responsible for the overall management and implementation of Health, Safety, Environment, and Quality (HSEQ) programs across the assigned region for Wind, Solar, Energy Storage, and Demand Response operations. This position provides consultative support to senior executives, O&M Regional Managers and O&M Field Support teams with the goal of promoting a culture rooted in safety, environmental responsibility, quality excellence, and compliance. The role ensures alignment with company values, regulatory obligations, and international standards including ISO 45001, ISO 14001, ISO 9001.

This position manages a team of HSEQ professionals and partners closely with O&M leadership to drive continuous improvement, support incident prevention, and ensure consistent application of the Integrated Management System (IMS).

What you will do

Responsibilities include, but are not limited to:

Strategic Leadership & Oversight

* Lead the North American HSEQ function across Wind, Solar, Storage, and Demand Response operations in the assigned region.

* Manage and mentor O&M HSEQ Regional Managers and staff and ensure effective support across all assigned plants.

* Align North American HSEQ strategy with national and global corporate goals and initiatives.

* Promote and maintain the company's HSEQ values through strong leadership and presence in the field.

Program Development & Compliance

* Develop, maintain, and implement HSEQ programs and compliance plans aligned with ISO 14001, ISO 37001, ISO 45001, ISO 9001, and local regulations.

* Define, implement, and continuously improve Emergency Plans at plant/area/site levels.

* Ensure execution of safety and environmental risk assessments and compliance with relevant legal and regulatory requirements.

Training & Cultural Development

* Support and implement training programs to enhance employee awareness and compliance with HSEQ standards.

* Oversee selection, use, and training of Personal Protective Equipment (PPE).

* Foster a proactive and participative safety culture through coaching, education, and leadership presence.

Incident Management & Auditing

* Lead investigations into incidents and near misses, ensure timely reporting, root cause analysis, and implementation of corrective actions.

* Conduct and oversee field inspections, safety observations, and compliance audits.

* Maintain consistency in data collection and reporting across the region through company systems (e.g., SAP).

Supplier & Contractor Oversight

* Ensure HSEQ compliance among contractors and vendors in line with company standards and Integrated Management Systems.

* Verify implementation of safety protocols during field activities and contractor operations.

Data, Reporting & Administration

* Coordinate regulatory and internal HSEQ reporting and ensure timely, accurate submissions.

* Maintain data integrity in HSEQ systems and support reporting for budgeting, labor, and resource allocation.

* Act as a buyer for HSEQ-related procurement needs in coordination with the procurement team.

Cross-functional Collaboration

* Work collaboratively with Operations teams to develop site-specific safety programs and conduct hazard analyses.

* Participate in cross-departmental projects, special initiatives, and companywide improvement campaigns.

* Maintain strong relationships with local, state, and federal regulatory agencies.

Qualifications

* Bachelor’s degree in Safety, Environmental Sciences, Engineering, or a related field (or equivalent experience).

* 5+ years of experience in HSEQ roles within power generation or industrial operations; at least 2 years in a leadership capacity.

* Demonstrated expertise with ISO-based management systems, auditing, and regulatory compliance.

* Experience with SAP or similar systems for HSEQ reporting (preferred).

* Proven ability to influence across levels, work independently, and lead multidisciplinary teams.

Skills and Competencies

* Good initiative, strategic thinking, and continuous improvement mindset.

* Ability to communicate clearly, train others, and lead by example.

* Effective organizational, analytical, and decision-making skills.

* Superior written and verbal communication abilities.

* Demonstrated alignment with company core values:

* Trust

* Proactivity

* Innovation

* Responsibility

What you will bring

The minimum requirements for this role are:

* BS in Safety, Environment, or related work experience

* Minimum of 10+ years of related experience

* Thorough knowledge of applicable regulations, practices, and computer skills

* Strong initiative and drive for high achievement and continuous improvement

* Ability to be on call or work off hours as needed

* Demonstrated ability to be a team player with passion, drive, and the ability to self-start

* Ability to influence through peer relationships

* Availability for local, domestic, and international travel; up to 50%

* Experience with safety management systems/quality systems, (SAP a plus)

* Ability to be on your feet walking around the facility at least 50% of the day. Climb ladders/stairs and lift as required to perform necessary job tasks.

* Superior communications, interpersonal, and organizational skills required

*

While performing the duties of this job, employee may be exposed to reasonable hot/cold ambient weather conditions prevalent at any given time

Ability to climb turbine towers 100+ meters in height if needed (although not likely to occur regularly)

Comfortable working at heights of 300 feet or more in confined spaces and the ability to perform essential physical functions of the job with or without reasonable accommodation; this includes working in scissor lifts, lifting 50 lbs. on a daily continuous basis sometimes overhead, meeting the weight requirement of safety gear, 310 lbs. or less., and working in PPE such as arc flash suits/facemasks and high voltage gloves for extended periods of time

Who we are:

Enel North America:

Enel North America is a proven renewables leader delivering clean, flexible and sustainable energy solutions.

As part of the Enel Group, we develop, build, own and operate renewable power plants and demand response solutions, with over 11 gigawatts (GW) of installed wind and solar capacity, over 1 GW of energy storage and nearly 5 GW of demand response in the US and Canada.

For nearly 25 years, we've reliably powered modern life and driven climate action with our people, partners and communities by putting sustainability at the center of everything we do. Enel is a top-five industry leader for clean power capacity in the US, demand response in North America and utility-scale battery storage in Texas.

We are a smart and passionate team working together to build the Enel North America that we want for the long-term - one that is founded on strong financial, social and environmental values. Being on our team means being part of lasting progress to create a thriving and more sustainable world for our climate and communities. It means valuing safety, trust, innovation, proactivity, flexibility and respect in all we do. Our vision is ambitious, and we'll get there together.

Enel Spa

Enel is a multinational power company and a leading integrated player in the global power and renewables markets, present in 27 countries worldwide.

At global level, it is the largest renewable player, the foremost electricity distribution network player by number of grid customers served and the biggest retail operator by customer base

Enel generates electricity with approximately 93 GW of total capacity. Enel Green Power, the Group’s renewables arm, has a total capacity of approximately 68 GW and a generation mix that includes wind, solar, geothermal, and hydroelectric power, as well as energy storage facilities, installed in Europe, the Americas, Africa, Asia, and Oceania.

Enel Grids, the Group’s global business line dedicated to the management of the electricity distribution service worldwide, delivers electricity through a network of 1.9 million kilometers with more than 69 million end users connected to its distribution grids. Enel Commercial is the Group’s arm dedicated to customers around the world with the aim of effectively providing products and services based on their energy needs and encouraging them towards a more conscious and sustainable use of energy. It provides electricity, gas and integrated services to more than 29 million customers globally including households, enterprises, industries and public administrations. In addition, it offers flexibility services aggregating 10.2 GW and has over 34,000 owned public charging points for electric mobility.
