Full description

SOLV Energy is a leading provider of infrastructure services to the power industry, designing, building and maintaining utility scale solar, battery storage and high voltage substation projects nationwide.

Job Description Summary:

The High Voltage Preconstruction Project Manager is responsible for leading all preconstruction activities for high-voltage electrical infrastructure projects, including substations, transmission lines, renewable energy interconnections, battery energy storage systems (BESS), and industrial power distribution systems. This role manages project planning, estimating, scheduling, budgeting, constructability reviews, procurement strategy, risk assessment, and client coordination to ensure successful project execution from concept through construction handoff.

This role is fully remote. Specific location details and expectations will be discussed during the interview process.

Job Description:

*This job description reflects management's assignment of essential functions; it does not prescribe or restrict the tasks that may be assigned

Position Responsibilities and Duties:

Preconstruction Leadership

  • Lead preconstruction efforts for high-voltage electrical projects ranging from 12kV through 500kV+ systems.
  • Oversee project planning, budgeting, scheduling, and constructability reviews.
  • Develop project execution strategies that align with client objectives and company goals.
  • Coordinate preconstruction deliverables from project inception through construction turnover.

Estimating and Cost Management

  • Prepare conceptual, budgetary, and detailed cost estimates.
  • Analyze engineering designs, specifications, and bid documents.
  • Review labor productivity, equipment requirements, and material pricing.
  • Develop quantity takeoffs and identify cost-saving opportunities.
  • Maintain estimating databases and market pricing trends.

Client and Stakeholder Management

  • Serve as the primary preconstruction point of contact for clients, utilities, consultants, and internal stakeholders.
  • Participate in project pursuit and proposal presentations.
  • Facilitate project kickoff meetings and stakeholder alignment sessions.
  • Build and maintain strong client relationships throughout the project lifecycle.

Risk Assessment and Constructability

  • Identify project risks related to scope, schedule, procurement, labor, permitting, and site conditions.
  • Lead constructability reviews and value-engineering initiatives.
  • Develop mitigation strategies and contingency plans.
  • Review project execution plans for safety, quality, and operational efficiency.

Schedule Development

  • Collaborate with engineering, operations, and procurement teams to develop detailed project schedules.
  • Identify long-lead equipment requirements, including transformers, breakers, switchgear, and protection systems.
  • Monitor critical path activities and project milestones.

Procurement and Vendor Management

  • Support procurement strategy development.
  • Solicit and evaluate vendor and subcontractor proposals.
  • Develop bid packages and scopes of work.
  • Analyze supplier capabilities and delivery schedules.

Cross-Functional Coordination

  • Collaborate with engineering, operations, finance, procurement, and business development teams.
  • Ensure alignment between project estimates, schedules, and execution plans.
  • Facilitate smooth handoff from preconstruction to construction operations.

Minimum Skills or Experience Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Construction Management, Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Project Management, or related field preferred.
  • Equivalent industry experience may be considered.
  • 7+ years of experience in electrical construction, high-voltage projects, estimating, or project management.
  • 3+ years of experience leading preconstruction activities preferred.
  • Experience with substations, transmission lines, utility-scale solar, wind, BESS, or industrial electrical projects.
  • Experience managing projects exceeding $10M preferred.
  • Familiarity with:
  • High-voltage substations
  • Transmission and distribution systems
  • Utility interconnection requirements
  • Electrical codes and industry standards
  • EPC and design-build delivery methods
  • Ability to interpret engineering drawings, specifications, and contract documents.

Software Skills

  • Microsoft Office Suite
  • Microsoft Project or Primavera P6
  • Bluebeam
  • Accubid, InEight, HeavyBid, or similar estimating platforms
  • Procore or equivalent construction management software

Key Competencies

  • Leadership and team development
  • Strategic planning
  • Cost estimating and budgeting
  • Schedule management
  • Risk analysis and mitigation
  • Contract review and negotiation
  • Client relationship management
  • Communication and presentation skills
  • Problem-solving and decision-making

Performance Metrics

  • Estimate accuracy
  • Proposal win rate
  • Budget adherence
  • Schedule development accuracy
  • Risk identification and mitigation effectiveness
  • Client satisfaction
  • Successful project turnover to operations

Physical Requirements

  • Ability to travel to project sites, client meetings, and utility facilities as required.
  • Ability to work in office and field environments.
  • Occasional exposure to construction and industrial jobsite conditions.

Applicants must be legally authorized to work in the U.S. without requiring employer sponsorship now or in the future.
