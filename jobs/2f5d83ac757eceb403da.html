We are proud to announce the launch of the Vestas Blades Process Engineering Factory in Goleniów – a new organization where we will develop, build, and invest in the future of wind turbine blade production. Our aim is to create a world-class engineering hub with robust processes and capabilities, delivering consistent, high-quality support across all manufacturing sites while setting new standards in manufacturing excellence Vestas Technology & Operations > Manufacturing > Blades PE Layup The Documentation team is responsible for creating and maintaining production documentation, including Production Work Instructions (PWI), Process and Product Inspection (PII) documents, as well as updating generic inspections in our QDA system standards. The department also ensures proper maintenance of PFMEA, working closely with process engineers to secure accurate inputs. As part of the Lay-up Department, the Documentation team will consist of 3–5 engineers specializing in manufacturing process documentation.
