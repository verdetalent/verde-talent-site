Our Team

We have an amazing opportunity to join our Substation Civil Engineering & Design Team.  The Engineer in this role will work with complex systems and equipment, while providing engineering and technical services to support our growing organization. We offer a unique and exciting opportunity to explore the best of both worlds, working both in the office and in the field on a variety of interesting projects. Areas of expertise include designing new substations and infrastructure, analyzing systems and disruptions to service, working with transformers, engineering the electrical protection and control circuits that handles the flow of power, and dealing with acceptance testing of equipment, failures of equipment and analysis of system disturbances.

In this strategic role, the Engineer is responsible for the development, expansion, and design of the system and its equipment to meet the mission and objectives of the organization.  Responsible for civil, structural and site development designs and calculations for electrical substations and ensuring that the Eversource transmission and distribution systems are designed to high quality standards that provide customers with safe, reliable, competitively priced and environmentally sound electric service. This position shares accountability with peers in the strategy, development, and design of the organization’s comprehensive long-term plan for the transmission/distribution system.

The role is based in Berlin, CT

Essential Functions

  • Design and analysis of substation site infrastructure for high‑voltage electrical facilities, including structural steel, foundations, rigid bus systems, site grading, stormwater management, oil containment, and substation sanitary and water services, with emphasis on structural steel, foundations, and rigid bus design.
  • Assist in the development and approval of project scopes, specifications, engineering designs, details, material ordering, and cost estimates.
  • Adhere to project schedules, including detailed planning of successive construction phases to effectively implement designs while accounting for outage durations and maintaining realistic timelines.
  • Coordinate with Substation Engineering and Design to communicate project details and ensure all system additions, removals, and modifications are properly designed for safe operation and maintenance.
  • Communicate effectively, demonstrate strong computer proficiency, and produce clear, concise written documentation.
  • Perform root‑cause analysis and support the development and implementation of corrective actions.
  • Review engineering vendor submittals and participate in vendor meetings to address comments and questions throughout the design and review cycle.
  • Review all construction drawings and design documentation prior to issuance to construction contractors.
  • Ensure all work complies with Eversource standards and applicable Codes.
  • Verify the accurate design and specification of additions, modifications, and replacements to transmission and distribution systems in accordance with project schedules.
  • Provide Civil Engineering support during construction, including resolving field questions and approving field‑generated changes to issued designs.
