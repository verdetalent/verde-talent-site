Full description

Digital Solutions and Development > Vestas Development> Development Commercial Europe

In this role, you will not just crunch numbers; you will directly shape investment decisions. You will own the financial architecture of our renewable energy pipeline and run the analytical engine behind high-profile M&A processes, working closely with local project teams and senior stakeholders to enhance project value.

Responsibilities

  • Financial Modelling & Valuation: Build, optimize, and own complex financial models from early-stage project screening through to Ready-to-Build (RTB) status
  • Economic Assessment: Evaluate project economics and risk profiles using probability-weighted DCF and project finance frameworks
  • Asset Lifecycle Updates: Continuously update and refine valuations as projects progress through permitting, grid connection, and technical milestones
  • Commercial Structuring: Support early-stage structuring and origination of new pipeline opportunities (including feasibility studies, Joint Ventures, and DSAs)
  • M&A Execution: Run the analytical backbone of divestment and acquisition processes, managing investor lists, NDAs, Teasers, CIMs, Virtual Data Rooms (VDR), and Q&A coordination
  • Stakeholder Engagement: Prepare high-quality Investment Committee presentations and commercial materials for senior management
  • Business Partnering: Work directly with country Project Managers on budget allocation, prioritization, and value-creation decisions

Qualifications

  • Solid experience in financial modelling and valuation within Project Finance, Investment Banking, M&A, or Renewable Energy Development
  • Experience building and manipulating complex Excel-based valuation models from scratch (a financial modelling exercise will be part of the selection process)
  • Deep knowledge of corporate finance, DCF methodology, and financial statements
  • Familiarity with the renewable energy sector and the development lifecycle (Greenfield to RTB) is highly advantageous
  • Fluency in English (written and spoken) is required, as you will operate in an international environment

Competencies

  • Commercial Judgment: Ability to evaluate data carefully and convert it into concise, actionable business conclusions
  • Accountability: Maintains an organized approach, pays close attention to detail, and ensures work is accurate and reliable
  • Agility: Comfortable managing multiple workstreams simultaneously across different markets and development stages
  • Effective Communicator: Professional and concise when engaging with project managers, external counterparties, and senior executives
  • Flexibility: Practical and solution-oriented when responding to uncertainty or evolving project variables: Pragmatic and solution-oriented when dealing with uncertainty or shifting project variables
  • Motivated: Operates with little supervision and performs well in a lean, collaborative work environment

  • Megawatts (MW) advanced through the main development milestones, from Screening to Development to RTB.
