Basic Job Functions: Material Handler I is responsible for the safe, efficient, and accurate handling of materials in the warehouse and/or shipping dock environment. This role involves receiving, storing, cycle counting, and distributing materials, tools, equipment, and products within the establishment. Material Handler I ensures that the right products are delivered to the right location on time and in good condition, contributing to the smooth and efficient operation of the supply chain. Engages in packaging, loading, unloading, storing, and transporting materials and products by performing the described duties. Ensures the correct Oracle transactions are performed for goods receipts and item issuances out of inventory.
