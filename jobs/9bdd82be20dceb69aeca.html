Invenergy is North America’s largest privately held developer, owner, and operator of power infrastructure. With 25 years of trusted execution, we deliver reliable, affordable energy through a diverse portfolio that includes natural gas, solar, land-based wind, energy storage, transmission, and domestic manufacturing. Headquartered in Chicago, we develop, own, and operate large scale projects that power communities and support the energy future. This position will be open for application for at least 3 calendar days from the posting date. This position will remain open for application based on business need, which may be before or after the 3-day posting window. Job Description Corporate Recruiter The Corporate Recruiter supports growth by leading the full lifecycle recruiting process for our operating business corporate roles. Operating business corporate hiring includes high volume hiring of Operations & Maintenance (e.g., O&M, Wind, and Solar Technicians). Additional areas of focus include Energy Trading, Operations Engineering, Asset Management, support functions, AI and Data Analytics, and IT. The Corporate Recruiter partners closely with hiring leaders to build recruiting strategies, attract and assess talent, and deliver an excellent candidate experience. Responsibilities Partner with business leaders and hiring managers to define role requirements, hiring priorities, and interview plans; provide consultative guidance and timely communication. Develop and execute sourcing and recruiting strategies; share market insights and recommend adjustments to improve candidate flow and quality. Provide a superior candidate experience throughout the end-to-end recruiting process including candidate review, phone screens, interview scheduling/coordination and offer Screen and assess candidates, coordinate interviews, and manage the offer process while ensuring a consistent, high-quality candidate experience.
