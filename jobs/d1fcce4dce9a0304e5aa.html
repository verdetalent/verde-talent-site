Are you a highly motivated sales professional interested in renewable energy? Join us to accelerate the green energy transition and deliver best-in-class wind power solutions. Region NCE > Sales Central > Sales Germany Sales, Germany is part of Vestas Northern and Central Europe, a Sales Business Unit of Vestas Wind Systems A/S, the world's largest manufacturer of wind turbines. Vestas core business involves developing, producing, selling, and servicing wind turbines. With more than 24,500 employees worldwide, through our high-quality, innovative data capabilities and innovative 100 GW of wind turbines under service, we use data to interpret, forecast, and exploit wind resources and deliver best-in-class wind power solutions. A diverse and inclusive workforce at Vestas is vital for accelerating the green energy transition. We know our differences make us stronger, more innovative, and better equipped to address the issues of the future. We are looking for a Lead Sales Manager (f/m/d) based in Berlin or Hamburg to join the Sales Central Region. The Lead Sales Manager is responsible for promoting sales and business development as well as driving strategic initiatives, especially in Germany, together with a team of highly motivated people.
