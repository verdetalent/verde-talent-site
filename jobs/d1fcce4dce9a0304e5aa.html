Full description

Are you a highly motivated sales professional interested in renewable energy? Vestas, the global leader in wind turbine manufacturing, is looking for a Lead Sales Manager (f/m/d) to oversee the entire sales cycle, from prospect identification and tendering to negotiating and closing wind farm supply contracts.

Join us to accelerate the green energy transition and deliver best-in-class wind power solutions.

Region NCE > Sales Central > Sales Germany

Sales, Germany is part of Vestas Northern and Central Europe, a Sales Business Unit of Vestas Wind Systems A/S, the world's largest manufacturer of wind turbines. Vestas core business involves developing, producing, selling, and servicing wind turbines. With more than 24,500 employees worldwide, through our high-quality, innovative data capabilities and innovative 100 GW of wind turbines under service, we use data to interpret, forecast, and exploit wind resources and deliver best-in-class wind power solutions.

A diverse and inclusive workforce at Vestas is vital for accelerating the green energy transition. We know our differences make us stronger, more innovative, and better equipped to address the issues of the future. We are looking for a Lead Sales Manager (f/m/d) based in Berlin or Hamburg to join the Sales Central Region. The Lead Sales Manager is responsible for promoting sales and business development as well as driving strategic initiatives, especially in Germany, together with a team of highly motivated people.

Responsibilities

  • Leadership throughout the sales cycle: From prospect identification, tendering, and deal optimization to negotiation and closure of wind farm supply contracts
  • Establish solid customer relationships, build trust, and understand the customers´ business case to develop ideal value propositions
  • Ensure effective cross-functional collaboration within a matrix organization and across group functions
  • Monitor and assess market trends and customer/competitor activities
  • Handle internal and external stakeholders
  • Contribute to the development of Vestas commercial strategy
  • Lead strategic initiatives across Sales Central
  • Act as stand-in for Sales Director on an as-needed basis

Qualifications

  • Master's in Business or Engineering degree or equivalent
  • 5+ years of experience as Sales manager or Commercial Project Manager in complex environments
  • Contract negotiation skills and experience with complex technical contracts
  • Role model for team, in skills, attitude and problem-solving proficiency
  • Ability to identify and involve business stakeholders
  • Fluent in German and English

Competencies

  • Effectively deliver results under agile conditions
  • Solid communication skills and natural challenger
  • Influence internal stakeholders and work effectively in a matrix organisation
  • Financial understanding and investigative capabilities
  • Strong business acumen and proven leadership skills
  • Advanced strategic thinking and strong execution track record
  • Ability to work under time-sensitive environments and willingness to go the "extra mile"

What we offer

We offer an exciting job with great opportunities for development in an inspiring environment at an established wind turbine producer. We value initiative, responsibility, and accuracy. You will become part of an international environment with an exemplary commitment to development and colleagues who support each other. Additionally, we offer:

  • Permanent position
  • Onboarding program
  • Flexible working hours
  • 30 vacation days
  • Home Office

Additional information

Location: Berlin or Hamburg, Germany

Application Deadline: August 6th, 2026. We will review applications on an ongoing basis. We can withdraw from the job and reserve the right to do so at any time, including before the advertised closing date.

Start date: September or October 1st, 2026. Depending on our agreement, we are flexible.

About Vestas

Vestas is the energy industry’s global partner on sustainable energy solutions. We are specialised in designing, manufacturing, installing, and servicing wind turbines, both onshore and offshore.

Across the globe, we have installed more wind power than anyone else. We consider ourselves pioneers within the industry, as we continuously aim to design new solutions and technologies to create a more sustainable future for all of us. With more than 200 GW of wind power installed worldwide and 40+ years of experience in wind energy, we have an unmatched track record demonstrating our expertise within the field.

With 39,000 employees globally, we are a diverse team united by a common goal: to power the solution – today, tomorrow, and far into the future.

Vestas promotes a diverse workforce which embraces all social identities and is free of any discrimination. We commit to create and sustain an environment that acknowledges and harvests different experiences, skills, and perspectives. We also aim to give everyone equal access to opportunity.
