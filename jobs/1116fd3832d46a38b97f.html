Eversource is looking for a Lead Project Manager, Transmissionto support Major Projects - TransmissioninWestwood, MA. In this role, you will lead some of the most innovative and consequential Transmission Line and Substation projects in the region. In this role you act as the primary advisor to a group of Transmission project managers to ensure the successful execution of a state-wide project portfolio. Independently manages and advises other project managers on high-risk, high-profile transmission projects in Massachusetts to ensure all phases of approved projects are completed efficiently and within scope, on schedule, and within budget.

Essential Functions:

  • Develop and mentor associate project managers and project managers to be capable of resolving project challenges to achieve high value results that support the successful execution of the project.
  • Participate in the development and execution of regional annual budgets, initiatives, and goals.
  • Keep management informed and provides feedback of portfolio status, project management development.
  • Provides feedback to project managers and assists management in performance evaluation process.
  • Acts on behalf of the manager in their absence as assigned.
  • Individual is assigned traditional projects requiring close stakeholder interaction such as large substation construction projects, new transmission line projects, major transmission facility expansion projects, or projects of high complexity.
  • Identify emerging issues and recommend appropriate actions/projects that produce shareholder value and meet internal and external customer needs.
  • High level of independent management of projects, capable of successfully managing projects from initiation to close out on time and within budget.
  • Assigned projects are multi-discipline in nature, multi-stakeholder involvement, and high financial risk exposure profile requiring management reporting communications.
  • Responsible to take the necessary action to see that the project portfolio is completed efficiently, on time and within the established budget.
  • Works closely with Eversource Energy Delivery personnel, Legal, Regulatory Relations, Purchasing, Communications and all Transmission Business functions to ensure project success.
  • Integrates and manages a cross functional project team to develop appropriate project roles and responsibilities.
  • In general, manages the planning, engineering, siting/permitting, procurement, construction, commissioning and close out project phases.
  • Manages the team’s development of a comprehensive project plan, schedule, communications plan and establishes the change control methodology.
  • Manages execution of the project plan and project change control management process.
  • Coordinates the development of a detailed project budget and cash flow.
  • Manages the monthly maintenance and reporting of project scope, budgets and schedules.
  • Manages the development of a comprehensive siting and permitting needs assessment.
  • Manages the development of and participates in the required regulatory siting, permit filings and other regulatory filings.
  • Coordinates environmental assessment and the necessary regulatory approvals (federal, state and local) needed to begin construction.
  • Ensures the timely preparation ISO-NE project submittals.
  • Manages the development and evaluation of bid documents for engineering, design, construction and test procurement of material and support systems.
  • Manages the development of required outage and energization plans.
  • Manages the timely preparation of the long-term outage plan project submittals.
  • Manages project close out in a timely manner to support corporate business goals.
  • Provides clear concise and comprehensive reports for management.
  • Provides expert witness testimony for assigned projects at ISO-NE, CSC and other regulatory preceding.

Technical Knowledge/Skill/Education/Licenses/Certifications:

Technical Knowledge/Skill:

  • Demonstrated ability to investigate and solve problems, analyze data, write reports and make presentations.
  • Possesses the ability to cope with deadlines, manage time, set priorities, define roles and responsibilities, and make decisions.
  • Ability to run meetings, motivate others, and interact with others at all levels of the organization.
  • The ability to schedule, plan and manage numerous complex transmission projects concurrently.
  • Self- starter with strong communication skills, including listening, speaking and writing skills.
  • Individual leadership abilities, planning skills, decisiveness and customer focus.
  • Knowledge of Project Management principles and practices.
  • Excellent leadership, teambuilding, coaching, problem solving, interpersonal and motivational skills required.
  • Ability to develop skills to prepare presentations and expert witness testimony for local and state regulatory presentations.

Education:

  • Bachelor’s Degree in Engineering (preferred) or bachelor’s degree in Finance, Business or the equivalent degree will be considered or equivalent experience.
  • All of the above plus the following: Master’s Degree is preferred; Project Management class work is desirable.

Experience:

  • Ten (10) years of Project Management experience.

Licenses & Certifications:

  • Valid Motor Vehicle Operator’s License is required.
  • Project Management Professional (PMP) through the Project Management Institute (PMI) is preferred.
