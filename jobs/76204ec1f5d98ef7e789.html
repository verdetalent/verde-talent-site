Equipment Technician

Windsor, CO Blades Factory

The Equipment Technician supports production by helping maintain, repair and fill automated resin dispensing machines and production moulds.

Responsibilities:

  • Support Production self-managed teams.
  • Assisting in repairs or maintenance of dispensing machines.
  • Fill machines with components and complete all production paperwork.
  • Perform mould repairs/support refurbishment
  • Cleaning of machines as part of first line maintenance.
  • Maintain mould, mouldette's, root plates, mould services.
  • Work to detailed planned maintenance using electronic asset management.
  • Using electronic asset management record all work against each machine.
  • Keep records of parts used and up to date Kanban system.
  • Understand mechanical drawings.
  • Understanding of basic electricity, hydraulics and pneumatics.
  • Maintain a clean and orderly work environment.
  • Ability to receive and maintain forklift certification.
  • Lift, position, and/or remove material from work areas.
  • Use and handle hazardous materials and understand their hazards at various locations using machines and equipment, properly segregate hazardous materials and waste.
  • Communicate to warehouse and/or shipping the transport of hazardous materials and waste according to strict regulatory requirements.
  • Comply with state and federal regulations for handling, storage, disposal, transport etc. of hazardous materials and waste.
  • Other duties as assigned.

Qualifications

  • High school diploma or general education degree (GED)
  • 1–2 years of relevant experience in mechanical systems, electrical work, or equipment maintenance and repair.
  • Ability to read and comprehend simple instructions, short correspondence, and memos. Ability to write simple correspondence. Ability to effectively present information in one-on-one and small group situations to other employees of the organization
  • Ability to add, subtract, multiply, and divides in all units of measure, using whole numbers, common fractions, and decimals
  • The ideal candidate will have excellent written and communication skills and a high degree of mechanical aptitude and experience
  • Able to multi-task in a fast-paced, labor-intensive production environment
