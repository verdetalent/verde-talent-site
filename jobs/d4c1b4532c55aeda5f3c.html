Job Description:

Role Overview

Fluence offers a sign-on bonus for this position.

Sales Engineering is a key role at Fluence, critical to our continued growth and success. As a Sales Engineer, you will be the technical lead throughout the sales process for some of the largest and most challenging projects in the energy storage industry. This is a customer-facing role, and also requires interaction with many different groups internally including Product, Supply Chain, Logistics, and Project Execution.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop a deep understanding for the Fluence energy storage technology platforms and provide sales teams with technical support as well as product configuration guidance to drive successful equipment-only and turn-key sales.
  • Review, understand and negotiate detailed technical specifications for energy storage core and BOP scope (MV, HV, and civil)
  • Develop detailed and accurate cost estimates for new sales.
  • Lead technical aspects of contract negotiations – scope, schedule, and performance guarantees.
  • Assist in the development of sales tools and support materials to streamline and grow sales.
  • Capture customer technical and application requirements to help shape product roadmaps.

Required Qualifications & Experience

  • A Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Engineering (Electrical Preferred)
  • 3-10 years’ work experience in energy storage, solar, or other power generation
  • Ability to solve complex technical problems and communicate results.
  • Strong verbal and written communication skills in English.
  • Experience with contract negotiations and familiarity with key contract areas.
  • Knowledge of applicable standards, codes and regulations.
  • Entrepreneurial drive and ability to prioritize and execute on many tasks in parallel.
  • Willing and able to travel, domestically and internationally, up to 20% of the time.
