Full description
Vestas has a prominent position in wind technology and a Defining-force in the development of the wind power industry. Vestas' core business comprises of Development, Manufacturing, Sales, Marketing and Service of Wind Turbines. Come and Join us at Vestas! Vestas Technology & Operations > Manufacturing > Blades PE Finish & Paint Come join the new Vestas Blades Process Engineering organization. The Finish & Paint Blades training department within the Blades Finish & paint Process Engineering: Will create and own the Process Specific Training Material, including video and other documentation. Will also own the Training roadmap for deployment of process specific training, including key PFMEA elements. Support to specific sites for implementation of training can occur. There will be between 1-2 Professional engineers working with Finish & Paint Training. The whole Finish & Paint department will consist of 9-12 high educated engineers or equal education, and they will be divided into 5 teams: Process, Tooling, Documentation, Operational Support and Training. Responsibilities As a Training Engineer at Vestas, you will play a key role in develop and improving the Training material for wind turbine blades. If you do not have a deep knowledge within composite materials and blades manufacturing methods, you will have a high motivation to get it. You will have a strong focus on modern training systems and material including process vision, laser systems and other digital enabling systems. The training methods are focused on the receivers, have the factory employees in focus.
