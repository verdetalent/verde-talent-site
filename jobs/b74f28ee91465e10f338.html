Distribution Design Engineer

Identifies improvements and major repair needs on the distribution system.  Assess the effect of alternative performance improvements on a cost/benefit basis and determines priorities.  Responsible for engineering support to Field Engineering Design for any customer additions that require a substantial change to the distribution system.  Plans system improvements in-line with customer needs and engineer circuit changes to improve performance for assigned circuits.  Reviews daily interruption reports.  Analyzes projected circuit deficiencies (e.g. voltage, overloads, etc.) for assigned circuits.  Develops alternatives to improve circuit reliability.  Recommends equipment replacement vs repair to maximize Return on Asset (ROA). Participates during major restoration efforts including Emergency Preparedness.

  • Education: A Bachelor’s Degree in Engineering is required.
  • Experience: Two (2) to Five (5) years of equivalent experience in Energy Delivery is preferred.

SENIOR DISTIBUTION DESIGN ENGINEER

In addition to performing the duties of a Distribution Design Engineer, responsible for the performance and design of the electrical distribution system. Responsible for the development of projects to meet customer needs, capacity needs and address performance concerns. Position works collaboratively with all other departments to address system performance including but not limited to switching support, contingency planning, customer concerns, power quality issues and any other distribution needs. This position will be responsible for the training and mentoring of other engineers within the group, including oversight of project creation, presentation and documentation. This position will have leadership opportunities to lead 3-state engineering initiatives and will act as SME for distribution tools and engineering. Note: This is not a step progression and will be based on behavioral based interview and work experience.

  • Education: A Bachelor’s Degree in Engineering; Electrical preferred; M.S. preferred.
  • Experience: Minimum 5 years of related experience in electric utility engineering required. Three (3) years of experience in distribution engineering preferred.

Accountabilities – Primary Duties:

• Mentors and trains other Engineers.
