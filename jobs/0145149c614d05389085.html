Professional | Full-Time | Quality, Health, Safety and Environment This position is part of the Supplier Quality NCE region located at Taranto, Italy Production Site to ensures the highest focus on quality, safety and environment, take an actions in case of any quality problem at production line related to purchase components by contacting suppliers and all Vestas stake holders to ensure that problem is solved and will not be repeated. Responsibilities You will ensure quality from suppliers for all production material groups used in Vestas Taranto site. The responsibilities are for NCR handling, analyzing and approve RCA provided by suppliers and Corrective Action implementation at supplier location. Driver down Cost of Poor Quality is important part of this position. Interface internally to the Regional and Global SQ&D, sourcing and logistics as well as external customers. Qualifications You have an engineering or material science background as well as proficiency in applying advanced quality methodology. Solid working experience with demonstrated quality management and problem-solving skills can replace the need for a formal education. Experience in Wind business will be big advantage. Competencies To perform well in this position, it is important that you are results-oriented and have good communication and collaboration skills enabling you to collaborate internally and work with suppliers.
