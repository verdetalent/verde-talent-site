Quality Manager – Lead Quality Excellence in Renewable Energy Manufacturing

Are you a Quality leader who is committed to developing people, supporting continuous improvement and making a meaningful contribution to a more sustainable future?

At Vestas' Isle of Wight manufacturing facility, you'll lead a skilled team of quality professionals and play a critical role in ensuring the quality and reliability of products that support the global energy transition.

This is an opportunity to combine technical skills with people leadership, working in a collaborative environment where your knowledge, ideas and contributions will impact safety, quality, customer satisfaction and business performance.

Whether you are an established Quality Manager or an experienced quality professional ready to advance your career, we would be pleased to hear from you.

Technology & Operations > Manufacturing > Isle of Wight

We are looking for an experienced and motivated Quality Manager to manage our Production Quality organization of engineers and technicians. In this role, you will coach and develop a motivated team, building capability, encouraging innovation, and creating an environment where people can perform at their best within a busy manufacturing environment which operates 24/7.

You will be responsible for maintaining and continuously improving quality standards across the manufacturing site, ensuring full alignment with Vestas requirements and industry standards. By placing safety and quality at the center of all decisions, you will help enhance productivity, improve customer satisfaction, and support the delivery of high-quality products.

Responsibilities

Every improvement you make will directly contribute to the production of renewable energy solutions used around the world, helping accelerate the global transition to sustainable energy.

  • Lead and develop the Production Quality team.
  • Oversee product and process quality across the factory to ensure consistent delivery of high standards.
  • Manage preventive quality engineering and continuous improvement.
  • Conduct root cause analysis and corrective action activities.
  • Maintain the local Quality Management System and ISO 9001 readiness.
  • Manage internal, external, and customer quality audits.
  • Use quality performance data and insights to support continuous improvement and strategic decision-making.
  • Represent Quality in strategic projects and stakeholder forums.

Qualifications

Essential qualifications:

  • Bachelor's degree in Engineering or an equivalent technical qualification.
  • Minimum 5 years of experience within a manufacturing environment.
  • ISO 9001 Internal or Lead Auditor certification.
  • Six Sigma Green Belt certification as a minimum.

Desirable additional qualifications:

  • Experience with Quality Core Tools, including APQP, PFMEA, Control Plans, and SPC.
  • Black Belt certification.
  • Quality certifications such as ASQ Certified Quality Engineer (CQE).
  • Experience as a Quality Specialist, Non-conformity Case Handler, or Quality Approver.
  • Experience in managing quality compliance, audits, and customer requirements.

Competencies

  • Demonstrated quality ownership and governance mindset.
  • Experience applying preventive quality engineering methodologies.
  • Solid understanding of Quality Management Systems and audit processes.
  • Demonstrated structured problem-solving skills using methodologies such as A3, 8D and 5 Whys.
  • Ability to build quality capabilities and support a culture of continuous improvement.
  • Demonstrated leadership skills with the ability to engage, develop, and motivate teams.
  • High level of technical knowledge within manufacturing and quality management.
  • Demonstrates decision quality, delivers results, builds effective teams, and instills trust.

About Vestas

Vestas is the energy industry’s global partner on sustainable energy solutions. We are specialised in designing, manufacturing, installing, and servicing wind turbines, both onshore and offshore.

Across the globe, we have installed more wind power than anyone else. We consider ourselves pioneers within the industry, as we continuously aim to design new solutions and technologies to create a more sustainable future for all of us. With more than 200 GW of wind power installed worldwide and 40+ years of experience in wind energy, we have an unmatched track record demonstrating our expertise within the field.

With 39,000 employees globally, we are a diverse team united by a common goal: to power the solution – today, tomorrow, and far into the future.
