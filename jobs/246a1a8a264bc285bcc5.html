Job Summary

The Sr. Manager, Stakeholder Relations - Central Region leads locally tailored engagement for energy infrastructure and multi-technology campus development across the Central Region, with a primary focus on Texas. The role supports projects from prospecting through development, permitting, construction and execution, with near-term emphasis on projects in active execution and continuity through transition to Operations. Depending on project scope, campuses may integrate generation, storage, data centers or other large-load infrastructure; configurations are project-specific.

As a key representative of AES, this position serves as a bridge with local communities, fostering positive interactions between the company and the community. The Sr. Manager functions as a local spokesperson for the organization, taking on external responsibilities to elevate AES' presence and reputation within the region.

This role is a people leader responsible for empowering, inspiring and developing a talented regional team of industry-leading engagement professionals. The successful candidate will be a transformational leader who builds team capability, creates an inclusive and high-performing environment, and helps team members grow while delivering strong results.

The role also provides expert guidance and support to the team, leveraging deep knowledge in community relations to drive performance toward strategic objectives. The breadth of engagement extends beyond local communities to include customer counterparts, unions, nonprofits, local governments, EPC partners, industry associations, economic development organizations and local news organizations, among others.

The successful candidate has demonstrated experience designing and executing community benefit agreements and other benefit structures, and cultivating innovative, responsive and meaningful partnerships in service of local communities and responsible infrastructure development.

Job Functions

People Leadership and Team Development

  • Lead, coach and develop a talented team of stakeholder engagement professionals, setting clear priorities and expectations while empowering team members to make decisions, grow their capabilities and deliver industry-leading engagement.
  • Foster an inspiring, inclusive and high-performing culture; provide timely feedback and development opportunities; and apply a transformational leadership approach to strengthen the team for current and future business needs.

Strategic Community Engagement

  • Develop and execute local engagement plans for Central Region energy projects and campuses across prospecting, site and acquisition diligence, early planning, development, permitting, construction and execution, with a particular emphasis on Texas.
  • Support stakeholder and community diligence for potential new projects AES may seek to acquire.
  • Advise project teams on community needs, stakeholder relationships and near- and long-term risks, aligning engagement with project milestones and responsible development.
  • Build cross-functional alignment with Development, Permitting, Execution, Finance, Legal, Government Relations, Operations, Communications and other relevant internal teams.
  • Coordinate with customers and development partners on engagement strategies, siting considerations and stakeholder expectations.
  • Translate community insight into feasible responses and clear engagement responsibilities across interconnected projects and technologies.
  • Manage engagement spending and resources within approved processes and oversee relevant consultants or agencies when requested.
  • Mobilize coalitions of individuals and organizations to demonstrate support for AES' energy projects.

Local Policy and Inclusive Participation

  • Maintain informed understanding of Texas and relevant state, municipal and local-level political landscapes, in coordination with AES' Government Relations team. Build professional, nonpartisan relationships with elected officials and staff, regulators, trade and business groups, community and educational organizations, and public and private institutions. Monitor local proceedings and support transparent public participation and appropriate project advocacy.
  • Engage a broad and diverse local audience through respectful, culturally competent consultation, including underrepresented communities. Conduct Indigenous engagement where applicable, consistent with the AES Voluntary Tribal Engagement Policy. Create accessible opportunities to understand concerns and influence engagement planning.
  • Maintain accessible, responsive inquiry and notification channels. Listen, document feedback, communicate timely responses, and work with project teams and partners to resolve concerns. Escalate unresolved issues appropriately and communicate outcomes to stakeholders, maintaining continuity as projects move into execution.

Community Benefit Agreements and Social Impact Partnerships

  • Cultivate innovative, responsive and meaningful local partnerships through the AES Social Impact Program.
  • Lead the design and implementation of community benefit agreements and other community benefit structures. Listen and co-design with local stakeholders and relevant partners, assess feasible benefits with project teams, and negotiate and structure agreements in alignment with AES' Social Impact Program and compliance requirements. Ensure partnerships are ethical, and the long-term benefits serve community priorities and responsible development and are not an exchange for public support or approvals.
  • Implement agreed commitments, track accountability and outcomes, and report progress in adherence to internal and external reporting requirements.
  • Coordinate outreach and volunteer activities where relevant and aligned with company and community priorities.

Stakeholder Intelligence and Accountability

  • Maintain accurate stakeholder lists, interaction records, feedback, commitments and engagement reporting using designated CRM tools.
  • Analyze local sentiment, social and traditional media, and emerging political or community issues to identify near- and long-term risks and opportunities.
  • Lead stakeholder risk management and communications across the project lifecycle, including issue escalation and executive-facing risk reporting.
  • Provide practical guidance to project teams and maintain transparent, accountable engagement across assigned projects.

Economic Development, Communications and Events

  • Apply understanding of economic development and tax incentive structures relevant to Texas and assigned state energy and large infrastructure development.
  • Support the negotiation and approval of relevant economic and tax incentives, alongside relevant internal team members and external consultants and partners.
  • Partner with Communications to develop clear, locally tailored, multichannel project information, advocacy materials, talking points, presentations and public displays.
  • Coordinate construction messaging and local media opportunities, explain relevant issues and support issue and crisis communications with the responsible teams.
  • Plan and deliver public meetings, community events, virtual engagement and site visits, including preparation, presentations and follow-up.
  • Represent AES professionally in business, government and community settings, advancing informed dialogue and practical collaboration.
