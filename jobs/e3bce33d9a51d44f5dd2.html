Full description

At Bloom Energy, our vision for a world powered by clean, reliable, and affordable energy is more than just a dream—we’re making it reality.

For over two decades, we’ve been at the forefront of the global energy transition, pioneering solutions that empower critical industries to thrive in a rapidly digitizing, energy-intensive world. From revolutionizing power for AI-driven data centers to ensuring resilience for hospitals, electric grids, manufacturing facilities, and utilities, our solid oxide fuel cell (SOFC) and solid oxide electrolyzer (SOEC) technologies are redefining what’s possible by delivering energy abundance for all. With more than 30,000 fuel cell modules deployed worldwide, we are the trusted partner for Fortune 100 companies and innovators alike. Our cutting-edge solutions enable unparalleled “time-to-power” capabilities, reliability, and sustainability, ensuring our customers remain ahead in a world where soaring energy demand and intensifying energy scarcity are rapidly becoming the new norm.

At Bloom, we thrive on collaboration, bold thinking, and relentless innovation. We believe that, together, we can create a brighter, more sustainable future while tackling the most pressing challenges of the 21st century.

We are looking for a  Director Installations Innovationsto join our team in one of today’s most exciting technologies.  This role will report to the VP, Customer Installations Groupand will based in Arkansas.  This is a remote position.

Role and Responsibilities:

  • Scope, plan, and execute a wide array of special engineering and construction projects to improve installation safety, efficiency, and quality through construction process innovation, product evolutions, and vertical integration strategies
  • Work with external construction and installation partners to improve Installation Quality, Field hour reductions, and on-site project efficiency
  • Co-Develop and lead Bloom’s Installer model needs for USC&I installations
  • Evaluate and identify requirements and risks of new products to installations, working cross-functionally to ensure requirements are met, install guidelines are updated, and mitigate risks through the course of development
  • Implement key strategic business initiatives with partners, monitoring initial progress and then creating a path for self-sustainability
  • Identify areas of improvement and vulnerability across the organization, and make actionable recommendations
  • Drive closure on issues preventing Installations success, particularly on items that have no natural organizational home
  • Mentor Installations employees to develop strategic problem solving, sustainable process development, and executive presentation skills

Skills and Experience:

  • 15+ years of experience in a cross-functional, fast-paced environment with rapidly shifting priorities, including at least 10+ years of technical or construction project management experience
  • Demonstrated success managing cross-functional teams through influence rather than authority
  • Excellent executive communicator and presenter, able to distill multi-faceted issues into concise key messages
  • Proven track record of motivating and inspiring cross-functional teams to come together and develop creative solutions to challenging issues
  • Ability to break down complex problems into root causes and provide meaningful, actionable solutions
  • Command of Microsoft Powerpoint and Excel; advanced experience is strongly preferred
