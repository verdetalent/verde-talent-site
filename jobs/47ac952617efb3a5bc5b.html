RTR Senior GL Accounting Analyst

Vestas
Pasay, 00, PH, 1300 Full-time Individual Contributor
WindExcel
Applying takes you to Vestas's own careers site to complete your application.
Full description

The team is under Global Finance of Vestas Shared Services Centre. General Ledger is mainly responsible for periodic closing of the company's financial records, Statutory reporting and SOX Compliance documentation. The group is expected to have close collaboration with regional finance, payroll & treasury department as well as with the other functions in SSC such as AP/AR/TE & Controlling and Reporting.

Finance SSC - Record to Report > SSC Finance Record to Report - General Ledger & Cash Accounting

Responsibilities:

  • Manage complex month-end, quarter-end, and year-end finance and accounting closing activities in full compliance with group accounting policies/controls and performance targets (SLAs. KPIs, OPIs)
  • Deliver timely, accurate, and insightful financial reporting and analysis to support strategic decision-making and meet the needs of key stakeholders
  • Promptly communicate potential and identified issues and its updates or help needed to the relevant stakeholders as an input for pre-close meetings and ensure follow-through until resolution with speed and quality
  • Resolve accounting issues promptly and escalate complex matters in advance of the month-end close to ensure timely resolution
  • Identify and implement process improvements within assigned processes and ensure timely, high-quality updates of standard operating procedures (SOPs)

Qualifications:

  • Bachelor's Degree in any Finance, Accounting, Business Management or related area
  • At least 3-5 years of relevant working experience in finance particularly Business Controlling, Financial Analysis, or any equivalent finance functions
  • Good communication skills particularly in English language - both oral and written and able to communicate insights to stakeholders and team members
  • Proficient in Microsft Office applications i.e. Excel, Powerpoint, Word. Knowledge in using ERP system. Experience in SAP
  • Possess solid analytical skills relevant for financial analysis i.e. variance analysis, formulating basic insights, and linking to finance and with curious growth and continuous improvement and learning mindset; With experience in managing stakeholders and reporting insights generated from the analysis
  • Flexible in work shift schedule depending on the region catered and willing to extend during month-end close, as needed. Experience in a shared service set-up is desired
  • Good at working with others and results-oriented with a self-motivated attitude; able to handle a complex and demanding environment

Competencies:

  • Advanced working knowledge of accounting principles and able to apply to day-to-day operations
  • Effective English communication skills, including speaking and writing, are essential. Ability to generate insights and analytics
  • Demonstrates solid problem-solving skills for the position and can identify concerns and work them through to completion
  • Exhibits interest, curiosity, growth mindset, simplicity, and motivation to learn
  • Thrives in collaborative settings and demonstrates initiative, a growth mindset, and the ability to absorb new information in complex and demanding environments
  • Good customer service mindset but able to challenge any deviations or non-compliant requests
  • Shows flexibility towards work shift schedule depending on the region catered and willing to extend during month-end close as needed
