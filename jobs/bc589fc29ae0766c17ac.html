Performs analysis on carrier spend and can speak to the data. Education/Experience: Bachelor’s degree in a business or supply chain discipline, or equivalent experience required. Experience in a manufacturing environment preferred. Good working knowledge with commonly used concepts, practices, and procedures within Supply Chain. Experience with MRP concepts and one or more ERP systems (Oracle or SAP preferred). Comfortable working with engineering databases and discussing specification changes with suppliers. Required Skills/Competencies: Technical: Proven organizational skills – ability to prioritize. Proven computer skills, including Microsoft Office or equivalent. In depth knowledge of TMS concepts. Demonstrated experience in managing suppliers and supplier relationships. Demonstrated experience in negotiating cost reductions and terms and conditions. Proven communications and interpersonal skills. Accountabilities: Some degree of creativity and latitude is expected. Carrier performance. Supplier relationships are maintained and improved. Essential Responsibilities: Responsible for managing the commercial relationship between First Solar and its carrier base. Utilizing OTM and other input from internal stakeholders to determine what items capacity is needed to maintain contractual delivery obligations.
