Full description
As a Cyber Services Product and Program Lead (m/f/d), you will be responsible for shaping Vestas Cyber Security Services. You will work in Vestas Service business area and manage the commercial portfolio of OT and Cyber Security Services, this is a dual role that combines product and program ownership. This position sits within Global Service Commercial, under Plant and Energy Solutions Product Development. The responsibility of the department is to define and mature-to-market plant, cyber and digital service offerings for selling long term service agreements and other contractual service offerings within the renewable energy sector. Cyber security is an important strategic initiative that supports Vestas' vision to be a leader in renewable energy solutions and shall ensure long-term profitability and competitiveness of Vestas Service business. Join us in ensuring resilient cybersecurity solutions in the global renewable energy projects. Ready to safeguard the energy revolution? Responsibilities: As Cyber Services Product and Program Lead you will work closely with global and regional cross-functional teams to lead the roadmap of cyber security services within Vestas' service business. The role will be the Global Service Commercial product lead for bringing ideas to market around Vestas' cyber security offerings. Program Management - you oversee the portfolio of our projects in the OT and Cyber Security services area. You are first point of contact on reporting around our initiatives, summarizing development budgets and defining governance in cooperation with other departments. You support strategic work and can manage projects, when needed. Service product roadmap - you will be responsible to build a long-term roadmap while ensuring the strategic direction is firm and accepted in close alignment with relevant parts of the business.
