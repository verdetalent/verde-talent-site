Basic Job Functions:

  • Design and execute tests and experiments to optimize laser processes – be extremely hands-on.

Plan, organize, and execute engineering experiment execution.

  • Attention to detail and summarize experimental data, monthly presentations with next steps/conclusions expected.
  • Be able to work in a fast-paced environment, within strict deadlines, while handling multiple tasks.
  • During equipment install, which lasts for a week or 2 (per install), flexibility in schedule may be needed.

Education/Experience:

  • High School Diploma or equivalent and 10 years of relevant technical experience or 2 years of experience as a Development Engineer Technician II at First Solar.
  • Associate’s Degree in an engineering discipline (Electrical, Instrumentation, or Mechanical preferred) or similar technical discipline (Physics preferred) and 5 years of relevant technical experience or 2 years of experience as a Development Engineer Technician II at First Solar.
  • Bachelor’s Degree in an engineering discipline (Electrical, Instrumentation, or Mechanical preferred) or similar technical discipline (Physics preferred) and 1 year of relevant technical experience or 1 year of experience as a Development Engineer Technician II at First Solar.
  • Master’s Degree in an engineering discipline (Electrical, Instrumentation, or Mechanical preferred) or similar technical discipline (Physics preferred) without prior technical work experience.
  • Experience with 2D and 3D modeling software's (AutoCAD, SolidWorks, or equivalent) required.
  • Experience with optical setups, solid-state lasers and familiarity with laser material interactions required.
  • Experience with Python or C++ or LabVIEW, preferred.
  • Experience with basic circuit design and components (resistors, inductors, diodes, capacitors Arduino, Raspberry Pi DYI project experience could be helpful in this role).

Required Skills/Competencies:

  • Ability to take ownership of a problem and own the resolution.
  • Strong analytical, communication, and organizational skills
  • Proficient use of all Microsoft Office suite programs.
  • Continuous improvement/learning.
  • Ability to make and execute detailed plans to achieve objectives with a strong sense of ownership.

Essential Responsibilities:

  • Tool set up, optical alignment, wiring, and process optimization/development.
  • Create and update equipment preventive maintenance work instructions, equipment specifications, and equipment troubleshooting.
  • Responsible for interfacing with other cross-functional groups to identify, brainstorm, create, and implement equipment improvement projects.
  • Will coordinate installation of new equipment into a development line.
  • Work with the facility team to prepare electrical, mechanical, and data drops for new equipment.
  • Effective team member that can support cross functional project and take ownership of sub-deliverables.
  • Works under minimal supervision.  May assist in orienting, mentoring, and training lower-level employees.
  • Complies with Company's Environmental, Quality, Safety or any other policies that have been enforced.
  • Other duties as assigned.
  • Job description subject to change at any time.

Reporting Relationships:

  • Reports to Development Engineer or Development Manager.
  • May have direct reports.

Travel:

  • Ability to travel to vendor site during equipment procurement (5 – 10% travel).
