We anticipate an opening for one or more Operator Repairman for the Riverside Generating Plant located in Minneapolis, MN. The base rate of pay for this classification will be $59.78 hourly (as listed in Exhibit "A" Wage Schedule). The position will be posted for 10 Working Days from July 16, 2026, to July 28, 2026. Position Summary Operator Repairman must have a general knowledge of the total plant equipment, procedures, and operations. The Operator Repairman must have specific expertise for the operation of plant systems. Essential Functions Performs all the functions trained and qualified for. As provided by the training and qualification program, must have full knowledge of operation of CT’s, HRSG, steam turbines, and auxiliary power plant equipment, and shall have the fundamental knowledge of electrical theory along with full knowledge of generator synchronization, excitation, voltage and load control, main and auxiliary transformer operation, and station and transmission switching. Operates, monitors, inspects, and maintains boilers, turbines, condensers, generators and plant auxiliary equipment and systems as assigned including water treatment systems and chemical additions as needed. Perform startups, shutdowns, equipment tests and operating adjustments. Monitors and records operating of equipment. Recognizes abnormal equipment performance and takes corrective action. Reports problems and initiates trouble reports. Responds to plant emergencies, unit upsets, and changing operating conditions. Must evaluate system condition and initiate corrective action.
