Full description

In Vestas, we #PowerTheSolution – Can you?

Region MED > SBU MED Service > Master Scheduling GR

Vestas Service Department is responsible for Sales, Service and Maintenance of the Wind Turbines. It is a busy area where a lot of exciting challenges happen. Inside the Operation Area, the Planning Team is responsible for setting and managing planning activities connected to Wind Turbine life cycles.

Responsibilities

  • Create a comprehensive weekly package of prepared work orders for each service team
  • Service schedule is aligned with the obligations in service contracts
  • Timely communication with crews and customers
  • Key performance index and process performance indicator related to planning & scheduling are balanced and under control

Qualifications

  • More than 2 years of experience in the wind industry experience
  • Professional experience in a technical service environment with a focus on planning & scheduling
  • Professional IT skills and experience with information processing in databases and planning tools
  • Experience in coordinating smaller development/improvement projects
  • Advanced English, both written and spoken

Competencies

  • Planning and organizational skills
  • A systematic and analytical approach with attention to detail
  • Able to handle unexpected changes with a focus on problem-solving
  • Capacity to respond effectively to shifting priorities and manage a range of work demands
  • Demonstrated skill in analyzing numerical data and recognizing trends
  • Clear and concise oral and written communication skills
  • Self-organization/good time management and the ability to handle work challenges professionally

What We Offer

You will be part of a regional team and be responsible for Service Dispatching in your own area. You will also play an important part in securing and delivering products and services to meet our customers' expectations. In order to be effective, we need skilled dispatchers who can handle changing situations with a methodical approach.

Additional Information

This is a hybrid position based in Greece and requires up to 20 days of travel per year.

We will be reviewing applications on a rolling basis, therefore, early applications are encouraged. To apply for this opportunity, please submit your CV in English.

About Vestas

Vestas is the energy industry’s global partner on sustainable energy solutions. We are specialised in designing, manufacturing, installing, and servicing wind turbines, both onshore and offshore.

Across the globe, we have installed more wind power than anyone else. We consider ourselves pioneers within the industry, as we continuously aim to design new solutions and technologies to create a more sustainable future for all of us.
