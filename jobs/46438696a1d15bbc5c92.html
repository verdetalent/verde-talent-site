Full description
The Tool Coordinator is a 100% travel role with 6 weeks on, one week off, and is a critical member of the on-site team supporting the construction phase of building and connecting the wind power plant to the grid. More specifically, they have responsibility for assisting with site setup, transport, installation, mechanical completion, commissioning, and site close-out of a project. The Tool Coordinator follows defined processes and procedures for safety, inventory management and documentation, with a clear focus on Construction Contractual tooling and additional tooling request Responsibilities: Provide business and administrative support to site leadership for daily operations, including creating and running regular reports (consumption, availability, production, safety, etc.) assist with compiling data for budgets, travel arrangements. Coordinate inventory and tools for site with monthly cycle counts, Monthly Reports-Consumptions, assist with budget, organize a clean and safe warehouse. Support all released planning processes and support improving planning performance. Willing to support and promote Vestas' commitment to diversity, equity, inclusion and belonging. Solid verbal and written communication skills; responsive and effective listener; ability to get others to follow standard processes and procedures. Ability to onboard and train new hires. Conduct site audits and close out audits. Corrective actions to meet KPI requirements on close outs. Ability to work in both a team-based environment and with limited supervision, capable of working remotely without need for supervision. Ability to identify problems and solve them through root cause analysis; demonstrate solid pragmatical skills and significant attention to detail. Solid time management and demonstrated organizational skills to ensure department goals are met. Ability and willingness to travel to multiple sites or locations on short notice.
