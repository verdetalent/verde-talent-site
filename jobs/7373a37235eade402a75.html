Are you ready to shape the future of renewable energy through cutting-edge SCADA solutions? Join Vestas as a key global interface between regional teams and central functions, where you will drive technical excellence, influence product development, and enable large-scale energy projects worldwide. In this role, you will collaborate with international stakeholders, lead cross-functional initiatives, and play a vital part in advancing the global energy transition—while working at the forefront of innovation in wind technology. Vestas Technology & Operations (VTO) > Product Line > Global Power Plant Solutions > Global SCADA Solutions Excellence Vestas Technology & Operations (VTO) serves as the integrated CTO and COO organization within Vestas, covering the entire industrial value chain—from Research & Development, Procurement, and Manufacturing to Supply Chain and Quality. To ensure customer centricity and successful delivery of customer projects, VTO operates through a Product Line organization composed of Regional Engineering & Technology teams supported by Global Functions. This setup manages regional technical front-end activities across the entire customer project lifecycle, while also serving as the ears to the market and the voice of the customer within the broader organization. Global Power Plant Solutions is one of the Global Functions responsible for enabling the regional technical front-end colleagues with Technical- and Functional Excellence within Cybersecurity, SCADA and Grid connection. Responsibilities In this global role you will bring a strong blend of advanced SCADA technical expertise and a product-oriented mindset, serving as the key link between regional, front-line SCADA teams and central functions such as R&D, Service, and Digital Solutions.
