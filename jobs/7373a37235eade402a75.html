Full description

Are you ready to shape the future of renewable energy through cutting-edge SCADA solutions?

Join Vestas as a key global interface between regional teams and central functions, where you will drive technical excellence, influence product development, and enable large-scale energy projects worldwide.

In this role, you will collaborate with international stakeholders, lead cross-functional initiatives, and play a vital part in advancing the global energy transition—while working at the forefront of innovation in wind technology.

Vestas Technology & Operations (VTO) > Product Line > Global Power Plant Solutions > Global SCADA Solutions Excellence

Vestas Technology & Operations (VTO) serves as the integrated CTO and COO organization within Vestas, covering the entire industrial value chain—from Research & Development, Procurement, and Manufacturing to Supply Chain and Quality.

To ensure customer centricity and successful delivery of customer projects, VTO operates through a Product Line organization composed of Regional Engineering & Technology teams supported by Global Functions. This setup manages regional technical front-end activities across the entire customer project lifecycle, while also serving as the ears to the market and the voice of the customer within the broader organization.

Global Power Plant Solutions is one of the Global Functions responsible for enabling the regional technical front-end colleagues with Technical- and Functional Excellence within Cybersecurity, SCADA and Grid connection.

Responsibilities

In this global role you will bring a strong blend of advanced SCADA technical expertise and a product-oriented mindset, serving as the key link between regional, front-line SCADA teams and central functions such as R&D, Service, and Digital Solutions.

  • Collect and analyze requirements from customers, regulatory instances and the Vestas value chain subsequently translating them into structured requirements to be prioritized and delivered through products, services and processes
  • Provide regions with product expertise, including both product capabilities, supporting processes and guidance regarding requests for non-standard products and features
  • Represent the Product Line organization in development projects
  • Enable regions to deploy SCADA solutions throughout the project lifecycle with global solutions and processes for example new product releases, automation tools and deployment process improvements
  • Lead global, cross-functional SCADA communities focused on continuously enhancing processes, methodologies, and knowledge through active engagement and training, including driving and overseeing related initiatives

Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Automation/Control Engineering, Electronics/Telecommunications Engineering, Computer Science, or a related discipline
  • Proven experience in SCADA systems, including development and/or deployment roles
  • Minimum of 5 years’ experience working with SCADA architecture and hardware platforms, preferably within the wind or broader renewable energy industry
  • Strong knowledge of industrial communication protocols such as OPC UA, Modbus, and similar
  • Proficiency in working with Linux, VMware, Windows, and SQL-based environments
  • Demonstrated ability to troubleshoot, maintain, and optimize SCADA solutions in operational environments
  • Experience working with Vestas SCADA products is considered an advantage

Competencies

  • Strong product and systems mindset, with the ability to translate complex inputs into clear problem statements and actionable outcomes
  • Proven capability to prioritize competing requirements and contribute effectively to product development and backlog management processes
  • Skilled in stakeholder management, with the ability to collaborate across cross-functional and matrix organizations
  • Demonstrated ability to influence decisions and build alignment without formal authority
  • Experience leading or contributing to communities, training sessions, or knowledge-sharing initiatives
  • Excellent communication skills in English, with confidence to engage stakeholders across all organizational levels
  • Proactive, analytical, and self-driven individual with a strong customer-centric mindset and a passion for continuous learning in fast-paced environments

What we offer

This position offers a unique chance to engage in an international environment where your strategic insights and involvement can drive substantial changes. You will collaborate with a diverse and inspiring group of individuals from across the globe, striving together for progress in the global energy transition. In this position, you will represent Vestas' product and technology solutions in close alignment with customers and key stakeholders across regions - playing a vital role in advancing sustainable energy on a global scale.

Additional information

This position can be based in Aarhus (Denmark) or Porto (Portugal)

About Vestas

Vestas is the energy industry’s global partner on sustainable energy solutions. We are specialised in designing, manufacturing, installing, and servicing wind turbines, both onshore and offshore.

Across the globe, we have installed more wind power than anyone else. We consider ourselves pioneers within the industry, as we continuously aim to design new solutions and technologies to create a more sustainable future for all of us. With more than 185 GW of wind power installed worldwide and 40+ years of experience in wind energy, we have an unmatched track record demonstrating our expertise within the field.

With 30,000 employees globally, we are a diverse team united by a common goal: to power the solution – today, tomorrow, and far into the future.

Vestas promotes a diverse workforce which embraces all social identities and is free of any discrimination. We commit to create and sustain an environment that acknowledges and harvests different experiences, skills, and perspectives. We also aim to give everyone equal access to opportunity.
