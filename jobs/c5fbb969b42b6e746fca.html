Working in pairs, you will report to the Area Supervisor and will be in charge of: Carry out the curative maintenance of the turbines, in order to optimize the performance of the turbines to the benefit of our clients, following the Quality, Safety and Environment standards. Ensure a smooth operation of our turbines and control the operation of the different elements of the wind turbine at all stages of intervention Carry out preventive maintenance of the turbines according to current rules or work instructions, (complete refurbishment of turbines). You will be reporting your interventions. Qualifications: BTEC Higher National Diploma, BTS/DUT industrial or Electrical / Mechanical Engineering certified by the BZEE certification if possible Ideally 2 to 5 years of experience in industrial maintenance or electricity, with knowledge of high voltage Knowledge of technical English (written/spoken) and familiar with the Office Pack. Competencies: Enthusiastic contact with customers regarding completed and upcoming service and repairs Motivated, autonomous, results-oriented, innovative, mobility required because trips are planned for the activity Good communication, team spirit Solid commitment to the concept of safety in general and safety in the work environment, ability to work at height.
