Job Summary The Product Analyst supports Duke Energy Florida’s Demand Response portfolio by helping convert known operational pain points, data gaps, and manual workarounds into organized trackers, clearer workflows, and measurable execution improvements. Reporting directly to the manager, this role supports issue resolution, process cleanup, reporting, action tracking, and follow-through on assigned priorities, with financial tracking as a supporting responsibility. The ideal candidate is organized, detail-oriented, comfortable working with data and business systems, and able to help improve existing workflows by reducing manual errors, rework, and follow-up gaps. Responsibilities Key Responsibilities: Organize already identified operational choke points into draft project plans, including proposed owners, deadlines, status updates, and follow-up actions, for review and collaborative refinement with the manager and appropriate team members. Help organize historical artifacts such as vendor invoices, regulatory data requests, contractor purchase orders, invoices, and related program documents into a shared repository with clear naming, ownership, and retrieval structure.
