Manufacturing Engineer, Plant Layout

Role Summary

The Manufacturing Engineer, Plant Layout is responsible for designing, optimizing, and maintaining factory layouts to support safe, efficient, and scalable manufacturing operations. This role focuses on equipment placement, material flow, and space utilization, using CAD tools to translate process requirements into practical, high-performing physical systems.

This role reports to the Senior Manager, Operational Excellence.

Key Responsibilities

Factory Layout & Design

  • Develop and maintain 2D factory layouts using AutoCAD
  • Design equipment placement, line configurations, and production areas
  • Ensure layouts optimize material flow, labor efficiency, and safety
  • Create clear, accurate drawings for cross-functional use

Installation

  • Serve as the owner of layout accuracy on the factory floor, ensuring equipment is installed per exact drawing dimensions
  • Translate CAD layouts into physical floor markings (taping, marking) to prepare for equipment installation and line changes
  • Partner with operations and vendors during installation to verify real-time alignment to layout drawings and post-installation sign-off

Space Utilization

  • Improve space utilization across production and warehouse interfaces
  • Eliminate unnecessary movement and handling through layout improvements

Capital Projects & Facility Changes

  • Support capital projects with layout development, scenario analysis, and documentation
  • Collaborate with engineering and project teams during planning and execution phases
  • Ensure alignment between design intent and on-site implementation

Standards & Documentation

  • Apply and uphold established facility layout standards (clearances, spacing, access, safety)
  • Ensure drawings reflect accurate as-built conditions
  • Maintain up-to-date facility documentation and layout files

Qualifications

Required

  • Bachelor's degree in Mechanical, Industrial, or related Engineering field
  • 1-3 years of experience in manufacturing, industrial engineering, or facility design
  • Strong proficiency in AutoCAD for factory layout design
  • Experience designing layouts for production lines, equipment, and material flow
  • Strong cross-functional collaboration and communication skills

Preferred / Nice to Have

  • Experience in high-throughput manufacturing environments (e.g., food, CPG, packaging)
  • Familiarity with facility expansions, line commissioning, or capital project execution
  • Experience collaborating with external vendors, integrators, or contractors

Relevant Software & Tools

  • AutoCAD (required)
