Are you looking for an exciting job where you can put your skills and talents to work at a company you can feel proud to be a part of? Do you want a workplace that will challenge you and offer you opportunities to learn and grow? A position at Xcel Energy could be just what you’re looking for. Position Summary The Senior Data Product Engineer delivers and curates certified data products within a defined business data domain, turning the Data Product Owner’s vision and backlog into trusted, reusable analytics assets on the enterprise data platform. This role blends hands-on analytics engineering with an enablement focus, building data products against the enterprise certification framework while coaching federated business teams to build to standards themselves. The role enables business success by delivering well-modeled, governed data products that scale across the domain, by transferring capability to business users and domain analysts so delivery is not bottlenecked centrally, and by ensuring every product aligns to enterprise data platforms, standards, and architecture. Essential Responsibilities Data Product Delivery & Curation (35%): Build, curate, and maintain certified data products within an assigned business domain. Translate backlog items, user stories, and acceptance criteria into well-modeled datasets, semantic layers, and analytics solutions. Develop and optimize queries, transformations, and data models on Databricks and enterprise BI platforms. Ensure products are documented, performant, and fit for reuse. Enablement & Capability Transfer (30%): Coach federated business teams and domain analysts to build and extend analytics to enterprise standards on Databricks (Xcel Data Fabric). Develop reusable templates, patterns, and self-service assets that reduce dependence on central delivery. Enable business users on Databricks, Power BI, SAP Analytics Cloud, and BusinessObjects.
