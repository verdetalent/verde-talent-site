About Redwood Materials Redwood is localizing a global battery supply chain that seamlessly integrates recovery, reuse, and recycling — keeping critical minerals in circulation and driving the energy transition. Founded in 2017, we’re delivering low-cost and large-scale energy storage and producing battery materials in the U.S. for the first time, all from batteries we already have. Position Overview We are looking for a self-motivated, proactive, and detail-oriented Analytical Laboratory Technician II to join our growing startup team. This role is split between executing daily routine analysis to support CAM1 Production and advancing CAM R&D initiatives. As a crucial bridge in our operations, you will coordinate directly with CAM1 operators and R&D scientists to resolve technical questions and ensure data accuracy. Beyond benchmark, you will take ownership of daily lab functionality, manage inventory, and troubleshoot instrumentation. Because we operate in a fast-paced startup environment, the ideal candidate is a natural multi-tasker who anticipates team needs and steps in to help without being asked. Responsibilities will include: Perform daily routine chemical and physical analysis to support CAM1 production quality control and CAM R&D projects. Strictly adhere to Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), safety regulations, and CAM lab compliance requirements. Efficiently multi-task between high-volume sample analysis, regular lab maintenance, and non-laboratory supporting tasks as needed. Act as a key point of contact, communicating and coordinating effectively with CAM1 Operators and CAM R&D Scientists regarding sample status, data discrepancies, or technical questions. Deliver clear, accurate, and timely data reports to cross-functional teams to ensure production and research timelines are met.
