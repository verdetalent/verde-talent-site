Job Description: Sr.Staff Engineer Mechanical Engineer (T10)

Role and Responsibilities:

  • Ensure safe and efficient operation of plant equipment and utilities through preventive, predictive, and breakdown maintenance.
  • Monitor and improve equipment reliability.
  • Develop and implement maintenance strategies, shutdown planning, and asset lifecycle management.
  • Drive root cause analysis (RCA) for recurring failures and ensure corrective & preventive actions.

Safety, Quality & Compliance

  • Demonstrate a strong commitment to safe work practices and promote a proactive safety culture across the plant.
  • Ensure compliance with OSHAS / HSSE standards, and statutory safety requirements.
  • Implement safety systems, tools, and audits to sustain best-in-class safety performance.
  • Apply Quality & Reliability tools such as 5S, FMEA, and RCA to optimize maintenance systems.

Projects & Engineering

  • Lead and execute greenfield and brownfield projects, including equipment erection, installation, and commissioning.
  • Experience in R&D lab setup, testing facilities, or specialized installations such as fuel cell systems is an added advantage.
  • Coordinate with contractors, vendors, consultants, and internal stakeholders to ensure on-time and cost-effective project delivery.
  • Managing gas infrastructure systems, including Gas Storage, Pressure Regulation, and Distribution Systems—required to support Fuel Cell testing operations
  • Prepare technical specifications, BOQs, and support procurement activities.
  • Create 2D and 3D part drawings using SolidWorks and AutoCAD
  • Develop detailed manufacturing drawings with dimensions, GD&T, and tolerances
  • Read, interpret, and analyze CAD drawings with tolerances
  • Modify and optimize existing designs based on engineering or manufacturing feedback
  • Prepare assembly drawings, BOMs, and related documentation
  • Coordinate with manufacturing, quality, and engineering teams to resolve design issues

Skills and Experience:

  • Expert-level proficiency in SolidWorks
  • Strong working knowledge of AutoCAD
  • Hands-on experience in part drawing and detailing
  • Strong understanding of tolerances, GD&T, and manufacturing processes
  • Ability to read and interpret CAD drawings accurately
  • Good attention to detail and drawing accuracy
  • Experience with sheet metal, machining, or fabrication drawings
  • Knowledge of ISO / ANSI drawing standards
  • Experience working in manufacturing or product development environments
  • CAPEX planning and execution
  • Maintenance and utility operations
  • Strong understanding of process equipment used in manufacturing environments.
  • Prepare, review, and update Process Flow Diagrams (PFDs) and Piping & Instrumentation Diagrams (P&IDs) for new and existing processes.
  • Coordinate with cross-functional teams to ensure accurate integration of process, mechanical, electrical, and instrumentation requirements.
  • Lead and support equipment erection, installation, and commissioning activities, ensuring adherence to design intent and safety standards.
  • Execute and document equipment and system commissioning, performance validation, and handover to operations.
  • Strong understanding of system‑level testing and process instrumentation - mechanical integration.

Must-Have:

  • B.E/B. Tech/M. E/M. Tech in Mechanical. 12–15 year
  • Mechanical.12–15 year of experience in Mechanical & Design of Mechanical.
  • Expert-level proficiency in SolidWorks & Auto CADD.
  • Strong understanding of process equipment.
