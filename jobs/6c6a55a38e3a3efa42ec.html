Responsibilities

  • Must be able to see and hear alarms, timers and follow verbal instructions
  • Must be able to wear the required Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) for the task
  • Must be able to tolerate an environment where chemicals and materials are used within OSHA guidelines
  • Operator is in the process of completing offered repair coursework and training
  • Operator can successfully complete basic repairs and in process repairs accurately to include all documentation under supervision
  • Travel is required

Qualifications

  • High school diploma or university diploma
  • Ability to read and comprehend instructions, short correspondence, and memos
  • Completion of on-the-job training may be substituted for experience requirements
  • Established work history of at least one year in preferably manufacturing
  • Experience working on teams
  • Ability to adapt to a changing environment
  • Set a good example of personal integrity
  • Ability to communicate effectively on the job
  • Use measuring tools and gauges

Competencies

  • 1 year minimum of complex composite repair experience
  • Understanding of Epoxy and Polyester resins, wet lay-up and vacuum bag techniques
  • Understanding how to handle balsa and foam core materials
  • Knowledge of fiberglass reinforcement and structural repair
  • Ability to use powered hand tools safely and correctly
  • Quality knowledge of specification (i.e. temperature and humidity restrictions)
  • Ability to read complex technical drawings and processes
  • IT literate (email, internet, laptop)
  • Ability to work well as a team and take guidance
  • Operate all manufacturing equipment efficiently and safely
  • Demonstrate ability to meet production standards on specific tasks within the required time
  • Lift, position, and/or remove material from work areas
  • Adhere to Production/Service Work Instructions (PWI/SWI)
  • Other duties as assigned
