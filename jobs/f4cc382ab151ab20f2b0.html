Project Administrative Assistant - BLUvera Manufacturing

Fort Worth, TX, United States | BLUvera

APPLY NOW

The Project Administrator serves as the administrative and operational hub of the facility, coordinating the people, processes, materials, records, and resources required to keep daily operations running smoothly. This role supports manufacturing and operations leadership by owning many of the administrative, logistical, and coordination responsibilities that enable teams to focus on safe, productive execution.

The ideal candidate is highly organized, proactive, detail-oriented, and comfortable managing a wide variety of tasks in a fast-paced manufacturing environment. Success in this role comes from identifying needs before they become problems and ensuring the facility, workforce, and operations teams have the resources and support required to execute effectively.

Key Responsibilities

Operations Coordination

  • Support day-to-day operational activities across the facility.
  • Coordinate visitors, contractors, vendors, customers, and other site guests.
  • Support hiring activities, interview scheduling, candidate visits, and onboarding logistics.
  • Coordinate training schedules, employee events, workforce activities, and facility communications.
  • Assist operations leadership with administrative support, communications, reporting, and special projects.
  • Help identify and address operational support needs to keep the facility running efficiently.

Purchasing, Receiving & Inventory Administration

  • Create and manage purchase requisitions and purchase orders.
  • Track orders, deliveries, and vendor communications.
  • Process receiving documentation and maintain accurate records.
  • Coordinate invoice matching and purchasing documentation.
  • Maintain inventories of PPE, office supplies, breakroom supplies, consumables, and common-use materials.
  • Coordinate replenishment of operational and administrative supplies.
  • Support inventory audits and material tracking activities.

Workforce & Training Administration

  • Process and review employee timekeeping records.
  • Support payroll-related administrative activities and resolve discrepancies.
  • Maintain employee records, onboarding documentation, and workforce files.
  • Support PTO administration, including tracking requests, coordinating approvals, and maintaining accurate time-off records.
  • Maintain training matrices, certifications, qualification records, and training documentation.
  • Coordinate required training, recertifications, and workforce compliance tracking.
  • Support onboarding activities for new employees and contractors.

Facility Coordination

  • Coordinate facility service requests and external service providers.
  • Assist with workspace setup, office equipment, furniture coordination, and employee resources.
  • Manage visitor processes, access requests, badges, keys, and facility support needs.
  • Coordinate common area needs and facility consumables.
  • Serve as a liaison between facility users and service providers to resolve site-related issues.

Documentation & Compliance Support

  • Maintain personnel, operational, training, safety, and administrative records.
  • Maintain document libraries, forms, templates, and controlled documents.
  • Assist with document control and revision management activities.
  • Support audits, inspections, and compliance recordkeeping.
  • Ensure current operational documents and standards are readily accessible.

Reporting & Administrative Support

  • Maintain labor, attendance, staffing, and training reports.
  • Compile operational data and recurring reports for leadership.
  • Assist with dashboards, presentations, and operational reporting.
  • Coordinate site communications, announcements, and information boards.
  • Maintain organized electronic and physical filing systems.

Required Qualifications

  • High school diploma or equivalent.
  • 3+ years of experience in administrative, operations coordination, office management, project support, or similar roles.
  • Strong proficiency in Microsoft Office, including Excel, Outlook, Word, and Teams.
  • Excellent organizational skills and attention to detail.
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills.
  • Ability to work independently while coordinating across multiple teams.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience supporting manufacturing, construction, logistics, distribution, or industrial operations.
  • Experience with ERP, purchasing, inventory, payroll, or workforce management systems.
  • Associate's or Bachelor's degree in Business, Operations, or a related field.
  • Experience supporting startup operations or project teams.

Success in This Role

A successful Project Administrator will:

  • Keep the facility organized, stocked, and operating efficiently.
  • Maintain accurate workforce, training, purchasing, and operational records.
  • Ensure employees have the resources, information, and support they need to be successful.
  • Coordinate the many administrative and logistical details that enable safe and productive operations.
  • Anticipate needs and resolve issues before they impact production or project execution.
  • Become a trusted resource for employees, supervisors, vendors, and operational leaders.
