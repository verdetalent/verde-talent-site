Full description
Eversource will not offer immigration-related sponsorship for this position (e.g., H-1B, O-1, J-1, TN, E-3, etc.). Applicants requiring visa sponsorship to start employment with Eversource will not be considered. Role and Scope of Position: This position provides small commercial and residential developments that are not strategic customers with a coordinated point of contact for all customer issues related to gas distribution services. This position also contributes to the Company by improving customer satisfaction, retaining and growing profitable distribution and value-added service revenue. This role assists with the contribution to the Gas Operations business objectives by increasing the number of new gas customers and providing a more seamless “end to end” sale and service experience for our gas customers. Essential Functions: Under the guidance of supervision team, the expectations are to manage customer relationships and develop customer relationships within the assigned customer segment (small commercial and residential developments ). New Customer Connections- responsible for ensuring timely installation of new or upgraded utility services and for accurately providing related load and revenue estimates for customers requesting loads of under 1000 MCF annually and under for defined customer segments. Corporate Positioning – Under the direction of the supervision team, assists with developing and implementing strategies for achieving corporate objectives with the small account customer base, such as customer education, identifying rate changes or promoting energy efficiency programs. Customer Service – Assist with the enforcement of customer service policies, coordination of installation of new or upgraded utility service, follow up on reliability or service quality problems, various administrative functions, billing related issues and general customer inquiries.
