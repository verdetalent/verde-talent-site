About Charm

Our mission is to return the atmosphere to 280 ppm CO₂. We convert excess inedible biomass into carbon-rich bio-oil and inject it into underground storage for permanent carbon removal. At scale, we can use bio-oil to make fossil-free iron.

Our carbon removal efforts began in 2020 with initial purchases from customers like Stripe, Shopify, and Microsoft. Since then, we’ve scaled to thousands of tons of carbon removal, grown to over a hundred employees, and established three locations in San Francisco, Colorado, and Louisiana.

We're expanding our team of ambitious, creative, and hands-on problem solvers. We value psychological safety, collaboration, and continuous learning. We take calculated risks and treat mistakes as opportunities to improve. If you're excited about tackling big challenges, we encourage you to explore our company values.

Come help us reverse climate change. Gigatons or bust!

About the Role

Charm Industrial is building toward ATFBR, and we need someone to own how Pyro Operations learns, qualifies, and improves. The Process Specialist is the person who keeps our processes documented, our operators trained, and our operational knowledge current - so that when engineering hands off a new process, operations can absorb it and run.

This is not a desk job. You will spend time on the floor with operators, writing and maintaining SOPs that reflect what actually happens, and closing the loop between how a process was designed and how it runs day-to-day.

This role reports to the Operations Manager

Your Responsibilities: • Own the operator training and qualification pathway for Pyro Operations - run the program, track progress, and maintain qualification records

  • Maintain SOPs and training materials; update documentation as processes evolve and before they fall out of sync with practice
  • Monitor process performance; identify deviations early and drive corrective action before they become incidents
  • Identify and implement continuous improvement opportunities on the pyrolysis platform
  • Participate in Train-the-Trainer with Manufacturing Engineering during new process introductions; formally accept process ownership at handoff
  • Serve as the first call for operators with process questions during production You Bring: • 3-5 years of hands-on experience in an industrial processing environment - oil & gas, heavy manufacturing, chemicals, or similar
  • Experience writing or maintaining SOPs
  • Comfortable working with data - you can read a performance trend, identify what's off, and explain it to an operator and an engineer in the same conversation
  • Strong written and verbal communication; you write clearly and you follow through
  • Prior experience training or qualifying operators is a plus
  • Familiarity with pyrolysis, gasification, or thermal processing
