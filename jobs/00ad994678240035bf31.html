Fluence (Nasdaq: FLNC) is a global market leader delivering intelligent energy storage and optimization software for renewables and storage. Our solutions and operational services are helping to create a more resilient grid and unlock the full potential of renewable portfolios. With gigawatts of successful implementations across nearly 50 markets, we are transforming the way we power our world for a more sustainable future. For more information, please visit fluenceenergy.com . Job Description: This position reports to the Director of Portfolio Finance Management and is responsible for supporting Portfolio Finance Managers with the administrative financial tasks of large projects in the Americas region. This includes setting up projects in the ERP, maintaining resource assignments, supporting purchasing transactions and gathering documentation and information for various commercial topics. The position requires close working relationships with the Portfolio Finance Managers and the Project Managers of the various projects. This role requires a background in accounting & finance and working with construction projects.
