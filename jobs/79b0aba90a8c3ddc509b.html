Job Description:

As a Working Student – Electrical Design & Engineering Tools, you will support the General Engineering team in developing and maintaining electrical design deliverables and improving internal engineering efficiency through digital and AI-based tools. You will work closely with electrical engineers and solution engineers. This role provides hands-on exposure to real-world energy storage projects and engineering workflows, offering an excellent opportunity to apply your technical knowledge while contributing to process standardization and automation within Fluence.

Key Responsibilities

  • Support the creation and maintenance of electrical diagrams and design documentation (e.g., single-line diagrams, wiring diagrams, templates) using CAD tools
  • Assist in the development and improvement of standardized engineering templates and technical documentation packages
  • Contribute to the maintenance and structuring of engineering data, including BoMs, datasheets, and documentation repositories
  • Support the development of engineering tools and scripts (e.g., Python-based utilities, data processing tools) to improve internal workflows and efficiency
  • Explore and prototype AI-based solutions to automate repetitive engineering tasks and enhance productivity (e.g., document generation, data structuring, quality checks) Assist engineers in processing and organizing project-specific documentation for customer deliverables
  • Collaborate with internal stakeholders to understand workflows and identify opportunities for process improvements and standardization
  • Support continuous improvement initiatives across engineering processes, tools, and documentation

Required Skills & Qualifications

  • Completed Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering (or closely related field)
  • Currently enrolled in a Master’s program in Electrical Engineering, Renewable Energy, or similar
  • Solid understanding of electrical engineering fundamentals, especially low-voltage systems
  • Experience with CAD tools (e.g., AutoCAD, EPLAN, or similar) for electrical diagram creation
  • Good working knowledge of MS Office (Excel, Word, PowerPoint)
  • Basic programming knowledge (preferably Python) or strong interest in developing scripting skills
  • Structured and detail-oriented working style
  • Fluent in English (written and spoken)

Additional Skills

  • Interest or initial experience in automation, digitalization, or AI-based tools
  • Familiarity with engineering documentation, electrical layouts, or BoMs
  • Understanding of renewable energy systems or battery energy storage systems (BESS)
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Proactive mindset and willingness to take initiative
  • Comfortable working in a cross-functional and international team environment
