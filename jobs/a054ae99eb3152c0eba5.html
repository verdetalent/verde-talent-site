Full description
Are you motivated by contributing to the strategic direction and priorities of a global organization? Do you thrive in high-impact projects that require strong analytical thinking and structured problem-solving in a complex environment? Then you might be our new Senior Specialist, Corporate Strategic Projects. Group Staff Functions (CEO) > Corporate Strategy The Corporate Strategy team partners closely with Vestas’ CEO, Executive Management, and Board of Directors to shape the company’s strategic direction and ensure effective execution of strategic priorities. The team plays a central role in orchestrating the annual strategic planning process, monitoring strategy execution, and managing selected enterprise-wide strategic initiatives critical to Vestas’ long-term competitiveness. We operate globally across Vestas’ regions and the value chain, working closely with senior leaders in all business areas. The team is characterized by high exposure to top management, deep analytical rigor, and a clear focus on business impact. As Senior Specialist, Corporate Strategy, you will report to Jacob Lihn, SVP Head of Corporate Strategy, M&A and Global Intelligence and work closely with the Heads of Corporate Strategic Planning and Strategic Projects and act as a key support to the CEO on strategy-related materials towards the Board of Directors, while also overseeing select corporate strategic projects. Responsibilities In this role, you will be responsible for preparing and coordinating high-quality strategy materials for the Executive Management and Board of Directors and supporting broader corporate strategy initiatives.
