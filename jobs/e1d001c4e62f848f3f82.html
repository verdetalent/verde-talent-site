**Job Summary**

Senior Engineer:

Includes senior to technical expert engineers, able to perform difficult and complex work requiring thorough knowledge of the subject matter and significant experience within functional assignment area. Staff Engineer:

Serve as a technical expert on engineering related legal and regulatory issues to outside entities.

**Job Summary**

Develop, design, analyze and evaluate transmission facility additions/modifications by applying engineering theories, principles, and practices that contribute to safe, reliable, and cost-effective transmission line designs.

Primary Responsibilities:

  • Provide conceptual scoping, design and estimates for high voltage transmission line projects.  This will include, but not limited to, building new lines, re-building and modifying existing facilities, fiber installations, and customer relocations.
  • Provide feasibility analysis and evaluation of high voltage electric transmission line facilities.
  • Select most cost-effective solution to achieve desired reliability for a given project.
  • Support projects through the State Cooperation Commission application process by providing engineering expertise in community and local government engagement, as well as during witness testimony at hearings, if needed
  • Interface with other departments or groups such as Project Management, Construction, Supply Chain, Permitting & Route Selection, Real Estate, Right of Way Administration, and Transmission O&M.
  • Provide detailed design for high voltage transmission line projects.  This will include, but not limited to, building new lines, re-building and modifying existing facilities, fiber installations, and customer relocations.
  • Provide field support for construction activities related to transmission line projects. This will include, but not limited to, construction site visits and project meetings.
  • Interface with other departments or groups such as Project Management, Construction, Supply Chain, Permitting, Real Estate, Right of Way Administration, and Transmission O&M.
