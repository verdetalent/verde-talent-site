About the department

The Global Offshore CPX Cost Management team advances offshore projects by delivering dependable cost estimates, clear visibility into costs, and support for informed decision-making throughout the project lifecycle. Collaborating closely with internal teams, the group creates project budgets, leads benchmarking efforts, and enhances costing methods. As part of Global Offshore Customer Project Execution (CPX), we ensure cost competitiveness while maintaining robust cost governance and risk awareness.

Your responsibilities – here's where you can get involved!

As part of our team, you will support offshore wind projects around the world by delivering dependable cost insights and promoting commercial Excellence.

  • You are responsible for developing construction budgets and cost estimates for offshore wind projects in markets around the world
  • You collaborate closely with Bid Management, Project Management, and other key stakeholders, delivering practical support on cost matters
  • You enable cost transparency and continuous improvement through benchmarking, business analysis, and data-driven insights to enhance business performance and estimate accuracy
  • You advance costing tools, processes, and governance standards, ensuring lessons learned are captured and embedded into future projects

Your profile – what makes you well suited for this role

  • You hold a degree in Finance, Economics, Business Administration, or Engineering
  • You bring 2-3 years of experience in cost management, project controls, commercial management, estimating, or financial analysis
  • You have solid analytical skills and advanced Excel capabilities; experience with BI tools, SQL, Copilot Agent Builder, or data analytics is a plus
  • You ideally have experience in offshore wind, energy, infrastructure, or other large-scale project environments
  • You combine commercial awareness with a forward-thinking, solution-oriented mindset and enjoy turning complex data into actionable insights
  • You cultivate productive stakeholder relationships and make complex concepts clear, engaging, and easy to follow
  • You work in a structured, practical, and detail-oriented manner, taking charge of your tasks and delivering high-quality results
  • You thrive in collaborative and international environments, working effectively across teams, functions, and organizational boundaries
  • Very good English skills, both written and spoken, round off your profile

About Vestas

Vestas is the energy industry’s global partner on sustainable energy solutions. We are specialised in designing, manufacturing, installing, and servicing wind turbines, both onshore and offshore.

Across the globe, we have installed more wind power than anyone else. We consider ourselves pioneers within the industry, as we continuously aim to design new solutions and technologies to create a more sustainable future for all of us. With more than 200 GW of wind power installed worldwide and 40+ years of experience in wind energy, we have an unmatched track record demonstrating our expertise within the field.

With 39,000 employees globally, we are a diverse team united by a common goal: to power the solution – today, tomorrow, and far into the future.

Vestas promotes a diverse workforce which embraces all social identities and is free of any discrimination. We commit to create and sustain an environment that acknowledges and harvests different experiences, skills, and perspectives. We also aim to give everyone equal access to opportunity.
