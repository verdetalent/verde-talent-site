Full description

The Production Technician I is an intermediate-level position in the middle submodule to the finishing end of the manufacturing process. The core function is to safely operate specific equipment, manage material and processes to manufacture high-quality, photovoltaic solar modules at the required rate. Additionally, Technician I’s will be responsible for conducting operational preventative maintenance procedures, addressing intermediate-level alarms, performing basic quality control procedures, and interfacing with equipment and factory computer systems.

Required Skills/Competencies:

  • Safety First mindset
  • Maintain a high standard for Quality
  • Basic troubleshooting and problem-solving skills.
  • Basic Math skills appropriate to understand typical manufacturing production metrics

  • Perform maintenance on equipment, in the required time, with hand tools and instrumentation
  • Interface with equipment computers to monitor parameters and alarms
  • Monitor process data & make decisions based on procedures to maintain process control
