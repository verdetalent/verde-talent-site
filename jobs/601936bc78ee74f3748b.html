Full description
Basic Job Functions: Performs Payroll processing and analysis and review of payroll data. Serves as specialist on new tax/payroll requirements and works with ADP to ensure that needs are properly addressed. Administers payroll for expatriates and short/long term project assignees. Education/Experience: 3 + years’ experience in Payroll or Accounting. Strong knowledge of state and federal Payroll related regulations. Working knowledge of ADP payroll and Oracle timekeeping systems. 2 - year college degree required in accounting or related fields; 4-year degree preferred. Required Skills/Competencies: Highly motivated, people sensitive, results oriented. Excellent interpersonal and management skills. Process oriented with strong organizational and communication skills. Must be detailed oriented. Strong research and problem-solving skills. Aptitude for learning and utilizing systems. Need to be hands-on. Knowledge of multi-state Payroll process and tax implications. Proficient in MS Word, Excel, Access, Power Point, etc. Proven computer skills in ADP and Oracle timekeeping systems. Essential Responsibilities: Perform payroll processing in a timely manner while ensuring accurate results. Analyze and review payroll files prior to submission. Identify, research, and assist in the resolution of Payroll related discrepancies. Prepare and/or reconcile periodic tax and compliance filings. Serve as payroll liaison with HR and the relocation company to properly process payroll for short and long-term assignees. Maintain working knowledge of international and domestic tax regulations to properly process payroll for expatriates. Prepare journal entries for Bi-Weekly payroll processing and monthly accruals. Prepare account reconciliations for all payroll balance sheet accounts in adherence with accounting deadlines.
