Full description

Basic Job Functions:

  • Performs Payroll processing and analysis and review of payroll data.  Serves as specialist on new tax/payroll requirements and works with ADP to ensure that needs are properly addressed.  Administers payroll for expatriates and short/long term project assignees.

Education/Experience:

  • 3 + years’ experience in Payroll or Accounting.
  • Strong knowledge of state and federal Payroll related regulations.
  • Working knowledge of ADP payroll and Oracle timekeeping systems.
  • 2 - year college degree required in accounting or related fields; 4-year degree preferred.

Required Skills/Competencies:

  • Highly motivated, people sensitive, results oriented.
  • Excellent interpersonal and management skills.
  • Process oriented with strong organizational and communication skills.
  • Must be detailed oriented.
  • Strong research and problem-solving skills.
  • Aptitude for learning and utilizing systems.
  • Need to be hands-on.
  • Knowledge of multi-state Payroll process and tax implications.
  • Proficient in MS Word, Excel, Access, Power Point, etc.
  • Proven computer skills in ADP and Oracle timekeeping systems.

Essential Responsibilities:

  • Perform payroll processing in a timely manner while ensuring accurate results.
  • Analyze and review payroll files prior to submission.
  • Identify, research, and assist in the resolution of Payroll related discrepancies.
  • Prepare and/or reconcile periodic tax and compliance filings.
  • Serve as payroll liaison with HR and the relocation company to properly process payroll for short and long-term assignees.
  • Maintain working knowledge of international and domestic tax regulations to properly process payroll for expatriates.
  • Prepare journal entries for Bi-Weekly payroll processing and monthly accruals.
  • Prepare account reconciliations for all payroll balance sheet accounts in adherence with accounting deadlines.
  • Research general ledger account discrepancies and work with accounting team to resolve any issues.
  • Work with state agencies and Corporate Tax to establish tax accounts for withholding and SUI in new jurisdictions.
  • Work with ADP to define requirements and test changes to configuration.
  • Prepare state submissions to obtain tax credits and perform other reporting as needed.
  • Provide required ADP reporting to internal departments such as Tax and Stock Administration.
  • Open tickets with ADP through CRM portal and manage ticket completion.
  • Perform Oracle administration, a good understanding of the inner workings of the system. Configure new pay rules and troubleshoot at system level as needed.
  • Comprehend the automated interfaces to payroll, such as the interface to Fidelity, Benefits Administration, etc.
  • Employee Liaison, manage payroll support in box.
  • Streamline payroll process and execute changes effectively.
  • Perform ad-hoc payroll as needed.
  • Liaison with Finance on wire transfers and payments.
  • SOX Compliance. Perform assigned controls in a timely manner.
  • Other duties as assigned.
  • Job description subject to change at any time.

Reporting Relationships:

  • Reports to Lead Payroll USA.
  • This position will not have direct reports.

Travel:

  • No travel is required.
