Procurement Process & Capability is under the Procurement Transformation & Operations Department with Procurement. We are responsible for enabling a well-functioning, future-ready Procurement organization through process excellence, governance, digitalization, and capability development. The department supports Procurement Transformation initiatives by managing end-to-end procurement process design, implementation, and continuous improvement, while ensuring alignment between processes, systems, policies, and organizational capabilities. Working closely with Procurement, Digital Solutions, and business stakeholders, the team establishes and maintains governance frameworks, supports audit readiness and compliance requirements, advances process digitalization and automation, and develops competency frameworks, training programs, and capability-building initiatives that enable sustainable adoption of new ways of working and support the long-term transformation of Procurement organization.
