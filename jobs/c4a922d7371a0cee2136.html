Full description

Invenergy is North America’s largest privately held developer, owner, and operator of power infrastructure. With 25 years of trusted execution, we deliver reliable, affordable energy through a diverse portfolio that includes natural gas, solar, land-based wind, energy storage, transmission, and domestic manufacturing. Headquartered in Chicago, we develop, own, and operate large scale projects that power communities and support the energy future.

This position will be open for application for at least 3 calendar days from the posting date. This position will remain open for application based on business need, which may be before or after the 3-day posting window.

Job Description

Position Overview

As the Manager of Contracts, you will participate in the negotiation of Power Purchase Agreements, Build Transfer and Development Transfer agreements for renewable energy, thermal energy, and data center projects, as well as assist with customer presentations. The ideal candidate will possess the ability to comprehend, organize, and communicate the complex risks associated with these types of transactions. In addition, the candidate will have a strong understanding of project development and/or project management including development milestones, project schedule, and technical intricacies of a project. This is an ideal opportunity for a project developer or project manager looking to broaden their experience in commercial transactions.

This position is on-site at Invenergy's downtown Chicago, IL office.

Responsibilities

  • Participate in the preparation and negotiation of Power Purchase (PPA), Build Transfer (BT) and Development Transfer (DT) Agreements.
  • Lead coordination efforts with internal development and construction teams for contracting activities for multiple projects.
  • Lead coordination efforts with development, finance, engineering and other departments as it relates to contracting activities for multiple project sites.
  • Assist origination team with RFP responses related to PPA, BT, and DT transactions.

Minimum Qualifications

  • 8+ years of relevant experience across project development, project management, power origination, business development, real estate, or asset management
  • Experience in the energy, infrastructure, utilities, data center, or commercial real estate industries
  • Bachelor's and/or master's degree in business, finance, or engineering.
  • Experience with Power Purchase Agreements, Build Transfer Agreements, Development Transfer Agreements, and/or EPC transactions across multiple power markets and technology.
  • Eligible to work in the United States without the need for employer visa sponsorship now or in the future.

Preferred Skills

  • Over 8+ years of experience in project development, project management, power origination, business development, real estate, or asset management in the energy, infrastructure, or utilities sector.
  • 5+ years of experience negotiating PPAs, BTAs, or DTs preferred. Candidates with significant experience negotiating Energy Service Agreements (ESAs) or other commercial agreements with utilities, data center operators, or large energy customers will also be considered.
  • Legal experience and/or experience negotiating other types of agreements.
  • Ability to comprehend complex documents and communicate transactional risks and project impact.
  • Excellent solution-seeking, teamwork, leadership and communication skills which extend across all organizational and management levels.
  • Critical thinking skills with the ability to identify and solve complex problems
  • Ability to manage priorities, meet deadlines and complete time-sensitive work.
