The Opportunity

The Operations Support Specialist is responsible for the monitoring, reporting, and analyzing the power delivery flow from Enel Green Power North America, Inc (EGPNA) generating facilities to the bulk electric power system. This position ensures proper meter function and accurate generation reporting for all current and new projects under the EGPNA umbrella. This includes coordinating with various internal (EGPNA Control Room, Energy Management, and Plant Operations Teams) and external (Transmission System Operators, Balancing Authorities, Schedulers, etc.) regarding power generating facilities in matters relating to Meter, Generation, and Outage Management processes for the EGPNA Fleet.

This position will also focus on the efficiency and standardization of the control room operations, processes, procedures and tool improvements to support the remote control and monitoring of power generation.

This role will support the CMR Operations toolset, implementation and sign off of new CMR SCADA systems, Fault Management, Automation, meeting NERC requirements, supporting PI Data infrastructure.

This position will report directly to the Operations Support Manager.

What You'll Do at Enel North America:

Operations Support Specialist responsibilities include, but are not limited to:

  • Generation & Metering

o Monitor Itron’s MV-WEB database for meter data integrity

o Ensure meters for new plants are integrated into PI and Itron prior to first

synchronization of the plant

o Responsible for managing Check Meter Testing Coordination and Scheduling

o Maintain ISO Metering Protocols for Testing Intervals

o Manage metering services/agreements

o Interface with digital hub for metering technical/communications issues

o Monitor automated "watchdog" systems to notify for nonfunctioning or

noncommunicating meters

o Oversight of generation review, approval, and reporting processes within O&M

o Interface with E&C in the design of the metering schemes to ensure design meets all internal and external needs.

  • Outage Management

o Coordinate all significant planned outages with internal and external stakeholders

ensuring the least impact on EGPNA operations.

o Troubleshoot grid outages and develop action plans with goal of minimizing any

negative impacts on the EGPNA portfolio of projects.

o Coordinate any potential grid operation impacts with Energy Management to optimize transmission and congestion issues and energy revenues.

o Interface with Energy Management for PPA outage related reporting requirements

o Act as Control Room Lead for outage management software utilization and

coordination, including user management, interfacing with other departments, etc.

o Interact with Control Room for outages as they approach to ensure Control Room has up to date information

o Acts as the primary interface for non-real-time third party coordination

o Participation in operations committees, etc. as required by PPAs, as well as operating subcommittees of ISOs, etc.

o Primary interface with Transmission Group and Energy Management for operational

needs

  • Control Room Support

o Developing CMR user interfaces for real time operations

o Developing automation of active and reactive power systems including Ancillary Service market requirements

o Support remote data sets to external regulatory and customer entities (ICCP, RTU)

o Manage tracking of CMR Operational Improvements

o Ensure that CMR system and procedures meet NERC and other compliance requirements

o Interface between CMR and the ICS Service Desk to support ticket escalation and resolution

o Continued development of tools for CMR

  • Documentation Management
