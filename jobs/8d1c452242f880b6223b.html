Full description
Are you looking for an exciting job where you can put your skills and talents to work at a company you can feel proud to be a part of? Do you want a workplace that will challenge you and offer you opportunities to learn and grow? A position at Xcel Energy could be just what you’re looking for. Position Summary Develop, retain, and administer transmission related agreements for Xcel Energy Transmission's present and prospective investor-owned utilities, municipalities, rural electric associations and other non-retail customers. Oversee and administer contracts with Power Producers who interconnect to the Xcel Energy system and deliver their product through the Xcel Energy transmission system. Develop customer driven solutions for, and resolve disputes with, aforementioned customers. Interface with Transmission Operations, control areas, reserve groups, RTO's, FERC and neighboring transmission systems. Develop high-level relationships with industry customers while having extensive knowledge of Xcel Energy competitors. Essential Responsibilities Conceive, coordinate, negotiate and implement complex proposals, contracts and amendments to retain and increase transmission services for existing and new customers. Maintain strong understanding of FERC orders/rulings, PSC requirements and RTO policies and procedures which affect interconnection and transmission service. Develop and maintain relationships with wholesale customers. Liaison to ISO, FERC and other non-retail Xcel Energy functions. Initiate, develop, and maintain relationships with other utilities, suppliers, and other companies. Identify and analyze customers' and competitors' strategies, needs and plans, create and develop market and competitive intelligence and market plans for new transmission services. Originate, lead and direct interdisciplinary teams to provide and implement customized solutions to customers that bring added value to Xcel Energy delivery business unit.
