About Fervo Energy

Fervo Energy (NASDAQ: FRVO) is a modern power company developing next-generation geothermal resources to meet the growing demand for affordable, reliable, 24/7 carbon-free energy. Our mission is to transform geothermal into America’s most dependable and affordable source of clean, around-the-clock power. By combining innovations in horizontal drilling, fiber-optic sensing, and advanced reservoir engineering, Fervo has developed a repeatable approach to building utility-scale enhanced geothermal systems. Our flagship Cape Station development in Utah is planned to deliver 500 MW of firm, carbon-free power, and we are advancing a development pipeline of more than 50 GW. Join us and work alongside world-class engineers, geoscientists, operators, project builders, and business leaders working to make geothermal a cornerstone of a more reliable, affordable, and sustainable energy future.

Position Description

The Head of Investor Relations will report to the Senior Director, Corporate Development & Investor Relations and work closely with executive leadership and cross-functional teams across finance, legal, and project development. This role is suited for a seasoned finance professional with deep public company experience, strong capital markets fluency, and a genuine passion for the role.

The Head of Investor Relations will serve as Fervo’s primary point of contact for the investment community; owning the company’s investor communications strategy and helping shape how Fervo is understood and valued by current and prospective capital partners. This role demands strong financial fluency, excellent written and verbal communication skills, and the ability to translate complex technical and financial information into clear, compelling narratives for a sophisticated investor audience.

Success in this position means building trusted, long-term relationships with institutional and retail investors, delivering high-quality reporting and disclosure, and ensuring Fervo’s story is told consistently and credibly across all investor touchpoints. The Director of IR will play a visible, strategic role, spearheading quarterly earnings processes, managing sell-side relationships, supporting Board reporting, and helping position Fervo as a pioneering voice in energy.

Responsibilities

Investor Relations & Communications

  • Own Fervo’s investor communications strategy, including investor presentations, quarterly reporting packages, press releases, earnings call scripts, and other external materials.
  • Serve as a primary point of contact for current and prospective investors, managing ongoing relationships and ensuring timely, accurate responses to investor inquiries.
  • Engage proactively with the equity research community, managing analyst relationships and ensuring Fervo’s financial narrative is well understood by the sell side.
  • Proactively identify and pursue new institutional investors, managing a targeted outreach program alongside inbound relationship management.
  • Maintain a consistent and compelling investor narrative across all touchpoints, ensuring alignment between external messaging and internal strategy.

Capital Markets & Events

  • Manage investor conferences, roadshows, and investor day events- including targeting, logistics, and preparation of supporting materials.
  • Monitor and synthesize equity research coverage, peer company activity, shareholder base changes, and broader capital markets developments into regular briefings for senior leadership.
  • Track 13F filings and institutional ownership shifts, maintaining a current and accurate picture of Fervo’s shareholder base for leadership.
  • Maintain a comprehensive tracker of all investor and analyst interactions, including relationship status, follow-up commitments, and engagement history.
  • Support reactive and crisis communications, managing investor messaging around material events, market volatility, or other developments requiring rapid response.

Investor Reporting & Disclosure

  • Own all investor reporting obligations, including quarterly and annual reporting packages and earnings releases, ensuring accuracy, consistency, and timeliness.
  • Lead end-to-end earnings call preparation, including script development, management Q&A prep, and post-call transcript review.
  • Coordinate SEC filing obligations- including 10-K, 10-Q, and 8-K filings- in close partnership with legal and finance teams.
  • Ensure compliance with Reg FD and other public company disclosure requirements, serving as an internal resource on disclosure best practices and material information handling.
  • Contribute to Board of Directors reporting, giving directors clear visibility into investor sentiment, capital markets activity, and financing progress.
  • Liaise with the transfer agent and support equity plan communications to employees as needed.

Planning & Decision Support

  • Partner closely with the Corporate Development team to align investor communications with broader capital strategy and transaction activity.
  • Contribute to long-range planning and scenario analysis, providing an investor perspective on capital allocation and business strategy.
  • Synthesize market intelligence, policy developments, and competitive dynamics.

Qualifications

Required

  • Bachelor’s degree in finance, economics, accounting, business, or a related field.
  • 10+ years of experience in investor relations, investment banking, or equity research, including direct experience at or covering a public company.
  • Proven track record of building and managing relationships with institutional investors and equity research analysts.
  • Deep familiarity with public company reporting obligations, SEC disclosure requirements, and Reg FD compliance.
  • Strong proficiency in Excel and PowerPoint, with the ability to build and present compelling investor materials.
  • Solid command of financial statements and core corporate finance principles.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to distill complex financial and technical information for investor audiences.
  • Ability to manage multiple workstreams and deliver on deadlines in a fast-moving environment.

Preferred

  • Experience in the energy, infrastructure, or power sector — as an IR professional or analyst covering the space.
  • Familiarity with project finance structures, including tax equity, debt, and equity arrangements.
  • Experience managing earnings calls, investor days, and roadshows end-to-end.
  • Advanced degree (MBA) or CFA designation.
