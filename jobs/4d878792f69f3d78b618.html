L’opportunité

L’unité d’affaires Grid Integration de Hitachi Énergie est à la recherche d’un(e) Ingénieur(e) en conception contrôle et protection HVDC pour soutenir la conception et la réalisation de systèmes avancés de contrôle et de protection destinés à des postes convertisseurs HVDC au Canada et à l’international. Dans ce rôle, vous serez responsable des activités de conception matérielle essentielles au fonctionnement fiable d’infrastructures de transport d’énergie à grande échelle. Vous développerez des schémas électriques, des plans d’armoires, des listes de câblage et divers livrables d’ingénierie contribuant au transport efficace de l’électricité sur de longues distances, à l’intégration des énergies renouvelables et à l’amélioration de la fiabilité du réseau. En participant aux phases de conception conceptuelle et détaillée, vous collaborerez avec des équipes multidisciplinaires, des spécialistes de la fabrication et des clients afin de livrer des solutions conformes aux exigences techniques, aux échéanciers de projet et aux normes applicables. Ce poste vous permettra de contribuer à des projets HVDC complexes, d’approfondir votre expertise technique en contrôle et protection et de participer au développement des infrastructures énergétiques de demain.

Comment vous aurez un impact

  • Diriger et prendre en charge les activités de conception matérielle pour les systèmes de contrôle et de protection HVDC.
  • Développer les schémas électriques et les plans d’armoires, y compris les nomenclatures, les câbles d’interconnexion, les listes de câblage et la documentation requise pour les essais de système en usine (Factory System Testing – FST).
  • Contribuer aux phases de conception conceptuelle et détaillée des systèmes de contrôle et de protection.
  • Concevoir de nouvelles armoires de contrôle et de protection à partir de dessins de référence, de normes établies et de bases de données d’ingénierie.
  • Fournir un soutien technique et du mentorat aux autres membres de l’équipe de conception matérielle.
  • Collaborer avec les clients et les partenaires internes afin de garantir la conformité des solutions aux exigences, spécifications et normes applicables.
  • Participer au suivi de la fabrication, aux vérifications de qualité et à la planification des livraisons afin d’assurer l’exécution efficace des projets.
  • Contribuer à l’amélioration continue des outils, processus et méthodes de travail en proposant des solutions innovantes.
  • Responsable d’assurer la conformité aux réglementations, procédures et directives internes et externes applicables.
  • in an international and multicultural environment while collaborating with a broad range of stakeholders.
  • Travel requirments 15%
  • Fluency in English and French, both written and spoken, is required to support collaboration with internal and external stakeholders.

The Opportunity

Hitachi Energy's Grid Integration Business is seeking an HVDC Control & Protection Design Engineer to support the design and delivery of advanced control and protection systems for HVDC converter stations across Canada and around the world. In this role, you will take ownership of hardware design activities that are essential to the reliable operation of large-scale power transmission infrastructure. You will develop electrical schematics, cabinet layouts, wiring documentation, and engineering deliverables that support the transmission of electricity over long distances while helping utilities integrate renewable energy sources and strengthen grid reliability. Working across both conceptual and detailed design phases, you will collaborate with multidisciplinary engineering teams, manufacturing specialists, and customers to deliver solutions that meet technical requirements, project schedules, and industry standards. This position offers the opportunity to work on complex HVDC projects, expand your technical expertise in control and protection systems, and contribute to infrastructure that supports the future of Canada's power grid and the global energy transition.

How You'll Make an Impact

  • Lead and take ownership of hardware design activities for HVDC control and protection systems.
  • Develop cubicle electrical diagrams and layouts, including bills of materials, interconnection cables, wiring lists, and Factory System Testing (FST) documentation.
  • Contribute to both conceptual and detailed design activities for control and protection systems.
  • Create new control and protection cabinet designs using established engineering databases, standards, and base drawings.
  • Provide technical guidance and support to other members of the hardware design team.
  • Collaborate with customers and internal stakeholders to ensure solutions meet applicable requirements, specifications, and industry standards.
  • Participate in manufacturing reviews, quality checks, and delivery planning to support high-quality, on-time project execution.
  • Identify opportunities to improve engineering processes, tools, and ways of working while contributing to continuous improvement initiatives.
  • Responsible to ensure compliance with applicable external and internal regulations, procedures, and guidelines.

Your Background

  • Candidate must already have work authorization that would permit them to work for Hitachi Energy in Canada.
  • Bachelor's or master's degree in Electrical Engineering or a related field.
  • Minimum of 4 years of experience preparing electrical drawings and layouts using electrical CAD software.
  • Strong interest and practical experience in electrical system design, electrical schematics, and cabinet layouts.
  • Experience with control system CAD tools such as Engineering Base or Ruplan is considered an asset.
  • Professional Engineer (P.Eng.) designation, or eligibility for registration with a Canadian provincial engineering association, is considered an asset.
  • Experience providing technical guidance or coordinating the work of a small engineering team is considered an asset.
  • Strong organizational skills with a structured, persistent, and results-oriented approach.
  • Proven problem-solving abilities with the capacity to simplify complex technical topics and develop practical solutions.
  • Ability to work effectively in an international and multicultural environment while collaborating with a broad range of stakeholders.
  • Fluency in English and French, both written and spoken, is required to support collaboration with internal and external stakeholders.
