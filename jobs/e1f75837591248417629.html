Development Manager Role Overview Title: Development Manager Hours: Full time; Salaried Location: Salt Lake City, UT BenefitsEligible: Yes Manager: Chief Development Officer Mission – Why we exist and why we need you Geothermal energy is the most abundant renewable energy source in the world. However, historically it’s been hard to find and expensive to develop. At Zanskar, we’re using advanced technology to find and develop new geothermal resources that make baseload geothermal a cost-effective and vital contributor to a carbon-free energy future. We are revolutionizing the exploration process, setting new industry standards for discovery rates by finding new prospects at an unprecedented scale and working to develop faster and cheaper with scale and reduced risk drilling. The development team at Zanskar is advancing a multi-gigawatt portfolio of firm, carbon-free energy projects. We need you to take ownership of a portfolio of greenfield projects that you’ll oversee from inception to the start of construction; to bring projects online, on schedule and within budgets that you build. You’ll be working in close coordination with interdisciplinary teams across Zanskar as well as third-party consultants to ensure we execute our vision of building best-in-class geothermal energy projects. Outcomes - Problems you’ll solve Success in this role includes identifying new commercially viable sites for geothermal projects, helping to secure necessary land rights (including geothermal rights and surface use rights) applying for and leading all discretionary topside permits with support from the subsurface permitting, geology, drilling, transmission engineering teams. Additionally, you will support in obtaining interconnection rights and assisting with commercial offtake for a portfolio of geothermal development projects located across the western USA.
