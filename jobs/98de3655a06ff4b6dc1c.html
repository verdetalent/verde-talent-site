Full description

Are you looking for an exciting job where you can put your skills and talents to work at a company you can feel proud to be a part of? Do you want a workplace that will challenge you and offer you opportunities to learn and grow?  A  position at Xcel Energy could be just what you’re looking for.

We anticipate the need for one or more General Plant Helper/Repairman to be located at the High Bridge Combined Cycle Plant. The starting wage rate for this position will be $42.96 per hour. This position will be posted for 10 working days from July 13, 2026, to July 24, 2026. The High Bridge plant is located at: 155 Randolph Road, St. Paul, MN.

Entry level responsibilities include, but are not limited to the following:

Cleaning and monitoring of power generating equipment throughout the natural gas combined cycle plant; general plant clean up; general maintenance duties as trained and assigned. Responsibilities may also include: snow removal; grass cutting and grounds keeping.

Position will be required to perform/accomplish the following: complete training and qualification in general mechanical maintenance skills; be trained on and operate plant equipment; actively participate in required training programs, to include boiler license certification, as directed by plant management.

Qualifications

1. High School Diploma or equivalent.

2. Must have successfully passed the Edison Electric Institute Power Plant Maintenance and Plant Operator selection exams or be a grandfathered employee.

3. Prefer post-secondary technical training in a related area.

4. Must have a valid driver’s license.

5. Applicant is required to successfully pass the Xcel Energy screening requirements, which include a drug test and security clearance. Other exams may be required by federal regulations, such as OSHA and DOT.

Requirements:

1. Able to lift up to 50 pounds safely.

2. Work in inclement weather.

3. Must be qualified to wear a respirator.

4. Will be required to perform duties when necessary, from scaffolds and catwalks in high and confined spaces.

5. Must be able to work overtime and various shifts as required. Selection criteria may include testing and/or assessment.
