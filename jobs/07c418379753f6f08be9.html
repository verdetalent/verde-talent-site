Commercial Improvement Manager

ScottishPower HQ, Glasgow (hybrid)

Salary from £66,000 + car allowance, up to 20% bonus & other excellent benefits

Closing date- 12th of August 2026

Help us create a better future, quicker!

The Commercial Improvement Manager is responsible for driving end-to-end commercial improvement across the Business Energy portfolio, supporting SME and Industrial & Commercial (I&C) segments and optimisation of CPPA and sustainable energy solutions.

The role focuses on improving sales processes, customer journeys, systems and commercial frameworks to drive efficiency, scalability and performance.

Working cross-functionally across Sales, Pricing, Product, Operations, Digital and Wholesale, the role supports revenue growth, margin optimisation and improved customer outcomes.

What you’ll be doing

  • Drive commercial improvement initiatives across SME, I&C and PPA sales channels
  • Improve end-to-end customer lifecycle performance from lead through to billing
  • Support optimisation of CPPA end to end processes including contract negotiation, governance, onboarding and in contract lifecycle management.
  • Enhance sales effectiveness across direct and partner channels
  • Define and track commercial performance metrics
  • Support system and process improvements with Digital / IT
  • Ensure compliance with regulatory and governance frameworks
  • Deliver improvement roadmaps aligned to Business Energy strategy
  • Responsible for the collation of submission packs for responding to CPPA tenders/RfQs/RfPs, including pricing from each business to provide a full ScottishPower offering
  • Working with Compliance , Legal , Regulation and Cyber teams in assessing , designing, delivering or evolving necessary requirements across Business Energy Sales
  • Assist in the engagement of customers, both new and existing, to continue bi-lateral discussions on the pipeline of projects seeking a route to market through CPPAs
  • Supports SME and I&C customer base and revenue performance
  • Contributes to CPPA portfolio development and optimisation
  • Operates across Sales, Product, Pricing, Operations and Digital
  • Influences customer acquisition, onboarding and retention performance

What you’ll bring

  • Proven experience leading business transformation, process improvement or commercial change programmes within the energy, utilities or B2B services sector.
  • Demonstrable success delivering measurable commercial outcomes, such as revenue growth, margin improvement, cost reduction, customer experience improvements or operational efficiency gains
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Senior Stakeholder management and cross-functional delivery experience
  • Proven experience managing multiple initiatives across different business areas
  • Structured approach to project delivery
  • Understanding of Business Energy markets (SME/I&C) is highly desirable
  • PPA or renewable contract experience, alongside regulatory knowledge is desirable

Minimum Criteria

  • Demonstrated experience in commercial or operational improvement at a senior level
  • Strong stakeholder management skills
  • Ability to deliver measurable improvements
  • Ability to operate effectively in fast-changing environment
