About Commonwealth Fusion Systems:

Commonwealth Fusion Systems is on a mission to deliver the urgent transition to fusion energy.

Combining decades of research, top talent, and new technologies, we’re designing and building commercially viable fusion power plants. And we're working with policymakers and suppliers to build the energy industry of the future.

We’re in the best position to make it happen. Since 2018, we’ve raised nearly $3 billion in capital, making us the largest and leading private fusion company in the world.

Now we’re looking for more thinkers, doers, builders, and makers to join us. People who’ll bring new perspectives, solve tough problems, and thrive as part of a team.

If that’s you and this role fits, we want to hear from you.

Join the power movement as a Senior Talent Acquisition Recruiter - Contract

We have an opportunity to change the world and fight climate change by hiring world class talent. Commonwealth Fusion Systems is looking for a senior recruiter to own full-cycle recruiting across all departments. This individual will be part of a rapidly growing dynamic passionate company.

This role will be a great fit for you, if the following speaks to you...

➢ You are a great relationship builder and have expert business facing influencing skills.

➢ You are energized by the idea of trying new approaches, failing fast and continuously improving for better outcomes.

➢ You are passionate about building diverse teams.

➢ You will evaluate current recruiting practices, recommend changes to core processes to improve candidate experience, reduce organizational time on recruiting, and shorten time to fill.

What you'll do:: • Be a champion of CFS Energy’s operating principles and unique company culture

  • Manage the full-cycle recruitment process from sourcing to candidate decision meeting, ensuring an exceptional candidate journey
  • Execute high-volume requisitions, both technical and non-technical, across all departments including but not limited to supply chain, engineering, finance, operations, and manufacturing.
  • Build strong hiring manager relationships
  • Prioritize the candidate experience by ensuring stellar communication, high touch processes, and representing CFS
  • Look beyond direct applications and leverage search engines, career sites, recruiting events, and other innovative and creative sourcing techniques to find the best talent in the market
  • Lead new manager interview trainings and Fireside Chats to discuss the latest trends in recruiting with hiring managers
  • Collaborate with the talent acquisition and human resources team for recruitment best practices and growing the company What we’re looking for:: • Bachelor’s Degree or equivalent years of experience
  • 10+ years of full life cycle recruiting including targeted outreach, sourcing, hiring manager collaboration, compensation negotiation, scheduling, closing and onboarding of candidates
  • Well-versed in Diversity, Equity and Inclusion strategies, building networks within talent communities, and creative candidate lead generation
  • A solid understanding of recruiting metrics and how they drive performance
  • Demonstrated strength in communication, negotiation and organization skills
  • Demonstrated ability to successfully lead and facilitate interview stakeholders through the interview, selection and hiring process
  • Success at creating and executing complex recruitment strategies and techniques Bonus points: : • Knowledge of applicant tracking systems, including detailed reporting preferred
  • Exposure to US immigration practices is highly valued, but not required Essential Requirements: : • Perform activities such as typing, standing, or sitting for extended periods of time
  • Willingness to occasionally travel to recruiting events, batch hiring days, hiring training events, and team building sessions
  • Dedication to safety to mitigate industrial hazards that may include heat, cold, noise, fumes, strong magnets, lead (Pb), high voltage, and cryogenics
