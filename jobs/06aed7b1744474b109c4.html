Position Description:

Individual performs a wide variety of tasks including, but not limited to, assisting with the testing, maintenance, and operation of protection and control equipment including IED’s (Intelligent Electronic Devices), electromechanical and solid-state protective relaying, PLC (Programmable Logic Controller) equipment, Eversource NH telecommunication equipment (including fiber optics, two-way radio, and microwave), pole-top Distribution Automation equipment (NOVA, Viper, & Scadamate, reclosers), SCADA (Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition) communication systems, and switchboard devices.

Work will be performed under direct and limited supervision. Tasks include, but are not limited to, the installation, testing, and repair of protective relays, SCADA systems, and telecommunication equipment. This position requires the ability to perform math functions, have a working knowledge of personal computers, interpret electrical/electronic drawings, and comprehend manufacturer technical instruction manuals. The position also requires the ability to follow instruction, have good communication skills (oral and written), and work under limited supervision (when required).

In addition, individual will perform work associated with preventive and corrective maintenance on electronic circuits, operate test equipment, utilize hand/power tools, and work from elevated positions (ladders) or within confined spaces. Position requires travel throughout the Eversource territory. Individual filling this position will be required to work a reasonable amount of overtime when offered. Individual storm assignments will include, but are not limited to, damage assessment and service restoration. This position is one step in a multi-step progression program.

Starting Rate of Pay: $41.84 per hour

Position Requirements:

  • An Associate’s degree in electrical technology, electrical power system technology, electrical control systems, telecommunication technology, electrical engineering, electronic engineering, or electronics. Individuals with a BSEE or BSET degree are encouraged to apply.
