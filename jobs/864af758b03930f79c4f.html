Site Manager (Overhead Lines)

Site Based role – We have projects in the East Coast, Central Belt, Borders, Glasgow, Ayrshire & Dumfries and Galloway.This is a site-based position with travel across multiple project locations.

Salary up to £62,425 with a strong benefits package, overtime opportunities available (further details below) and our excellent pension scheme, where we’ll double match your contribution up to a company contribution of 10%.

Full Time / Perm role based on 37 hour week.

Closing Date: 24th July 2026

Help us create a better future, quicker!

At ScottishPower, our people are the driving force behind everything we achieve. Their energy, expertise and ambition power our success — and we’re now looking for individuals who share that passion to help shape the future of our Transmission business within SP Energy Networks.

With major investment underway and some of the most ambitious infrastructure projects in our history, our Transmission teams are delivering essential upgrades that will transform how energy is transmitted across the South of Scotland. From large-scale network developments to future-focused expansion, the work you’ll be part of will play a vital role in enabling a cleaner, greener energy system.

As a Site Manager (Overhead Lines), you’ll join a multi-disciplined team responsible for the construction of new build, replacement, refurbishment and major overhead line projects. You’ll be working as part of a team committed to delivering high-profile construction projects safely and efficiently.

Acting as the primary on-site representative, you’ll take ownership of site activities and provide technical support and guidance throughout the overhead line construction phase.

What you’ll be doing

  • Monitor and manage contractor programmes in line with overall project delivery timelines
  • Oversee the construction and commissioning of overhead line projects
  • Ensure compliance with Health & Safety legislation, CDM regulations, and environmental standards
  • Drive quality and environmental performance, ensuring mitigation measures are implemented and monitored
  • Support the delivery of works across distribution and transmission networks

What You’ll Bring

You’ll thrive in a contractor-led environment and be motivated by leading teams to deliver high-quality outcomes. Your ability to influence, challenge and collaborate will be key to success in this role.

We’re open to candidates from a range of backgrounds within overhead lines and utilities. You might already be a Site Manager, or be looking to step up from a hands-on or supervisory role.

We’d be keen to hear from you if you have:

  • Experience working on overhead line or transmission projects, ideally within utilities or infrastructure
  • Exposure to projects across distribution and/or transmission networks (11kV – 400kV)
  • Experience on higher voltage works (132kV, 275kV or 400kV) would be advantageous, but not essential
  • A good understanding of overhead line construction, maintenance or commissioning activities (e.g. foundations, pole/tower erection, stringing or reconductoring)
  • Experience working within a DNO, utilities, or infrastructure environment
  • Knowledge of CDM regulations, site safety and temporary works
  • Experience reviewing, approving or working with RAMS and permit systems
  • Strong communication skills and the ability to work effectively with contractors and site teams

Qualifications / certifications (or working towards):

  • SMSTS
  • CSCS Card
  • First Aid

We’re also keen to hear from

  • Experienced transmission & distribution linesmen/women looking to transition into site management.
  • Candidates with strong practical overhead line experience and a solid understanding of industry delivery.

Overtime Payments (Overtime is optional)

  • Mon–Sat (8pm–midnight): Time & ½
  • Mon–Sat (midnight–8am): Double Time
  • Sundays: Double Time

Why SP Energy Networks

SP Energy Networks is part of the Iberdrola Group, one of the world’s largest integrated utility companies and a world leader in wind energy. We keep electricity flowing to homes and businesses through Central and Southern Scotland, North Wales and in the North West of England. We operate over 4000km of cables and lines that make-up the transmission network – connecting infrastructure like wind farms into the electricity system. It’s a role that puts us right at the heart of Scotland’s ambition to be Net Zero by 2044. And we’re taking it very seriously.

We’re investing over £12 billion into our transmission network, directly supporting the rapid growth needed in renewable energy. With diverse opportunities across our businesses and a commitment to invest in our own internal talent, ScottishPower can offer people real career opportunities that meet personal and professional goals, in a global organisation.

Inclusion, diversity, and a social purpose are at the heart of everything we do. Together with our values, they bring us together into a stronger, more sustainable business with direct links to the communities we serve. It takes all kinds of people to build a large-scale business like ours, so whatever your background, you’ll fit right in.
