Position Specific Description

We are seeking a detail-oriented and organized Sr. Financial Analyst to provide support to our legal and compliance team. In this role, you will:

  • Lead all financial and accounting tasks to ensure the smooth operation of accounting functions.
  • Identify processes to improve or streamline in financial reporting or analysis.
  • Respond to compliance requests and prepare forms.
  • Prepare simple legal forms for attorney review.
  • Project manage reporting requests for data from the entity management database.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Coordinate with accounting to maintain the team's financial records.
  • Project Manager of team accounting and vendor payments.
  • Managing all vendor payments.
  • Assist with data entry and maintenance of financial records.
  • Prepare and process invoices, expense reports, and purchase orders.
  • Support reconciliation of accounts and verification of financial data.
  • Project manager for reporting out of the entity management database.
  • Review and prepare responses to compliance requests.
  • Assist with compiling reports and summaries for management review.
  • Lead liaison with financial teams to provide documentation and deliverables for requests.
  • Assist in the preparation of simple legal documents.
  • Skills should include strong proficiency with Excel, Word, including using Mail Merge features, ability to manipulate excel data from a database.
  • Strong proficiency in use of AI tools to reconcile and generate reports, including ChatGPT and/or CoPilot.

Job Overview

Employees in this role conduct complex operational analyses and lead medium to large project teams that support informed decision making within the business unit.

Job Duties & Responsibilities

  • Conducts analyses and prepares reports that provide operational and/or technical support to business operations
  • Conducts quantitative and qualitative research to support small to medium special projects
