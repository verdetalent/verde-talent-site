Student / Trainee / Apprentice | Full-Time | Quality, Health, Safety and Environment In Taranto, Vestas Blades Italia S.R.L. is operating one of the most modern blade factories in the world. In this factory we build blades for both on- and offshore wind turbines. Our clients range between small and medium local enterprises, project developers and large international independent power producers and grid operators. To expand our tactical capabilities, we are seeking a student specializing in Quality and Process Engineering. The "Quality and Process Engineering" department is responsible for the entire quality and process of the production lines present at the Vestas Blades Italia production Plant in Taranto. On top of this, it is also a key stakeholder for improvements of the process in terms of cost of poor quality (reworks and scraps costs reduction). As an intern you will closely work together with our engineers, the operation team and various project and factory stakeholders. The Department has an open and informal culture, where teamwork and good communication is key. Responsibilities You will work as an intern at a technical level in a role that reflects how your daily work will look like after you graduate. In practice, the nature of the work is relevant to the engineering education and consists of both development oriented tasks and tasks of a more routine nature. As an intern, you participate in our regular work processes and thus become an essential part of everyday life. The tasks can be related to the operation and quality of systems as well as optimization of processes in all types of technical systems. Additionally, you may assist with planning tasks, management etc. with a particular focus on optimization, operation, safety and the environment.
