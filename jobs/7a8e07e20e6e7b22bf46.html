Full description

Business Improvement & Implementation drives the deployment and execution of Global Operating Model (GOM) initiatives across regions. The function translates field insights and Operating Model Assessment findings into targeted improvement initiatives that close performance gaps, improve operational execution, and deliver measurable business value.

The team ensures effective regional anchoring of strategic priorities, GOM standards, and transformation initiatives through implementation support, assessments, gap closure, and value realization activities.

Responsibilties

- Act as the primary GOM interface for the AME region

- Deploy and secure adoption of GOM standards, processes, and ways of working

- Translate strategic priorities into clear regional actions and implementation plans

- Execute Operating Model Assessments and identify operational gaps and improvement opportunities

- Define and develop regional gap closure plans together with regional stakeholders

- Track progress, KPIs, risks, and implementation impact

- Support implementation of transformation initiatives linked to operational performance and scalability

- Provide structured field feedback to improve the Global Operating Model

- Build alignment and close collaboration with regional leadership teams

- Ensure initiatives deliver measurable operational and business value

Qualifications

- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Business, Engineering, Operations, or related field

- Experience within Operations, Business Improvement, Transformation, Strategy Execution, or Operational Excellence

- Experience working in cross-functional and regional/global organizations

- Experience in implementing and supporting change initiatives in operational environments

- Deep knowledge of operational workflows, performance management, and continuous improvement

- Experience within renewable energy, service operations, or industrial environments

- Fluent in English

WHAT WE OFFER

- Opportunity to play a key role in shaping and implementing the Operating Model across the AME region

- High exposure to regional and global leadership within an international environment

- Ability to deliver tangible operational improvements and business impact

Close collaboration with Operations, Strategy, Finance, SMO, and regional leadership teams

- A results-driven transformation environment that provides considerable ownership and cross-functional reach

- Opportunity to work on strategic and operational topics across multiple functions and regions

- Professional growth within a global renewable energy company focused on operational excellence and scalability

- A collaborative team environment focused on execution, continuous improvement, and value realization

About Vestas

Vestas is the energy industry’s global partner on sustainable energy solutions. We are specialised in designing, manufacturing, installing, and servicing wind turbines, both onshore and offshore.

Across the globe, we have installed more wind power than anyone else. We consider ourselves pioneers within the industry, as we continuously aim to design new solutions and technologies to create a more sustainable future for all of us. With more than 200 GW of wind power installed worldwide and 40+ years of experience in wind energy, we have an unmatched track record demonstrating our expertise within the field.

With 39,000 employees globally, we are a diverse team united by a common goal: to power the solution – today, tomorrow, and far into the future.

Vestas promotes a diverse workforce which embraces all social identities and is free of any discrimination. We commit to create and sustain an environment that acknowledges and harvests different experiences, skills, and perspectives. We also aim to give everyone equal access to opportunity.
