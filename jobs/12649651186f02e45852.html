Professional | Full-Time | Engineering Vestas Australia is offering an outstanding opportunity for an experienced Power Systems Engineers. The Australian Grid presents a challenging yet fascinating field to work in. With Vestas Australia's significant expansion, your expertise and experience will contribute to building a sustainable future. The Vestas APAC Power Systems Performance Analysis team is dedicated to coordinating, managing, and conducting in-depth power systems analysis related to the grid connection aspects of wind power plant projects. This analysis spans the complete lifecycle of these projects, including sales, construction, and service phases. By leveraging Vestas' products, the team assesses plant performance against respective grid code requirements, ensuring optimal functionality and compliance. In addition to their core responsibilities, the team collaborates closely with other global and regional cross-functional teams within Vestas. These teams include Electrical Simulation Models, Converter Control, EBoP, SCADA, Technical Grid Management, and Grid Execution. Through this collaboration, the Power Systems Performance Analysis team delivers competitive and compliant solutions, driving innovation and excellence in renewable energy.
