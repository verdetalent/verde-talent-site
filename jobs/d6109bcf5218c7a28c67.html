Full description
The role is part of the newly formed team under the Global Finance of Vestas Global Shared Services Centre and is part of the expansion initiatives to deliver and cater to our internal Vestas businesses around the world. The team will be primarily responsible in providing standardized, rule-based services to the regions particularly VAT Controlling and Postings. The team will be directly working with the Group/Regional Tax Team specifically the VAT Team including actions to address issues on VAT controlling and establish ways to improve the process. The team will also proactively work with a wide range of stakeholders such as Regional Finance, Treasury, and other SSC functional areas to ensure issues and concerns are addressed positively and by the required deadlines. Responsibilities: Responsible for Tax Compliance, VAT Reporting, and Process Control ensuring accuracy, compliance, and timely delivery of operations.
