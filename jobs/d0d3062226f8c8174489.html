AES CE is an established and rapidly growing renewable energy company headquartered in Salt Lake City, UT.

The AES Operations team is looking for a Solar Technician II. This position will cover assigned O&M activities associated with the solar PV sites and substations associated with the utility-scale Solar Energy Plants located in Indiana. This individual would play a pivotal role in ensuring that AES CE operates the portfolio to standards that exceed industry best-practice. The ideal candidate will be a self-starter, and have the ability to build, train and lead other field personnel. As AES CE grows to become one of the leading IPPs in the industry, this role will provide an unparalleled opportunity for professional growth.

This role will work on our Hardy Hillsproject (Frankfort IN 46041). The candidate must be able to commute daily to the site.

Traveling up to 25% will be required, mainly to support operations in our Hardy Hills project, in Indiana.

Responsibilities:

  • Perform maintenance and operations for solar PV sites and substations.
  • Handle corrective, preventive, and emergency maintenance.
  • Work with equipment like Power Electronic HEMK Gen3 inverters and Nextracker Tracking systems, high/medium/low voltage switching.
  • Prepare and implement switching and clearance orders.
  • Ensure compliance with environmental and safety standards.
  • Work independently or in teams.

Skills and Proficiencies:

  • Experience with electrical/construction projects and basic IT equipment.
  • Knowledge of workplace safety and ability to follow and improve safety standards.
  • Strong interpersonal and communication skills.
  • Ability to work in a diverse organization.
  • Problem-solving skills and proactive attitude.
  • Technical proficiency with standard business software.
  • Knowledge of safety and environmental regulations.

Education and Experience:

  • 2-year technical degree or equivalent Journeyman qualifications (preferred).
  • 3+ years as a PV O&M solar technician.
  • Knowledge of electrical or mechanical fundamentals.

Working Conditions:

  • Outdoor work in various weather conditions.
  • Requires standing, sitting, walking, kneeling, climbing, and using protective equipment.
  • May work around rotating and energized equipment, hazardous materials, and chemicals.
  • Occasional lifting and working overtime or non-standard hours.
