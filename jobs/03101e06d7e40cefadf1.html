Expected Length of Internship:

Education/Experience:

  • Must be pursuing an Engineering, Mathematics, Data Science, Computer Science, or a related field degree.
  • Must currently be enrolled in a Masters program, who is fulfilling a graduation requirement for their degree.
  • First Solar encourages all interns to register their internship with their university.

Required Skills/Competencies:

  • Ability to review coding manuals to learn new syntax independently.
  • Ability to review and understand the purpose of existing scripts.
  • Ability to create reports/plots, analyze trends, and provide insights.
  • Proficiency in Python or R for data analysis and machine learning.
  • Skilled in exploration data analysis and feature engineering.
  • Familiarity with SQL for data querying and manipulation.
  • Robust knowledge and experience with statistical methods and classification models.
  • Demonstrated analytical, multi-tasking, problem solving, organizational, and planning skills.
  • Clear communication skills and ability to work well in a team environment.
  • Experience with fundamental technologies in a Microsoft environment: Windows OS, Office 365, etc.

Basic Internship Scope (Project):

  • Help Develop pipelines that extract significant features from key data sets.
  • Build custom analytical tools.
  • Contribute to automated scripting and analysis for larger software packages.
  • Support the team to perform data extraction, cleaning, analysis and deliver actionable insights.
  • Contribute to feature selection and development of machine learning models to uncover value of data.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to understand their needs and perform Ad hoc analysis.
  • The candidate will work closely with Data Scientists on multiple data analysis use cases.
  • Upon the completion of this internship, the intern is required to present their accomplishments and project summary to the leadership team.
