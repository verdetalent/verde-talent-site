AVP Corporate Development Position Summary Leads and develops the corporate development team responsible for negotiating and executing mergers, acquisitions, and divestitures along with asset project development to enhance financial performance and corporate capabilities. Leads efforts to evaluate, recommend, and develop strategic growth opportunities in coordination with senior leadership. Responsible for developing and executing on asset-level strategies focused on renewable energy (solar, wind, batteries), dispatchable generation, carbon-free technologies (e.g. geothermal, long-duration storage, hydrogen), and other infrastructure investments to enhance long term growth and positioning in the market for the company. Develop and manage a methodology for assessment and review of the business portfolio for financial contribution and strategic fit. Implement and manage a process for the screening and evaluation of business development opportunities and competitive threats. Essential Responsibilities Lead and manage the evaluation, and execution of corporate expansion opportunities including mergers, acquisitions, divestitures, or joint ventures. Assess opportunities for financial contribution and strategic fit. Coordinate and conduct financial valuations, due diligence reviews, and internal risk assessment of potential sale or acquisition. Lead negotiations of purchase/sales agreements, including all significant terms and conditions. Prepare materials supporting the transaction for senior management and board review and approval.
