Professional | Full-Time | Project Portfolio Management Department Welcome to the Customer Project Execution (CPX) team in Asia (Onshore), a dynamic and dedicated group committed to delivering wind energy projects to Customers across the region. Our core responsibility lies in the execution of Wind Turbine Supply and Installation projects, starting from the moment a contract is signed with the Customer, through to the successful handover of the completed project. We ensure that every project is delivered within contractual scope, on budget, on schedule, and in alignment with Vestas's highest standards of quality and safety. Success in CPX is driven by strong collaboration with customers, suppliers, and internal stakeholders. We focus on building trust, maintaining transparency, and fostering teamwork to ensure that each project is executed efficiently and sustainably. Join us in our mission to drive excellence and innovation in the renewable energy sector, and help shape a cleaner, greener future for Asia and beyond. Responsibilities As a member of the Project Execution team, responsible for driving project towards success. Cost control & management, which includes management of project budgets, cashflow & revenue. Preparation of payment claims in line with the contractual requirements in a timely manner, and regular updation of system to reflect accurate data. Vendor Invoice booking, GR, and payment processing within due date. Complete ownership of Project set up in SAP. Maintain Project WBS & Networks. Billing plan creation, milestone release for invoice & cash-in revision in SAP. Raise Purchase Requisitions and process Purchase Orders for supply & service, follow up till deliveries completed at site, handling spares, consumables, tools & stocks up to project close. Manage component creation and installation for as-built in SAP, up to Project closure. Support Project Reporting requirement for Internal Management & Customer.
