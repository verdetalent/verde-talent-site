We are looking for a Production Schedulerto join our team in one of today’s most exciting technologies.  This role will report toPlanning Managerand based in Newark, DE.  This is a fully on-site, in office role working the Weekend shift (Friday-Sunday, 6am-6pm).

Role and Responsibilities:

  • Establish the production schedule in the Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system by firming, releasing and tracking the flow of work orders
  • Serve as a single point of contact to the Manufacturing organization on material availability status for assigned production work centers
  • Work closely with procurement, planning, logistics and other supply chain functions for material status and to align manufacturing plans and material flow
  • Drive transaction discipline to ensure manufacturing reports each milestone operation in the ERP system and track timely close of work orders
  • Establish and maintain ERP item planning parameters including manufacturing lead times, planning time fences, order modifiers and continuously improves data integrity
  • Ensure accurate and timely set up for manufacturing Bill of Materials (BOMs) for all items
  • Monitor the integrity between engineering and manufacturing BOMs
  • Review and approve engineering change orders, planning BOMs and deviation requests
  • Revise the schedule according to work order specifications, established priorities and availability of parts & material
  • Track the accuracy of manufacturing routings and work closely with manufacturing engineers on the optimization of all routing steps and standard operations
  • Monitor work order aging in the ERP system and continuously drive for reductions in Work in Progress (WIP) inventory
  • Collaborate with all functions to ensure buy in and adherence to the production schedule
  • Contributor to the implementation of all materials affected on Engineering Change Notices
  • Monitor accuracy of plan assumptions on a daily basis, communicate required adjustments to the rest of the materials team
  • Analyze monthly Inventory Aging reports based on agreed parameters and provide forward visibility on potential issues
  • Develop and publish inventory reports to management outlining current inventory levels/trends, potential excess/obsolete and drive inventory management initiatives to manage inventory to the agreed levels

Skills and Experience:

  • BS/BA Degree in Supply Chain, Logistics, Business or Engineering
  • 3-5 years of experience in Inventory Management, Production Planning and/or Scheduling
  • Experience using Oracle ERP or similar ERP systems
  • Excellent oral and written communications skills
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Ability to work cross-functionally to develop best solutions for the company
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced manufacturing environment
  • Self-starter with ability to complete activities with minimal direction
  • Proficiency with MS Office Suite
  • Highly analytical skill set with Excel experience and attention to details
  • American Production and Inventory Control Society (APICS) Certification preferred

Physical Requirements:

  • Ability to stand/walk for up to 10-12 hours per shift
  • Occasionally lift/carry up to 25–50 lbs
  • Use hands and fingers to operate machinery controls and computers throughout the shift
  • Visual acuity sufficient for product inspection and reading digital/printed materials
  • Hearing ability to detect alarms and communicate effectively with staff
  • Ability to perform essential tasks with or without reasonable accommodation
  • Must wear Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) in required areas (safety glasses, safety-toed boots)
