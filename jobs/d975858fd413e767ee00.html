Full description

Region NCE and Global Offshore > Global Offshore > Offshore CPX Commissioning Supervision APAC

The Commissioning Supervisor can be an on or offshore position. The supervisor will work together with commissioning resources; this team is commonly known as "the commissioning team" and is tasked to complete all of the commissioning. The commissioning tasks will commence after a hand over of the WTG location from our Installation team.

The Commissioning supervisor will work conjointly with other members of the construction team, in particular the Installation team, QA/QC team as well as with sub-contractors and client construction teams, overseeing that all commissioning tasks are carried out in a safe and timely manner.

The supervisor shall take necessary action to comply with Vestas safety and quality standards.

Responsibilities

  • Ensure a safe work practice by acting in compliance with Vestas HSE requirements
  • Ensure own all PPE checks are done, and monthly check sheets are logged
  • Responsible for carrying our NPU and Functional test on Pre-assembly site.
  • Daily communication with Deputy Commissioning Manager
  • Execute WTG completion and start up
  • Troubleshoot, operate and maintain WTGs
  • Identify, register and manage deviations
  • Maintain turbines and equipment
  • Knowledge sharing
  • Provide feedback and evaluation
  • Help in onboarding new starters

Qualifications

  • A technical diploma/degree - e.g. electrical or electro-mechanical
  • Taiwan Level B technician for interior wiring work (乙級室內配線).
  • Experience from the wind industry is beneficial - e.g. with commissioning and troubleshooting
  • Great skills in English language - written and spoken.
  • Experience working in a multicultural environment

Competencies

Personal skills required

(behavior, personality, working style, etc.)

  • Health and Safety awareness
  • A team person
  • Skilled motivator and source of team engagement
  • Solid Continuous Improvement mindset
  • Composed and focused when facing challenges
  • Effective problem solver
  • Customer focused
  • Motivated and highly motivated
  • Resourceful, and thrive in an international environment.
  • Willingness to travel and be mobilised globally
