Full description

Operation Support Area is the area in charge of controlling and monitoring the Services, it must provide support in the initial definition of spare parts, tools and resources for the execution by the Operational area. You must ensure that the entire System (SAP and Salesforce) are aligned with the Operation, establish KPI's and operational controls.

Responsibilities:

Balance all assigned territory orders to ensure minimum downtime and maximum productivity

Service crew composition is up to date and adjusted to demand

Tools, parts & services are planned and monitored on time before crew mobilization

Crew schedules are managed on a day-to-day basis

Service schedule is aligned with the obligations in service contracts

On time communication to crews an partners is guaranteed

KPIs and PPIs related to Planning & Scheduling are balanced and under control

Qualifications:

Experience in the wind industry

Industrial Engineering, logistics, or a related career.

SAP intermediate-advanced level. (PM, MM, SD)

Advanced level Microsoft tools. (Excel - Macro, Power BI)

Intermediate-Advanced English

Experience in logistics processes, without being exclusive

About Vestas

Vestas is the energy industry’s global partner on sustainable energy solutions. We are specialised in designing, manufacturing, installing, and servicing wind turbines, both onshore and offshore.

Across the globe, we have installed more wind power than anyone else. We consider ourselves pioneers within the industry, as we continuously aim to design new solutions and technologies to create a more sustainable future for all of us. With more than 185 GW of wind power installed worldwide and 40+ years of experience in wind energy, we have an unmatched track record demonstrating our expertise within the field.

With 30,000 employees globally, we are a diverse team united by a common goal: to power the solution – today, tomorrow, and far into the future.
