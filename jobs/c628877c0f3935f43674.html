Full description
Vestas is the largest renewable energy solutions provider in the World. Vestas NCE is responsible for sales, construction, and servicing of wind projects in Benelux and other European Markets. Service Sales works with multiple functions in an international environment to deliver on Vestas "profitable growth" strategy. Region NCE > Global Offshore > HKW VII - Oranjewind Purpose and reason for the existence of the job Do you have experience servicing and maintaining mechanical or electrical systems? Do you have experience in power generation and delivering renewable energy? Would you like an opportunity to apply your skills and qualifications in a prominent company in the industry? Reporting to the Service Manager, you will join our Service team for the Hollandse Kust West VII windfarm Serviced from Den Helder. Based offshore on one of our service operation vessels (SOV), the Shift Manager is responsible for managing the day-to-day operation of the windfarm from the vessel on a rotational basis. Encompassing HSE, production performance, operations, maintenance, and team, development. Responsibilities: In line with Vestas' quality, HSE and Operational procedures, you will work a 2 week on / 2 week off rotation, based at our Hollandse Kust West VII- Oranjewind offshore windfarm site.
