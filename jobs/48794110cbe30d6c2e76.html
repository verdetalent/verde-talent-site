Full description

We are looking for a position asStaff Test Engineer to join our team in one of today’s most exciting technologies.

Bloom Energy is the leading force in transforming the way energy is generated and delivered.  We are driving toward the parallel goals of growing a dynamic and forward-thinking company while creating a positive impact on our environment. Our company is filled with smart and innovative thinkers and doers, and we never stop striving to improve our technology, to expand and improve our company performance, and to develop and support the many talented employees that serve our mission.

Location: This position will be based in Juinagar, Navi Mumbai.

Role and Responsibilities:

  • Responsible for conducting functional tests on blowers and compressors for pressure mapping, efficiency curves, transient response characteristics.
  • Plan and execute reliability tests, including life cycle, endurance, HALT/HASS testing, thermal cycling, vibration, fatigue and environmental stress tests on compressors/blowers used in fuel‑cell systems.
  • Design and develop test rigs for flow, pressure, and temperature measurements, including the selection of instrumentation and DAQ systems.
  • Prepare and update P&IDs, Instrumentation Loop Diagrams, and Process Hook-up Diagrams.
  • Conduct PSHR reviews and perform safety checks for electrical and mechanical test setups.
  • Perform functional, performance, and qualification testing of sensors, transducers, valves, weld joints, and mechanical components.
  • Perform DFMEA, identify failure modes, conduct root-cause analysis, and recommend corrective actions.
  • Manage the setup, operation, calibration, and preventive maintenance of laboratory and test equipment.
  • Apply UL, KOSHA, EU, and American standards for safe component selection, testing, and compliance.
  • Execute qualification procedures, analyze test data, and prepare concise reports for stakeholders.
  • Update component and product specifications and support value engineering initiatives.
  • Support vendor qualification activities and identify alternative component suppliers.
  • Maintain clear communication across teams and ensure adherence to 5S practices in the laboratory.

Skills and Experience:

  • Degree in Mechanical / Mechatronics Engineering.
  • 6–8 years’ experience in Turbo-machinery and High‑speed blower/compressor testing.
  • Hands‑on experience in motor‑drive systems, including control, testing, and performance validation.
  • Strong understanding of airflow dynamics, pressure systems, and thermodynamics.
  • Strong communication and problem‑solving skills.
  • Exceptional analytical skills and attention to detail.
  • Ability to work effectively in a matrix organization with a multicultural team.
