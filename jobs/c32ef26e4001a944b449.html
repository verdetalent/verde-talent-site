Position Specific Description The Senior Power Generation Division (PGD) Engineer role focuses on generator and rotating electrical machine reliability across the wind fleet, responsible for leading root cause analysis, reliability strategy, and data-driven performance improvements to reduce failures and O&M cost. Scope & Impact Own reliability performance for wind turbine rotating electrical machines (motors/generators) across the fleet Lead complex failure investigations and systemic issue resolution using structured RCA methodologies Drive fleet-wide reliability improvements through engineering solutions, repair strategies, and data insights Define and standardize repair specifications, vendor quality requirements, and maintenance practices Influence business decisions and cost outcomes through technical and financial analysis The Ideal Candidate Would Have the Following: 4+ years of experience with generators, motors, or rotating electrical machines across design, manufacturing, repair, or field environments. Strong expertise in electrical machine theory, electromagnetic behavior, and machine dynamics. Experience with troubleshooting electrical machines. Proven capability in root cause analysis (RCA) and failure mode identification. Experience developing repair procedures, specifications, and engineering standards. Strong data-driven mindset, including analysis of failure data and fleet trends. Ability to translate technical findings into actionable business and financial recommendations.
