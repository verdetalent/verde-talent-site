Senior Staff Engineer-NPI

Bloom Energy
Location not listed Full-time Individual Contributor
Hydrogen

Full description

​Roles and Responsibilities:

  • Lead NPI process for new product development (NPD)
  • Technology Transfer of new products from design to mass manufacturing
  • Drive manufacturing, material and test readiness at "Contract Manufacturers"
  • Conduct DFx reviews and process capability to ensure product design and manufacturing processes are capable of meeting the required Bloom quality standards
  • Structure and maintain new product BOMs
  • Develop assembly work instructions and procedures
  • Set clear goals and expectations to ensure all stakeholders are aligned
  • Implementation of ECNs and Deviations in manufacturing, contract manufacturers and vendors for various commodities
  • Work cross-functionally with Engineering, Manufacturing, QA & SCM teams on issue resolution

Education & Desired Experience:

  • Bachelor of Engineering in EEE/ECE
  • 10+ years experience in NPI Program Management
  • Hands on experience in PCBA and Box builds is mandatory
  • Knowledge on various commodities like PCB, Sheet metal, Cable Harness is must
  • Knowledge of power electronics such as DC-DC converter and Inverters, is a plus
  • Knowledge of manufacturing-specific roles throughout NPI phases including HW, SW, manufacturing process, Testing and Reliability
  • Knowledge of PLM concepts including BOMs, ECN, Deviations
  • Good communication skills and ability to work across teams
  • Ability to relate well to a cross functional team from manufacturing, supply chain, NPI & Engineering
  • Ability to work effectively across time zones and support contract manufacturers in different geographies
  • Travel: 20%
