The Planning Coordinator supports the day-to-day and forward-looking operational planning activities of the Renewable Energy Control Centre, with a focus on photovoltaic (PV) and battery energy storage system (BESS) assets. This role is responsible for coordinating planned outages, maintenance activities, switching support, resource scheduling, operational readiness, reporting workflows, and cross-functional communication between the Control Centre, field operations, asset management, engineering, compliance, and third-party stakeholders.

The Planning Coordinator plays a key role in ensuring that planned work is organized, risks are identified in advance, required approvals are obtained, and all parties are aligned before activities are executed. The position helps improve control centre efficiency, reliability, compliance readiness, and operational consistency across a growing fleet of renewable energy assets.

Main Responsibilities

Accountabilities: **Operational Planning & Coordination

Outcome:**

  • Coordinate planned maintenance, outages, derates, testing, inspections, and operational activities for PV and BESS facilities.
  • Develop and maintain daily, weekly, and longer-range operational plans for control centre activities.
  • Review planned work requests and ensure proper documentation, scope definition, timing, and operational impact assessment.
  • Work closely with field technicians, regional managers, engineering, asset management, and third-party service providers to align execution plans.
  • Confirm operational readiness before planned activities begin, including communication plans, approvals, staffing, and system availability.
  • Track all scheduled work and ensure accurate status updates are provided to relevant stakeholders.

Accountabilities: Outage, Derate, and Work Scheduling Support

Outcome:

  • Support planning of outages and derates to minimize operational and commercial impacts.
  • Coordinate with internal and external stakeholders regarding timing of work, outage windows, restrictions, and return-to-service expectations.
  • Maintain planning records for planned events, work orders, and operational constraints.
  • Assist in prioritizing work based on reliability, safety, compliance, and production impacts.
  • Help ensure event creation, work order workflows, and operational tracking systems are updated properly.

Accountabilities: Control Centre Support

Outcome:

  • Act as a coordination link between real-time operators and non-real-time support teams.
  • Provide operators with advance visibility into planned work and expected system impacts.
  • Support turnover quality by ensuring upcoming work, risks, and priorities are clearly documented for operating shifts.
  • Help develop structured daily and weekly planning meetings to improve coordination across the control centre environment.
  • Assist with escalation tracking and follow-up on open operational issues.

Accountabilities: **Reporting & Documentation

Outcome:**

  • Maintain planning logs, schedules, trackers, and records related to maintenance, outages, derates, testing, and operational changes.
  • Prepare summaries, dashboards, and reports on planned work, schedule adherence, backlog, and execution performance.
  • Ensure planning documentation is complete, organized, and audit-ready where required.
  • Support the development and continuous improvement of procedures, checklists, planning templates, and coordination processes.
  • Assist with lessons learned reviews following major operational events or planned work execution issues.

Accountabilities: Compliance & Risk Awareness

Outcome:

  • Support planning activities in a manner consistent with applicable reliability, safety, and regulatory requirements.
  • Help ensure planned work is reviewed for compliance, operational risk, cybersecurity, and site access considerations.
  • Coordinate with compliance and operations leadership to ensure required documentation and evidence are retained where needed.
  • Escalate scheduling conflicts, planning risks, readiness gaps, and high-impact operational concerns in a timely manner.

  • Ability to work calmly under pressure and adapt to changing priorities.
  • Strong problem-solving skills and proactive mindset.
  • Ability to identify conflicts, gaps, and risks before they affect operations.
