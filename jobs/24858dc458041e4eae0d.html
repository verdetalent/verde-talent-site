About Redwood Materials Redwood is localizing a global battery supply chain that seamlessly integrates recovery, reuse, and recycling — keeping critical minerals in circulation and driving the energy transition. Founded in 2017, we’re delivering low-cost and large-scale energy storage and producing battery materials in the U.S. for the first time, all from batteries we already have. Digital Transformation Lead, EPC Our Engineering, Procurement & Construction (EPC) team delivers the infrastructure powering Redwood’s growth. We’re looking for a Digital Transformation Lead to define and execute our EPC digital strategy, streamline workflows, and build in-house tools that enable seamless collaboration across design, engineering, and construction. You’ll oversee core EPC systems while establishing scalable, data-driven processes that serve as a single source of truth. This role combines hands-on system leadership with long-term vision-setting and includes the opportunity to grow a dedicated digital innovation team. Responsibilities will include: Own and execute the EPC digital roadmap Integrate external software solutions and develop internal solutions or add-ons Streamline data flow and collaboration across enterprise systems Increase team productivity and automated workflows by developing in-house tools using AI-augmented coding Partner with EPC, Operations, and Engineering to solve workflow challenges Ensure reliability, security, and scalability of digital platforms Build governance, training, and adoption programs Lead vendor management and system integrations Serve as EPC’s digital subject matter expert and advisor Desired Qualifications 10+ years of experience in digital transformation, systems integration, or enterprise software operations in EPC, engineering consulting, construction, or energy Proven leadership of technical and cross-functional teams Strong knowledge of EPC workflows and project lifecycles (utility-scale solar/battery stationary storage a plus)...
