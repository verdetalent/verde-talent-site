Are you looking for an exciting job where you can put your skills and talents to work at a company you can feel proud to be a part of? Position Summary The Sr. Data Applications Engineer will be a senior hands-on engineer on the Xcel Vision Suite (XVS) Data & Applications Engineering team, reporting to the Manager of Data & Applications Engineering. This role focuses on building internal data-driven applications, workflows, and analytics products that sit on top of XVS data pipelines and modeling outputs. This position works closely with Product, Data Science, Enterprise IT, and external consultants to turn planning workflows, model outputs, and complex datasets into usable tools for planners and engineers. The role emphasizes building lightweight applications, dashboards, and workflows (e.g., data input, comparison tools, and decision-support interfaces) while ensuring they are maintainable, well-documented, and aligned with enterprise standards. The Sr. Data Applications Engineer will help establish and follow engineering practices for application design, documentation, deployment, and knowledge transfer, and contribute to how the team delivers and maintains XVS tools over time. The role requires strong technical judgment, comfort working in ambiguity, and the ability to translate loosely defined needs into practical, user-facing solutions. It also includes applying modern engineering practices, including AI-assisted development, to improve delivery speed and quality. Essential Responsibilities Solution Delivery: Lead and support solution lifecycle technical activities. Ensure solutions are designed for great user experience and operational performance.
