Specialist | Full-Time | Engineering Vestas Technology & Operations (VTO) > Product Line > Regional Technology >Technical Sales & Project Solutions, Asia The Technical Sales & Project Solutions department is the arm of our Regional Technology team in Asia Pacific and China (APAC), providing the link between our sales business unit and our customer needs towards the central power solutions team, responsible for global product development in Vestas. The Technical Bid Specialist role within Technical Sales & Project Solutions is a highly diversified role that requires strong technical skills as well as a solid commercial mindset. You will work with our commercial Sales group, our customers as well as central engineering functions, to deliver the best possible technical solutions for our customers, targeting exponential growth in the onshore and offshore wind segment. Responsibilities Your main responsibilities as a Technical Bid Specialist will be to ensure timely and correct responses to customer enquiries, prepare clarifications and deviations to tenders, and direct technical negotiations together with the Sales team. You will: Take full responsibility for the technical solution during the sales phase. Effectively supervise the technical value engineering of customer projects and direct technical negotiations as an active member of a Sales project team. Ensure timely responses to customer enquiries, prepare clarifications and deviations to tenders, as well as technical annexes and contract schedules. Network to build relationships with the key technical stakeholders in your customers' organisations, promoting their requirements inside Vestas and ensuring a winning technical value proposition. Be the link between the Onshore Sales organisation and VPS (Vestas Power Solutions) on your respective project(s) and topics.
