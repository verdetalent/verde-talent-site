Do you want to play an active role in shaping the energy future? At Vestas, you can! As the global leader in wind energy, we drive innovation to create a more sustainable world. With us, you will take on responsibility, grow both professionally and personally, and work in an environment that encourages collaboration, courage, and fresh ideas. Would you like to contribute your expertise in an international environment? At Vestas, you’ll find exciting projects, modern ways of working, and real opportunities to make an impact. Take the next step and start your journey as an Offshore Stock Keeper (m/f/d) for Deutsche Bucht. About the Department: The Vestas Offshore Supply Chain Department is responsible for the logistic and warehouse operations on board of the Service Operations Vessels and Jack-Up Vessels. We are supporting the Vestas Offshore Special Projects and Service Departments which installs, services and maintains the wind turbines in multiple windfarms across the European seas. The department has an open and informal culture, where teamwork and good communication is the key to our success. You will have the opportunity to work in an exciting industry and in a team of highly motivated employees. Your benefits – because your contribution counts! Security & Perspective: Permanent employment contract with long-term prospects, plus a 14/14 rotation model for predictable assignments and genuine rest periods Development & Expertise: Comprehensive onboarding and regular training, including technical courses at the Vestas Academy and qualifications as an electrical specialist (DIN VDE 0105-100) Working & Living Offshore: High-quality equipment, modern accommodation on an SOV with gym, cinema, and gaming zone, as well as full catering during your offshore shifts Technology & Responsibility: Independent work with cutting-edge wind energy technology, innovative solutions, and space for your own ideas Work-Life-Balance: 20 days of holiday during offshore deployment...
