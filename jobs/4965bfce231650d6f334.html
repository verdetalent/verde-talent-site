About the Department:

The Vestas Offshore Supply Chain Department is responsible for the logistic and warehouse operations on board of the Service Operations Vessels and Jack-Up Vessels. We are supporting the Vestas Offshore Special Projects and Service Departments which installs, services and maintains the wind turbines in multiple windfarms across the European seas.

The department has an open and informal culture, where teamwork and good communication is the key to our success. You will have the opportunity to work in an exciting industry and in a team of highly motivated employees.

Your responsibilities – here's where you can get involved!

  • You are responsible for picking, issuing and receiving materials and spare parts for and from teams of technicians.
  • You are in ongoing communication with the Service organization and technician teams to ensure the timely supply of necessary tools, spare parts and equipment.
  • You are responsible for maintaining stock levels and inventory records in the SAP ERP system.
  • You are ensuring accurate material postings and conducting regular inventories.
  • You are ensuring order and cleanliness in the warehouse and work areas, while maintaining compliance with all health and safety requirements.
  • You are coordinating the transport of goods and customs clearance during regular port calls.
  • You are managing PPE, hoists, testing tools and other equipment requiring periodic inspections, testing or calibration.
  • You are acting as the appointed person for calibration activities on site.
  • You are requesting quotations for local purchases and initiating the creation of purchase orders.
  • You are ensuring proper waste handling, disposal and offloading procedures on the vessel and in port.
  • You are coordinating ad-hoc spare parts deliveries to the wind farm.
  • You are supporting the Service Site Management team with a variety of organizational and administrative tasks.

Your profile – what makes you a great fit!

  • You have completed an apprenticeship and/or hold a degree in warehouse management, logistics, or a related field.
  • You have experience working in a warehouse environment; experience within the wind industry is considered an advantage.
  • You hold a valid driving license.
  • You have experience with customs clearance and customs declarations, which is beneficial for this role.
  • You are proficient in using SharePoint, Word, Excel, Outlook, TMS, and ERP systems such as SAP or Microsoft Dynamics AX.
  • You have strong communication skills and are confident communicating in English.
  • You have solid office and site management skills.
  • You have strong time management and planning skills and can effectively prioritize multiple tasks.
  • You are committed to adhering to and complying with all applicable local laws and regulations.

About Vestas
