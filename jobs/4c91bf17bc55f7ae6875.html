Full description

At Bloom Energy, our vision for a world powered by clean, reliable, and affordable energy is more than just a dream—we’re making it reality.

For over two decades, we’ve been at the forefront of the global energy transition, pioneering solutions that empower critical industries to thrive in a rapidly digitizing, energy-intensive world. From revolutionizing power for AI-driven data centers to ensuring resilience for hospitals, electric grids, manufacturing facilities, and utilities, our solid oxide fuel cell (SOFC) and solid oxide electrolyzer (SOEC) technologies are redefining what’s possible by delivering energy abundance for all. With more than 30,000 fuel cell modules deployed worldwide, we are the trusted partner for Fortune 100 companies and innovators alike. Our cutting-edge solutions enable unparalleled “time-to-power” capabilities, reliability, and sustainability, ensuring our customers remain ahead in a world where soaring energy demand and intensifying energy scarcity are rapidly becoming the new norm.

At Bloom, we thrive on collaboration, bold thinking, and relentless innovation. We believe that, together, we can create a brighter, more sustainable future while tackling the most pressing challenges of the 21st century.

We are looking for a Sr. Staff Mechanical Design Engineer, Equipmentto join our team in one of today’s most exciting technologies. This role will report to Sr. Director, Reliability Engineering.

Location: This position will be fully Onsite based in Fremont, CA

Roles and Responsibilities:

  • Design and develop R&D equipment, tools/test fixtures to support feasibility and investigation efforts.  These tools/test fixtures are designed, developed and built in-house.
  • Work with Operations and Process engineering teams to design equipment/tools to support production ramp and needs for inspection equipment.
  • Support existing equipment install base in BE Production and in BE Suppliers.  This includes troubleshooting issues, debugging, and new feature requests for both hardware and software.

Education and Experience:

  • Process Automation and Test Equipment Design experience is a requirement
  • Mechanical design expert using Solidworks or other 3d modeling applications is a requirement
  • Design Documentation:  Mechanical Detail Drawings, Electrical Wiring, P&ID, Plumbing Diagrams
  • Experience in the design and fabrication of hardware utilizing sheet metal, machined parts, casting, welding and brazing technology (eg. Heat exchangers) is a plus
  • Experience with Screen Printing, Adhesive Dispensing, Curing and working with high temperature ceramics is a plus
  • Experience releasing complex products from design to medium/high volume manufacturing with a understanding of DFM, DFX principles and applications
  • Superior communication skills, (written, verbal and active listening)
  • Highly motivated self-starter, capable of self management with little oversight
  • Ability to manage and lead projects
  • Experience managing external custom machine integrators
  • BS or MS in Mechanical Engineering, or related discipline
  • 5+ yrs working in a NPI/Manufacturing Engineering, or Design Development organization
