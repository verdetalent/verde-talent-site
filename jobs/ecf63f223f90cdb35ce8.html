ABOUT THE POSITION

We are seeking a Lead Design Quality Engineer to drive engineering quality excellence across BESS hardware programs. This role will be responsible for defining, implementing, and governing design quality processes and metrics to ensure first-time-right engineering execution. The position will lead initiatives to establish and monitor key quality indicators such as DFMEA coverage, design escape rate, engineering review compliance (ERC), and overall design maturity. The Lead Design Quality Engineer will work closely with cross-functional design teams to ensure all design reviews, validations, and quality gates are effectively executed, with structured data capture to enable continuous improvement and reduction of design-related issues. This role requires a strong combination of engineering expertise, quality mindset, process rigor, and data-driven decision-making, with the ability to influence teams and embed quality into the product development lifecycle.

Key Responsibilities

Design Quality Strategy & Governance

  • Define and implement design quality framework across BESS hardware programs
  • Establish and enforce First-Time-Right (FTR) principles in engineering execution
  • Own and govern design quality standards, checklists, and release criteria
  • Integrate quality gates into Stage-Gate / NPI processes

Engineering Quality Metrics & Insights

Define, track, and drive key metrics:

  • DFMEA coverage & quality
  • Design Escape Rate (post-release defects)
  • Engineering Review Compliance (ERC)
  • First-Time-Right (FTR %)
  • Key Performance Metrics

Build dashboards for:

  • Product / subsystem quality trends
  • Program-wise performance

Drive data-based decision making and accountability

Design Review & Compliance (ERC)

  • Establish structured design review framework (Concept, Detailed Design, Release)
  • Ensure:
  • All required reviews are conducted
  • Review outcomes are documented and tracked
  • Drive Engineering Review Compliance (ERC) across teams
