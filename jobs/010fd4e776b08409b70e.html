Position Specific Description Business Management is responsible for the overall performance of a portfolio of operating assets including the development and execution of plans to optimize financial performance of the assets. As the Business Manager, you will be responsible for the performance of a portfolio of operating assets, with full P&L accountability for the South region portfolio (primarily SPP). You will manage customer relationships and ensure compliance with power purchase agreements, while overseeing financing and regulatory requirements and developing commercial strategies, including repowering and recontracting initiatives. You will collaborate with various cross-functional teams, including NextEra Energy Power Marketing, Power Generation Division, Accounting, NEER Development, Project Finance Group, and other Business Management teams. Additionally, this role involves leading special projects to optimize the performance of existing renewable assets and support new development opportunities. Key Responsibilities: Develop major workstreams across the region, including asset repowering plans. Coordinate and manage projects throughout their lifecycle, from design to execution. Prepare analysis, reporting, and scorecards to track value creation and performance against plan. Maintain close working relationships with partners, customers, and stakeholders. Negotiate and resolve disputes related to PPAs and other stakeholder contracts. Develop and refine market and regulatory strategies to improve asset performance.
