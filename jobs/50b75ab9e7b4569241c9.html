Garage Mechanic - Hadley, MA

Eversource Energy
Hadley, MA Full-time Individual Contributor $53.01
Applying takes you to Eversource Energy's own careers site to complete your application.
Full description

Eversource will not offer immigration-related sponsorship for this position (e.g., H-1B, O-1, J-1, TN, E-3, etc.).  Applicants requiring visa sponsorship to start employment with Eversource will not be considered.

This is a Fully Qualified Garage Mechanic Position

POSITION REQUIREMENTS:

Individuals must have four and one-half years’ experience as fully qualified garage mechanics.

Education and training equivalent to graduation from high school or vocational school, supplemented

by a one-year trade school course are required. Preference will be given to individuals who are

qualified to hold a Commercial Driver's License (CDL). This is a shift position with the majority

of the work to be performed on the night (2nd) shift. Monday - Friday, 3:00 p.m. - 11:00 p.m.

The individual will be required to supply their own hand tools.

The individual will be required to work emergency and plan over time to support the 24/7 nature of

the utility business.

POSITION DESCRIPTION:

Under general supervision, individual performs various kinds of skilled and unskilled workers in

connection with the inspection, maintenance, and repair of automotive and mobile or stationary,

heavy-duty equipment, including any associated components and equipment. Must have a working

knowledge of the operation and construction of internal combustion engines and hydraulic

assemblies. Must have the ability to diagnose operating deficiencies of automotive and hydraulic

equipment and the mechanical ability to install new and maintain equipment for all types of

work-related to the Transportation Department. Will perform duties indoors and outdoors, at times

under adverse weather conditions and for extended periods.

Bargaining unit employees at WMECO with contractual rights to this position will be given first

consideration. This position is also being posted internally and externally.

Compensation and Benefits:

The hourly rate for this position starts at $53.01 and progresses in accordance with the union

contract. Check out the career site for an overview of benefits.

Emergency Response:

Responding to emergency situations to meet customers’ needs is part of every employee’s role. If

employed, you will be given an Emergency Restoration assignment. This means you may be called to

assist during an emergency outside of your normal responsibilities, work hours and location.
