Full description
Eversource will not offer immigration-related sponsorship for this position (e.g., H-1B, O-1, J-1, TN, E-3, etc.). Applicants requiring visa sponsorship to start employment with Eversource will not be considered. Supports the identification, development and implementation of business opportunities for advancing the Clean Technologies group and the Gas Business Unit in all areas of gas decarbonization and clean technologies. The role will involve researching and vetting various decarbonization and clean gas technologies opportunities. Will assist in assessing and building the business case for opportunities to pursue in either pilot or project form based upon agreed upon metrics with the Manager of Clean Technologies and other key internal stakeholders. Such metrics may include market conditions, customer desire/appetite, technology maturity and availability, cost, core business synergies, environmental value, and overall corporate risk profile. Will coordinate with both electric and gas business units, as well as other Eversource organizations, to assist with comprehensive planning for opportunities. The role will also involve providing support on the future of natural gas to internal groups for shareholder and investor messaging, regulatory filing support, and external stakeholder discussions. Will be expected to work collaboratively with the Clean Technologies organization to advance the gas business unit and company goals on the future of gas. Essential Functions: Maintains familiarity with current and upcoming decarbonization technologies through market research. Supports on developing business plans, including financial analysis and resource requirements to support proposed program initiatives. Presents plans to senior management for approval. Supports the implementation of initiatives related to clean technologies projects and business development.
