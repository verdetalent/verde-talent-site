Vestas Technology & Operations > Technical Sales Management North & Central Europe (NCE) Onshore The Technical Sales Management NCE Onshore department serves as the technical backbone of the Sales organization, effectively bridging commercial goals with engineering feasibility and organizational readiness. Within this structure, the SCADA Project Management team plays a pivotal role across the full lifecycle of wind power plant projects—from early-stage sales support through construction, commissioning, and into service operations. The team works with a diverse customer base, primarily consisting of large utilities and professional investors managing complex, high-value energy assets, where SCADA performance, reliability, and regulatory compliance are essential. This role operates at the intersection of technology, commercial decision-making, and project delivery. You will collaborate closely with customers, grid operators, internal execution teams, and R&D to ensure that SCADA solutions are not only technically robust but also commercially practical, compliant, and scalable across various regional and regulatory environments.
