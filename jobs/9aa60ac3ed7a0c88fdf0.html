Vestas Eólica, S.A.U. > Service > Global Service Operations > Remote Operations Center EMEA As a Remote Operations Engineer at the Madrid ROC, you will be part of the strategic core ensuring the global availability of our wind turbine fleet. This high-level technical position focuses on real-time monitoring, advanced diagnostics, and remote incident resolution, directly affecting performance optimization and the reduction of the cost of energy. What You Will Do As part of our 24/7 Monitoring Team, your role is built upon three strategic pillars: The Technical "Problem Solver": You will use advanced SCADA and SAP tools to diagnose why a multi-million euro machine has stopped. Your role is to determine with precision whether you can fix the issue with a single click from Madrid or if a technician must be dispatched to the field The Effective Communicator: You will manage communications with customers and field technicians during urgent incidents. You demonstrate the ability to translate complex technical problems into simple, professional solutions via both phone and email Operational Resilience: In a 24/7 environment, your responsibility is paramount. You will maintain high standards and attention to detail—especially during night shifts—remaining consistently meticulous with data and Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) What we are looking for At Vestas, demonstrated real-world experience and effective problem-solving count more than formal degrees. We are looking for technicians who know the field and want to take their careers to the next digital level: Experience: Minimum 1 year in the Wind Industry (specifically as a Field Technician or in Control Centers).
