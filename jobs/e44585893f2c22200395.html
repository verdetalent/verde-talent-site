Full description
Are you interested and experienced in electricity, mechanics, or hydraulics? Are you looking for a new exciting opportunity in a global company and would like to be a part of a green energy transition? As a Traveling Service Technician at Vestas, you can have an exciting job and develop your career in a constantly growing organization with the latest technology. Region NCE and Global Offshore > Global Offshore > Travelling Resources The Offshore Service Technician will work together with technicians in a team acting as Authorised Technicians. The Offshore Service Technician will implement varied work on WTGs under Service O&M, also Upgrade campaigns, Main Component Exchanges, and Blade campaigns throughout the Vestas Offshore WTG fleet. You will work conjointly with other members of Vestas sites in particular the Authorising Engineers, Service managers, Project managers, and Supervisors as well as with sub-contractors and Customer Service & construction teams, overseeing that all AT tasks are carried out effectively in a safe and timely manner. You will also have the opportunity to work across all platforms. Responsibilities Ensuring a safe work practice by acting in compliance with Vestas HSE requirements Ensuring own P.P.E checks are done, and monthly check sheets are logged Daily communication with Service Managers, SP Supervisors, and Project Managers Identifying, registering, and coordinating deviations Trouble shooting of electrical and mechanical systems and fault diagnosis Registration of time and material consumption in Vestas systems Carry out the maintenance, repair and replacement activities of turbine components Qualifications Marine Engineer, Electrician, or technical degree from a school/college/university Minimum 5 years of professional experience with electrical or mechanical systems.
