Full description
Are you able to apply financial knowledge with operational knowledge to create and deliver transparency and support enhanced performance? Do you want to be part of an internationally diverse team, interacting daily with global stakeholders? If so, this might just be a match. The NCE region covers more than 40 legal entities i.e. Sweden, Norway, Denmark, UK, Germany, Netherlands, Austria, Poland, Romania, Czech. The wide span of legal entities in combination of a centralized finance function requires the finance team to be structured, flexible and open for continuous change. The Regional Finance team in NCE is covering business and financial controlling activities for the region.
