Position Summary: The Physical Security Operations (PSO) Analyst provides 24x7 support to plan and respond to enterprise-wide crises or emergency situations impacting our facilities, employees, customers, or assets. This role is critical in ensuring the safety and security of our organization through analysis, escalated monitoring, incident management, and effective communication. The PSO Analyst supports Physical Security Operations groups, including Protection Services, Security Systems, and Regulatory. This role involves both virtual and in-person responsibilities. Essential Responsibilities: Holistic Risk Management: Identify, assess, and mitigate risks to ensure the safety and security of the organization. Implement as directed, comprehensive risk management strategies to address potential threats and vulnerabilities. Threat Intelligence and Response: Coordinate with Protection Services, Threat Intelligence, electrical operations units, and other relevant groups to resolve high or critical severity level incidents. Facilitate all layers of the Common Operating Picture. Perform critical analysis of escalations from event triage to incident escalation. Regulatory Compliance and Governance: Ensure compliance with relevant security regulations and governance standards. Maintain up-to-date knowledge of regulatory requirements and assist as directed, with necessary measures to adhere to these standards. Incident Response and Business Continuity: Provide operational support through analysis. Identify and respond to enterprise-level events, incidents, or crises.
