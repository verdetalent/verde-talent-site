Job Description:

Role Overview

We are looking for a driven FP&A Analyst to support financial reporting, variance analysis, and business insights. This role will play a key part in ensuring high-quality financial data, generating actionable insights, and enabling effective decision-making across the organization.

Key Responsibilities

  • Extract, consolidate, and validate financial actuals from SAP ERP
  • Retrieve and reconcile planning data from Anaplan
  • Perform monthly variance analysis (Actuals vs Plan/Forecast) and identify key drivers
  • Prepare concise financial summaries, dashboards, and presentations for business stakeholders
  • Track Purchase Orders (POs) and monitor spend against budgets, highlighting risks and trends
  • Support ad hoc analysis and data requests for FP&A and cross-functional teams
  • Contribute to improving reporting processes, data quality, and automation initiatives

Qualifications & Experience

  • CA / CA Inter / MBA (Finance)
  • 2–4 years of experience in FP&A, financial analysis, or corporate finance
  • Strong understanding of financial statements and cost drivers

Skills & Competencies

  • Strong Excel and data analysis skills
  • High attention to detail and data accuracy
  • Ability to work with large datasets and multiple systems
  • Effective communication and stakeholder coordination

Career Growth & Development

  • Opportunity to progressively take on responsibilities in forecasting, budgeting, and financial planning cycles
  • Exposure to business partnering and strategic decision support
  • Hands-on experience with enterprise systems such as SAP ERP and Anaplan
  • Development into a broader FP&A role with end-to-end ownership over time
