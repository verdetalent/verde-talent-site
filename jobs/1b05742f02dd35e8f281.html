About Us

With electric vehicles expected to be nearly 30% of new vehicle sales by 2025 and more than 50% by 2040, electric mobility is becoming a reality. ChargePoint (NYSE: CHPT) is at the center of this revolution, powering one of the world’s leading EV charging networks and a comprehensive set of hardware, software and mobile solutions for every charging need across North America and Europe. We bring together drivers, businesses, automakers, policymakers, utilities and other stakeholders to make e-mobility a global reality.

Since our founding in 2007, ChargePoint has focused solely on making the transition to electric easy for businesses, fleets and drivers. ChargePoint offers a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity to create an all-electric future and a trillion-dollar market.

At ChargePoint, we foster a positive and productive work environment by committing to live our values of Be Courageous, Charge Together, Love our Customers, Operate with Openness, and Relentlessly Pursue Awesome. These values guide how we show up every day, align, and work together to build a brighter future for all of us.

Join the team that is building the EV charging industry and make your mark on how people and goods will get everywhere they need to go, in any context, for generations to come.

Reports To

Senior Director - Support Systems & Tools Integration

What You Will Be Doing

Develop and support scalable customer platforms, APIs, integrations, and modern web applications using Node.js and React.js technologies. This is a hands-on senior individual contributor role focused on building high-quality software, solving complex technical problems, mentoring junior engineers, and contributing to modern API-first enterprise platforms.

The ideal candidate is a strong full stack engineer with practical experience across backend and frontend development, along with exposure to mobile technologies, cloud platforms, and modern AI-assisted engineering practices.

What You Will Bring to ChargePoint

  • Develop and enhance scalable backend services using Node.js and TypeScript
  • Build responsive and maintainable frontend applications using React.js
  • Develop and consume RESTful APIs and integration services
  • Contribute to API-first and integration-driven platform initiatives
  • Write maintainable, secure, and high-quality code
  • Participate in code reviews, technical discussions, and engineering best practices
  • Guide and mentor junior engineers on development standards and delivery quality
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams on solution implementation and delivery
  • Support CI/CD pipelines and modern engineering workflows
  • Comfortable using modern AI-assisted development tools (GitHub Copilot, Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, etc.) to enhance engineering productivity and development workflows

Requirements

  • 5+ years of software engineering experience in full stack development
  • Strong experience with Node.js, TypeScript, and React.js
  • Strong understanding of REST APIs, integration patterns, and scalable application design
  • Experience working with relational and/or NoSQL databases
  • Experience working with cloud platforms (GCP preferred)
  • Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines, Git, and modern engineering workflows
  • Exposure to React Native or mobile application development is preferred
  • Exposure to Python for automation, integrations, or backend services is a plus
  • Familiarity with AI/LLM APIs or AI-assisted engineering tools is preferred
  • Exposure to enterprise platforms or CRM-integrated applications is a plus
  • Strong problem-solving, debugging, and collaboration skills
  • Ability to work independently and mentor junior engineers
  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field preferred

Location

Bengaluru, India
