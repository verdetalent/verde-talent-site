Full description

The H&S Data & Compliance Manager supports the organization's Health, Safety, Environment, and Quality programs by analyzing safety data, identifying trends, and driving corrective actions that prevent recurrence of incidents. This role ensures compliance through accurate documentation, oversight of workers' compensation processes, and support for risk assessments, while also collaborating with cross-functional teams to promote safe practices. Serving as a key resource for employees, the H&S Data & Compliance Analyst helps maintain a safe, compliant, and proactive workplace aligned with both global and local standards.

What you will do

Responsibilities, include, but not limited to:

* Conduct in-depth analysis of injury, near-miss and inspection non-conformities data to identify trends, patterns, and root causes.

* Categorize events and develop reporting that highlights risks and opportunities for targeted safety initiatives to prevent the recurrence of safety events.

* Translate findings into clear, actionable recommendations for leadership and operations teams.

* Track the status of actionable recommendations and corrective actions to completion.

* Ensure compliance with Policy 106 for all event reports and provide support and as it pertains to all affected business units

* Complete and distribute the Monthly Health & Safety Report

* Monitor and track Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) related to safety events and inspection targets.

* Provide regular KPI status updates to relevant business units and leadership.

* Communicate event and inspection objectives to HSEQ Operations & Maintenance teams and applicable business units.

* Collaborate with the Fleet Management team to monitor, analyze, and trend safe driving scorecards, supporting proactive safety initiatives and improvements.

* Support the development and execution of risk assessments across departments and projects.

* Review, update, and maintain all HSEQ documents--including policies, procedures, and manuals--to ensure alignment with global standards and compliance with local requirements.

* Manage document review teams and work closely with subject matter experts to develop, validate, and improve the accuracy of relevant documents.

* Ensure timely communication of changes and updates to employees, with a focus on building awareness and understanding.

* Manage and maintain the Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) and safety equipment process, ensuring employees have the tools and resources they need to perform their work safely.

* Review, maintain, and ensure compliance with office emergency action plans and procedures.

* Assist in creating and delivering safety-focused communications and campaigns.

* Serve as a point of contact for employees regarding safety processes, resources, and documentation.

* Manage the internal process for purchasing PPE such FR clothing and safety boots and act as an administrator for the process (i.e. Gearcor)

* Assist in planning HSEQ-related needs and initiatives.

* Review HSEQ-owned contracts within the Health & Safety scope to ensure contractual obligations and operational needs are met (e.g., Gearcor, CORE).

* Evaluate contractual annexes and agreements to verify alignment within HSEQ requirements and standards.

* Act as purchasing support for H&S team needs

* Participate in or lead cross function project or audit teams to support company HSEQ programs and campaigns.

* Contribute to the overall needs and objectives of the unit and company.

* Maintain a positive rapport with various state and federal regulatory agencies

* Maintenance, development, and implementation of HSEQ manual, programs, compliance plans, and initiatives focused on Company Safety & Environment values, goals, objectives, and various regulations including OHSAS 18001, ISO 14001, and ISO 9001

What you will bring

* BS in Safety, Environment or related work experience

* Minimum of 5+ years of related experience

* Proficiency in Excel required; expertise in Smartsheet and/or Power BI highly preferred

* Comparing, copying, calculating, compiling, analyzing, coordinating, communicating, instructing, and interpersonal skills.

* Proficiency in Excel required; expertise in Smartsheet and/or Power BI highly preferred.

Who we are

Enel North America:

Enel North America is a proven renewables leader delivering clean, flexible and sustainable energy solutions.

Enel Spa

Enel is a multinational power company and a leading integrated player in the global power and renewables markets, present in 27 countries worldwide.

At global level, it is the largest renewable player, the foremost electricity distribution network player by number of grid customers served and the biggest retail operator by customer base.

Enel generates electricity with approximately 93 GW of total capacity. Enel Green Power, the Group's renewables arm, has a total capacity of approximately 68 GW and a generation mix that includes wind, solar, geothermal, and hydroelectric power, as well as energy storage facilities, installed in Europe, the Americas, Africa, Asia, and Oceania.

Enel Grids, the Group's global business line dedicated to the management of the electricity distribution service worldwide, delivers electricity through a network of 1.9 million kilometers with more than 69 million end users connected to its distribution grids. Enel Commercial is the Group's arm dedicated to customers around the world with the aim of effectively providing products and services based on their energy needs and encouraging them towards a more conscious and sustainable use of energy. It provides electricity, gas and integrated services to more than 29 million customers globally including households, enterprises, industries and public administrations. In addition, it offers flexibility services aggregating 10.2 GW and has over 34,000 owned public charging points for electric mobility.
