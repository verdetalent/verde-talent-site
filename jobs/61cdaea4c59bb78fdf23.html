Full description

Step into a role where your leadership ensures the quality of the world’s most advanced renewable energy solutions. At Vestas Szczecin, you’ll lead a collaborative Quality team in one of the region’s key manufacturing sites, promoting excellence in both processes and people. If you’re motivated by high standards, continuous improvement, and improving product quality on a global scale, this is your opportunity to contribute to the future of wind energy

The Quality Department covers both Quality Assurance (QA) and Quality Control (QC). Our team supports the organization in developing and maintaining procedures, policies, work instructions, standards, and records. Ultimately, we ensure that every product leaving the factory meets all required specifications.

We are also responsible for maintaining compliance of the Quality Management System (QMS) with international standards and for managing and resolving customer concerns.

We are seeking a motivated Manager for Production Quality. You will lead a Quality team of approximately 60 employees, including around 20 engineers and supervisors, as well as several external consultants.

Your role is to uphold and improve quality standards in the production area, ensuring alignment with Vestas requirements. You will enhance productivity and product quality by placing safety and quality at the forefront of all decisions and actions.

Responsibilities

  • Deploy the Factory Quality Strategy and policies, ensuring alignment with corporate standards and site objectives
  • Ensure a safe and healthy work environment, promoting consistent safety behaviors across all quality activities
  • Provide leadership that secures performance in safety, quality, delivery, and cost throughout the production area
  • Lead and develop a diverse and newly formed team, including engineers, supervisors, and quality specialists
  • Promote quality awareness and a customer focused zero failure culture across the factory.
  • Improve processes that impact product quality, establishing robust controls and consistent compliance
  • Facilitate and support quality improvement initiatives, including root cause analysis and the elimination of recurring defects and process shortcomings

Qualifications

  • Master degree in engineering, quality, production or related field
  • Several years work experience in quality/engineering environment within automotive or renewables
  • People management experience & experienced with customer relations and audits including ISO
  • Skilled with product verification/validation and very good Knowledge in quality systems
  • Good understanding of quality tools (e.g. Six Sigma Black Belt - welcomed)
  • Fluency in English (Polish language skills nice to have)

Competencies

  • Leadership skills; managerial decisiveness
  • Effective cooperation and communication skills
  • Customer-focused, technical and result-oriented
  • Good in building network across function internal and external
  • Solid analytical approach and problem-solving skills at minimum specialist level

About Vestas

Vestas is the energy industry’s global partner on sustainable energy solutions. We are specialised in designing, manufacturing, installing, and servicing wind turbines, both onshore and offshore.

Across the globe, we have installed more wind power than anyone else.
