Are you looking for an exciting job where you can put your skills and talents to work at a company you can feel proud to be a part of? Do you want a workplace that will challenge you and offer you opportunities to learn and grow? A position at Xcel Energy could be just what you’re looking for. We anticipate the need for one or more Journeyman Lineman to be located in Sterling, CO The starting rate of pay for this position is $63.95 hourly. Summary Perform the duties of lineman classification involving overhead and underground electric distribution systems. Special Instructions: 1. The successful candidate must have the physical ability, endurance, and desire to perform potentially strenuous outdoor work, at all times of the day and night, and under various weather conditions. 2. The successful applicant will be expected to respond to emergency callouts after normal work hours and on weekends. 3. The successful applicant must live within 40 minutes/40 miles of the Sterling Service Center. 4. The successful candidate must be able to perform safe, proficient, and effective pole-climbing. 5. Applicants must be self-motivated, work well with others, have a positive attitude and be committed to working safely and productively. For External and Non-Xcel Energy Journeyman candidates, verification of Journeyman Lineman qualifications and knowledge will include written testing and/or practical skills evaluation during the application process. Minimum Requirements High School diploma or equivalent. Valid Class A CDL driver’s license including a valid Medical Examiner’s Certificate. All applicants must have completed a qualified apprenticeship program and possess a Journeyman Lineman certification. Must be willing to live within 40 minutes/40 miles of the Sterling Service Center.
