Skilled Worker / Semi-Skilled Worker | Full-Time | Field Operations Job Title: CPX Technical Supervisor Location: Americas Region (Field & Remote) The CPX Commissioning Supervisor plays a key role in overseeing the full lifecycle of commissioning activities across the Vestas Americas region. This position is responsible for ensuring safe, efficient, and high-quality execution of commissioning operations by coordinating training, workforce deployment, and site support. The Supervisor ensures alignment between project schedules and available resources while driving operational excellence through structured planning, advanced troubleshooting, and consistent process execution. This role directly supports successful project delivery and contributes to achieving regional business objectives.
