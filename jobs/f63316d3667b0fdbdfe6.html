Role Overview

The Robotics Field Electrical & Mechanical Supervisor is responsible for overseeing all electrical and mechanical aspects of the MAXIMO module installation robot fleet, ensuring robotic equipment is inspected, maintained, and serviced safely and reliably in the field. Applying extensive technical expertise, this role leads the execution of preventative and corrective maintenance—including troubleshooting, repair, servicing, and calibration—to keep the fleet performing at a high and consistent level.

This role demands strong leadership, hands-on technical skill, and the ability to communicate effectively across teams. The Supervisor develops efficient maintenance strategies, enforces compliance with safety regulations, provides technical guidance to crew members, and works collaboratively to optimize robot performance, minimize downtime, and ensure uninterrupted operation.

A successful candidate will be highly motivated, passionate about their work, and eager to push the boundaries of what is currently achievable in the field. This mission-driven individual will collaborate closely across MAXIMO—including engineering, business leadership, and construction management—providing feedback, issues lists, reliability reports, and brainstorming solutions to be implemented on sites.

Note: No electrical installation work is performed under this role. Electrical responsibilities are limited to the inspection, testing, servicing, and troubleshooting of factory-built robotic equipment.

Major Responsibilities

  • Oversee all electrical and mechanical systems internal to the MAXIMO robotic equipment.
  • Inspect, test, service, and troubleshoot robotic electrical components, controls, safety systems, and power systems.
  • Perform and oversee preventative maintenance and corrective repairs on robotic equipment.
  • Diagnose electrical and electromechanical issues and determine appropriate corrective actions.
  • Establish, follow, and maintain standard inspection, maintenance, and troubleshooting procedures, including Safety/Quality SOPs.
  • Maintain accurate records of inspections, repairs, maintenance activities, and equipment downtime.
  • Provide routine reporting on robot condition, reliability, and field issues to senior managers.
  • Train and support field mechanics, technicians, and operators on safe operation and electrical awareness related to robotic equipment, and build training material as needed.
  • Coordinate with Operations, Engineering, Safety, and robotics vendors as needed.
  • Support continuous improvement efforts related to robot performance and uptime.
  • Work across MAXIMO and field construction leadership teams to build excitement and confidence around the MAXIMO vision and objectives, and to secure direct and indirect resources.

Regulatory, Compliance & Safety Responsibilities

  • Comply with all safety regulations and procedures, ensuring a safe working environment at all times for the whole crew.
  • Enforce safety standards and exercise stop-work authority when unsafe conditions exist.
  • Ensure robotic electrical systems meet applicable safety and regulatory standards.
  • Serve as the company point of contact for inspectors or regulatory agencies solely in matters related to licensing status and electrically powered robotic equipment, with no authority or responsibility for installed electrical work.

Electrical Scope (Boundaries)

  • Electrical responsibilities are limited to inspection, testing, servicing, and troubleshooting of factory-built robotic equipment.
  • No electrical installation work is performed.
  • No site power infrastructure, permanent wiring, or permitted electrical work is included in this role.
  • All jobsite electrical infrastructure is outside the scope of this position.
