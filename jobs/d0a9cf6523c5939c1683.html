Full description
Would you like to join an innovative and inspiring manufacturing environment? Are you motivated by producing high-quality products and working as part of a skilled team? We are currently looking for a Production Operative to join our blade manufacturing facility on the Isle of Wight. Tasks & Responsibilities As a Production Operative, you will join our blade factory on the Isle of Wight, working alongside skilled and motivated colleagues to manufacture high-quality products in accordance with established procedures, documentation, and quality standards. Your responsibilities will include: Building blades in accordance with the production plan Manufacturing products to the required quality standards using the latest controlled documentation issued by Vestas Maintaining a safe, clean, and organised work area Looking after tools and equipment within your work area Following safe working practices and encouraging colleagues to do the same Performing lay-up of materials (carbon fibre and glass fibre) in moulds according to lay-up plans Applying resins using vacuum equipment Applying bonding materials as required Using power tools for trimming and grinding to meet the required finish Preparing material kits for fellow operatives Contributing to continuous improvement initiatives, including quality improvements and Takt Time reduction activities Shift Pattern This role operates on a fixed rotating shift pattern within our 24/7 manufacturing environment: 4 days on, 4 days off: 06:45 – 19:00 (Day Shift) 4 nights on, 4 days off: 18:45 – 07:00 (Night Shift) Each shift is 12.25 hours in duration. Skills & Experience Our ideal colleague enjoys working in a manufacturing environment and takes pride in producing high-quality products while maintaining the highest standards of safety and quality. You may hold formal qualifications such as NVQ, HNC, HND, or City & Guilds , or simply demonstrate a solid work ethic, a willingness to learn, and a commitment to developing your skills....
