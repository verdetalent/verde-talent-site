Full description

Would you like to join an innovative and inspiring manufacturing environment?

Are you motivated by producing high-quality products and working as part of a skilled team? We are currently looking for a Production Operative to join our blade manufacturing facility on the Isle of Wight.

Tasks & Responsibilities

As a Production Operative, you will join our blade factory on the Isle of Wight, working alongside skilled and motivated colleagues to manufacture high-quality products in accordance with established procedures, documentation, and quality standards.

Your responsibilities will include:

  • Building blades in accordance with the production plan
  • Manufacturing products to the required quality standards using the latest controlled documentation issued by Vestas
  • Maintaining a safe, clean, and organised work area
  • Looking after tools and equipment within your work area
  • Following safe working practices and encouraging colleagues to do the same
  • Performing lay-up of materials (carbon fibre and glass fibre) in moulds according to lay-up plans
  • Applying resins using vacuum equipment
  • Applying bonding materials as required
  • Using power tools for trimming and grinding to meet the required finish
  • Preparing material kits for fellow operatives
  • Contributing to continuous improvement initiatives, including quality improvements and Takt Time reduction activities

Shift Pattern

This role operates on a fixed rotating shift pattern within our 24/7 manufacturing environment:

  • 4 days on, 4 days off: 06:45 – 19:00 (Day Shift)
  • 4 nights on, 4 days off: 18:45 – 07:00 (Night Shift)

Each shift is 12.25 hours in duration.

Skills & Experience

Our ideal colleague enjoys working in a manufacturing environment and takes pride in producing high-quality products while maintaining the highest standards of safety and quality.

You may hold formal qualifications such as NVQ, HNC, HND, or City & Guilds,or simply demonstrate a solid work ethic, a willingness to learn, and a commitment to developing your skills.

We are also looking for someone who:

  • Has good problem-solving skills and can contribute effectively within a production team
  • Can perform tasks efficiently while following work instructions and procedures
  • Demonstrates a professional attitude towards colleagues, management, and customers
  • Handles shifting priorities and schedules with a positive approach
  • Enjoys working collaboratively as part of a team
  • Demonstrates a solid commitment to quality and safety

Why Join Us?

Join a global organization leading innovation and growth in the offshore wind industry. You will become part of a skilled team within a professional, collaborative, and forward-thinking manufacturing environment.

In Manufacturing, We Offer:

  • Supportive colleagues who work together to meet shared goals
  • The opportunity to contribute to the global transition towards sustainable energy

About Vestas

Vestas is the energy industry’s global partner on sustainable energy solutions. We are specialised in designing, manufacturing, installing, and servicing wind turbines, both onshore and offshore.

Across the globe, we have installed more wind power than anyone else. We consider ourselves pioneers within the industry, as we continuously aim to design new solutions and technologies to create a more sustainable future for all of us. With more than 185 GW of wind power installed worldwide and 40+ years of experience in wind energy, we have an unmatched track record demonstrating our expertise within the field.

With 30,000 employees globally, we are a diverse team united by a common goal: to power the solution – today, tomorrow, and far into the future.
