Contracts Engineer – (Quantity Surveyor)

Location: Eurocentral office with hybrid working pattern

Salary: from £55,000 - £68,750 with strong benefits package and excellent career progression

Permanent / Full‑time– 37 hours per week

Closing date: 6th August 2026

Create a better future, quicker!

At ScottishPower, we're driving the transition to a cleaner, greener energy future. As our electricity network evolves to meet increasing demand and support the UK's net zero ambitions, we're investing significantly in our transmission infrastructure and the critical services that keep it operating safely and efficiently.

We're looking for a commercially minded Contracts Engineer (Quantity Surveyor) to join our Transmission Operations team at Eurocentral. This is an exciting opportunity to take ownership of a portfolio of high-value operational contracts, influencing strategic decisions, delivering best value for the business, and helping ensure the reliability of Scotland's electricity transmission network.

What you'll be doing

We are seeking an experienced and commercially focused Contracts Engineer (Quantity Surveyor)to join our Transmission business and support the development of a portfolio of Framework Agreements valued at approximately £100 million per annum.

You will work alongside the operational team to develop comprehensive tenders packs. You will support procurement in assessing the tender returns, technical and commercial evaluation where required, post tender presentations, engaging with legal as required to agree terms and conditions. On completion of the contract award, you will develop a handover document and socialise this with the framework users ensuring they are aware of how to manage the framework.

Key responsibilities include:

  • Lead and manage the tendering process from pre-procurement through to contract award.
  • Prepare cost estimates, tender documentation, and pricing schedules.
  • Develop procurement strategies aligned with project objectives.
  • Evaluate contractor and supplier tenders, including commercial and technical assessments.
  • Produce tender evaluation reports and recommendations for contract award.
  • Identify, assess, and manage commercial risks and opportunities.
  • Ensure compliance with organisational procurement policies and procedures.
  • Support contract negotiations and post-tender clarifications.
  • Review contractor valuations, variations, and change requests.
  • Monitor project costs and provide accurate commercial reporting.
  • Work collaboratively with internal stakeholders and external consultants to ensure successful project outcomes.

What you'll bring

We're looking for someone who combines strong commercial awareness with excellent stakeholder management skills and the ability to operate confidently in a fast-paced operational environment.

You'll have:

  • A degree in Quantity Surveying, Contract Management or a related discipline, or equivalent relevant experience.
  • Strong experience in commercial contract management, procurement and tendering activities across complex projects or operational environments.
  • Experience working with NEC/FIDIC.
  • Experience within utilities, energy, infrastructure or asset-intensive industries would be highly advantageous. We also welcome applications from candidates with relevant experience across the wider construction sector, including civils, engineering, maintenance, rail, water, highways or major projects.
  • Experience preparing tender packs, evaluating submissions and supporting procurement processes.
  • Strong commercial and contractual knowledge, with the ability to identify risks and develop effective mitigation strategies.
  • Excellent analytical and decision-making skills, with the confidence to prioritise and respond to changing business needs.
  • The ability to think strategically, balancing short-term operational requirements with long-term business objectives.
  • Strong communication and influencing skills, with experience building productive relationships across multiple stakeholders.
  • Expert knowledge of analytical and quantity surveying techniques is desirable.
  • Provide contractual advice as and when required.
  • Experience coaching, mentoring and supporting the development of colleagues and teams.

Why SP Energy Networks

SP Energy Networks is part of the Iberdrola Group, one of the world’s largest integrated utility companies and a world leader in wind energy. We keep electricity flowing to homes and businesses through Central and Southern Scotland, North Wales and in the North West of England. We operate over 4000km of cables and lines that make-up the transmission network – connecting infrastructure like wind farms into the electricity system. It’s a role that puts us right at the heart of Scotland’s ambition to be Net Zero by 2045. And we’re taking it very seriously.

We’re investing over £12 billion into our transmission network, directly supporting the rapid growth needed in renewable energy.
