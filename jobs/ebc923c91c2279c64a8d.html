About Us

With electric vehicles expected to be nearly 30% of new vehicle sales by 2025 and more than 50% by 2040, electric mobility is becoming a reality. ChargePoint (NYSE: CHPT) is at the center of this revolution, powering one of the world’s leading EV charging networks and a comprehensive set of hardware, software and mobile solutions for every charging need across North America and Europe. We bring together drivers, businesses, automakers, policymakers, utilities and other stakeholders to make e-mobility a global reality.

Since our founding in 2007, ChargePoint has focused solely on making the transition to electric easy for businesses, fleets and drivers. ChargePoint offers a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity to create an all-electric future and a trillion-dollar market.

At ChargePoint, we foster a positive and productive work environment by committing to live our values of Be Courageous, Charge Together, Love our Customers, Operate with Openness, and Relentlessly Pursue Awesome. These values guide how we show up every day, align, and work together to build a brighter future for all of us.

Join the team that is building the EV charging industry and make your mark on how people and goods will get everywhere they need to go, in any context, for generations to come.

Reports To

Senior Director - International Marketing & Strategy

What You Will Be Doing

ChargePoint is building its marketing engine in Europe from the ground up. As the a dedicated marketing hire in the region, you will work closely with the European sales team to generate pipeline, execute events, and lay the foundation for a high-impact marketing function in a fast-growing market.

This is a hands-on execution role where you will own delivery end-to-end. Rather than creating strategy for others to execute, you will be directly responsible for bringing campaigns to life—whether that’s booking venues, running webinars, building email sequences, or measuring results. Reporting to the Sr. Director of International Marketing, you will serve as the primary marketing operator in Europe with the opportunity to shape ChargePoint’s presence across the region and build a team over time.

Key responsibilities include:

  • Planning and executing field marketing programs across Europe, including trade shows, customer events, roundtables, partner events, and roadshows
  • Running regional webinars and digital events end-to-end (topic development, speaker coordination, promotion, and follow-up)
  • Partnering directly with the European sales team to support account-level marketing efforts that help open opportunities and accelerate deals
  • Localizing and adapting global campaigns, content, and messaging for European markets using AI tools to scale across languages efficiently
  • Building and managing regional email campaigns, landing pages, and nurture programs in collaboration with demand generation teams
  • Managing relationships with local event organizers, media contacts, and agency partners
  • Owning the European marketing calendar and aligning with global teams on launches, announcements, and campaigns
  • Tracking and reporting on campaign performance, event ROI, and pipeline contribution to demonstrate impact and drive further investment

What You Will Bring to ChargePoint

  • Proven B2B field marketing experience within European markets, including hands-on ownership of events, vendors, and campaigns, preferably within the EV Industry
  • Fluency in English plus at least one additional major European language (e.g., German, French, or similar)
  • Comfort operating as a team of one in a startup-like environment, with strong resourcefulness and self-direction
  • A collaborative, results-driven approach to working with sales teams, with a clear understanding of how marketing contributes to revenue goals
  • Strong organizational skills and attention to detail, with the ability to manage multiple campaigns, events, and stakeholders across regions
  • Willingness to travel regularly across Europe to support field marketing activities
  • A builder mindset with enthusiasm for creating processes, programs, and ultimately a team from the ground up

**Requirements**

  • Hands-on experience executing marketing programs across multiple European markets
  • Demonstrated ability to partner closely with sales to drive pipeline and measurable business outcomes
  • Proficiency in using AI tools to accelerate marketing execution (e.g., content creation, translation, campaign analysis, event planning)
  • Experience managing end-to-end campaign execution including events, webinars, email, and digital programs
  • Strong communication skills and ability to coordinate across international stakeholders and time zones
  • Ability to operate independently in a fast-paced, high-growth environment without an established regional marketing infrastructure

**Location**

UK, France, Munich, Amsterdam
