Administrative | Full-Time | Supply Chain and Logistics The Service Department is responsible for the proper functioning of our customers' wind farms. In this sense, we ensure the performance of each turbine throughout its useful life. We guarantee that the turbines are operational at their maximum capacity, providing the best energy yield for customers. As such, the team is crucial for us to maintain a loyal and long-term relationship with our customers. The dispatcher is responsible to plan and schedule all types of work orders in the established field of operations for the maintenance of wind turbines.
