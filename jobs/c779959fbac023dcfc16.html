Position Summary This position leads the program development, implementation, and management to capture, maintain, and analyze data & work processes accurately and consistently for operational, customer, and regulatory requirements. The position also plays a crucial role in handling and analyzing large datasets to uncover valuable insights. They use statistical methods, machine learning algorithms, and data visualization techniques to interpret complex data and translate the data findings into actionable business strategies that support data-driven decision-making. Additionally, they may assist in developing predictive models, automating data collection processes and ensuring data quality and integrity. Essential Responsibilities Responsible for obtaining, creating, and analyzing data to monitor business performance & create/generate operational. Identify performance trends and areas for improvement. Leads the development & implementation of technology, information systems, and strategies targeted at the maintenance/tracking of data ensuring compliance with all business, customer, and regulatory requirements. Liaison to other business areas regarding data quality & use for business reporting. Directs the formation, development, and implementation of tools specifically designed to improve the accuracy, timeliness, and integrity of data. Track & reports performance results. In cooperation with leadership, identifies actions to improve performance and/or performance tracking.
