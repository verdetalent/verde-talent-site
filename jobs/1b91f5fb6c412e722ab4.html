They will work with cross-functional stakeholders to deliver high-performing procurement strategies while improving cost, quality and on time delivery Responsible for directing the business priorities to shape, define and execute the procurement strategy for the Service Crane category. Crane subject matter expert in procuring crane services to support turbine repair, maintenance, and special projects. White collar, safety professionals, and other support personal related to wind services site activities. Manage and represent global initiatives at the RPO level Ability to manage the RFP process from specification development, quote process, supplier selection, contract development, stakeholder management and business awards. Close collaboration with key local and global stakeholders including construction, sales, project management, engineering, quality, legal and supply chain to optimize scope cost competitiveness Attend weekly sales management calls and translate potential contracts into procurement activities Provide weekly report out of all contract's status including future projects to align stakeholders with customer expectations Understand and interpret technical specifications.
