Basic Job Functions:

The Collections Specialist manages a portfolio of domestic and global customer accounts to accelerate cash collection, reduce past-due receivables, and support healthy working capital while preserving strong customer relationships. This position monitors account aging, investigates payment delays, resolves billing and account discrepancies, secures payment commitments, and coordinates with customers and internal stakeholders to remove barriers to payment. This role requires an assertive individual who is responsible for accurate and timely invoice submission to customers and collection follow-up. The role applies sound judgment, data analysis, ERP and collections technology, and approved AI-enabled tools to prioritize collection activity, identify emerging payment risk, and maintain accurate, timely account records across a complex global manufacturing environment.

Education/Experience:

Required

  • Associate degree with a minimum of two years of relevant experience in commercial collections, accounts receivable, credit, billing, customer account management, or a related financial operations function.
  • Experience managing a portfolio of business-to-business customer accounts, including aging review, customer follow-up, payment-commitment tracking, account reconciliation, and collection escalation.
  • Working knowledge of accounts receivable principles, invoice-to-cash processes, customer account reconciliation, payment terms, credit and debit memos, unapplied cash, deductions, and dispute resolution.
  • Experience working within an enterprise financial system such as Oracle ERP, SAP, Microsoft Dynamics, NetSuite, or a comparable platform.
  • Proficiency with Microsoft Excel, including filters, pivot tables, lookup functions, aging analysis, account reconciliation, and data validation.
  • Demonstrated ability to interpret invoices, account statements, purchase orders, payment remittances, contractual payment terms, and customer correspondence.
  • Ability to communicate professionally with customers and internal stakeholders regarding sensitive financial issues, overdue balances, disputes, and payment commitments.
  • Ability to work with approved AI-enabled or automated AR tools to prioritize accounts, identify collection risks, draft customer communications, or analyze payment patterns while independently validating outputs, protecting confidential information, and applying appropriate human judgment.
  • Knowledge of structured collection methodologies, including aging segmentation, risk-based account prioritization, root-cause analysis, escalation protocols, promise-to-pay tracking, and dispute management.

Preferred

  • Bachelor’s degree in accounting, finance, business administration, economics, supply chain, or a related discipline.
  • 3-5 years of Accounts Receivable or Collections experience, including management of large, complex customer portfolios and resolution of billing disputes and payment issues.
  • Strong working knowledge of Oracle ERP, CRM, Order-to-Cash processes, advanced Excel, reporting, dashboards, aging trends, and data analysis.
  • Strong analytical and root-cause problem-solving skills, with the ability to assess collection risk, identify payment-delay drivers, and clearly communicate exposure and priorities to leadership.
  • Assertive, proactive collections approach, with the ability to secure and track customer payment commitments, confidently escalate unresolved past-due balances, and maintain strong customer relationships.
  • Demonstrated ability to work cross-functionally across Finance, Fulfillment, Contract Management, Treasury, Business Development, and other stakeholders to resolve contract, tax, billing, and fulfillment issues affecting collections.
  • Experience working within structured escalation frameworks and driving accountability through clear follow-up and documentation.
  • Continuous improvement mindset, with demonstrated experience documenting, streamlining, and improving collections and supporting processes.
  • Professional certification or coursework related to credit, collections, accounting, or finance, such as NACM credit credentials, is advantageous.
  • Experience managing high-value, multinational, strategic, or contract-based customer accounts.
  • Experience working with automated collections, cash application, or credit management systems preferred.
  • Demonstrated ability to interpret customer contracts, payment terms, tax documentation, and commercial agreements preferred.

Required Skills/Competencies:

  • Commercial Collections Management: Knowledge of aging strategies, customer payment behavior, collection cycles, payment commitments, repayment arrangements, escalation thresholds, and recovery practices required to manage a business-to-business receivables portfolio effectively.
  • Account Reconciliation and Financial Investigation: Ability to analyze invoices, payments, credits, deductions, disputes, and customer account activity to identify the underlying cause of balances and determine the appropriate path to resolution.
  • Invoice-to-Cash Process Knowledge: Working knowledge of the interconnected billing, collections, cash-application, credit, dispute-management, and account-maintenance processes that influence receivables performance and customer experience.
  • Collection Risk Analysis and Prioritization: Ability to evaluate aging, balance exposure, payment history, customer commitments, dispute status, and business impact to prioritize collection activity and identify accounts requiring intervention or escalation.
  • Collections Systems and Data Proficiency: Ability to interpret and maintain accurate customer, invoice, payment, dispute, and collection information across ERP, CRM, collections, reporting, and analytical systems.
  • Negotiating Skills: Prepares options, balances interests, and achieves mutually beneficial agreements while maintaining strong relationships.
  • Data-Driven & Evidence-Based Thinking (Analytical Aptitude): Frames problems clearly, gathers and evaluates relevant data, tests assumptions (including AI-generated insights), and translates analysis into sound decisions while balancing detail with speed.
  • Financial Acumen: Understands financial drivers and implications of decisions. Uses cost-benefit thinking to manage resources responsibly and support sound business choices.
  • Problem Solving Skills: Identifies root causes using structured methods, tests countermeasures, prevents recurrence, and scales effective solutions.
  • Attention to Detail: Delivers accurate, high-quality work efficiently by applying checks, validation methods, and structured reviews. Prevents errors while maintaining pace and focus on overall objectives.
  • Effective Communication Skills: Communicates with clarity, structure, and empathy. Tailors messages to audience needs and ensures understanding of actions, ownership, and next steps.
  • Builds Effective Relationships: Builds trust through transparency, respect, and reliability. Listens actively, navigates conflict constructively, and collaborates effectively across cultures, functions, and working styles.
  • AI & Digital Fluency: Uses digital and AI-enabled tools responsibly to improve productivity, quality, and decision-making. Understands capabilities and limitations, validates outputs, protects data and confidentiality, and knows when to escalate or apply human judgment.
  • Microsoft Office Proficiency: Proficient use of all Microsoft Office suite programs.
  • Continuous Improvement Mindset: Identifies and contributes to improvements in collections processes, systems, and workflows, applying Kaizen principles by participating in or supporting improvement initiatives and problem-solving activities.
  • Learning Mindset and Adaptability: Seeks opportunities to expand knowledge and develop skills, responds to change, and adopts new tools and processes.
  • Integrity: Acts ethically and transparently, protects confidential information, complies with policies, and uses AI responsibly and appropriately.
  • Customer-Focused Professionalism: Maintains a respectful, composed, and solutions-oriented approach during difficult financial conversations while appropriately protecting the organization’s interests.
  • Ownership and Follow-Through: Demonstrates personal accountability for commitments, pursues issues through resolution, and closes communication loops with customers and internal stakeholders.
  • Sound Judgment: Balances urgency, customer circumstances, financial exposure, contractual requirements, and business relationships when determining the appropriate course of action.
  • Collaborative Mindset: Builds trust across functions, shares information proactively, and works constructively with others to resolve issues that cross organizational boundaries.
  • Results Orientation: Prioritizes actions that improve cash flow, reduce aged receivables, and achieve measurable performance outcomes.

Essential Responsibilities:

  • Manage an assigned portfolio of domestic and global customer accounts, maintaining consistent collection coverage and accountability for past-due receivables.
  • Review accounts receivable aging, open invoices, account balances, payment history, dispute status, and collection activity to identify overdue or potentially at-risk accounts, and prioritize collection activity based on aging, value, payment risk, customer commitments, contractual requirements, and potential business impact.
  • Contact customers through approved communication channels to secure payment, clarify account status, and resolve barriers affecting timely settlement; obtain, document, and monitor customer payment commitments and payment dates, following up promptly when commitments are missed or materially changed.
  • Partner with Credit, Treasury, Finance, and Commercial teams to improve working capital performance and reduce financial risk exposure.
  • Identify recurring and systemic causes of customer payment delays, late payment, billing errors, deductions, disputes, and process breakdowns; communicate findings to appropriate process owners and recommend corrective actions to improve cash flow performance.
  • Provide insights and recommendations that support forecasting, risk management, and continuous improvement initiatives across the Order-to-Cash process, including process-improvement efforts that strengthen billing accuracy, collection effectiveness, dispute prevention, data quality, and customer experience.
  • Serve as a trusted business partner by balancing effective collections execution with long-term customer relationship management.
  • Reconcile customer accounts and investigate customer questions and disputes by researching invoices, payments, credits, deductions, short payments, unapplied cash, duplicate billings, pricing, quantities, taxes, proof of delivery, purchase orders, account statements, contractual terms, and other supporting documentation.
  • Coordinate with billing, cash application, credit, finance, sales, customer service, order management, logistics, tax, legal, and other functions to resolve issues preventing payment.
  • Prepare and submit properly supported requests for credit memos, debit memos, account adjustments, write-offs, refunds, or other corrective transactions in accordance with established approval requirements.
  • Provide customers with invoices, statements, payment instructions, lien waivers, proof of delivery, tax documentation, and other information required to facilitate payment.
  • Maintain complete and accurate collection notes, dispute classifications, reason codes, customer correspondence, payment commitments, and escalation records within designated systems.
  • Escalate material collection risks, unresolved disputes, broken payment commitments, high-value exposures, and nonresponsive accounts in accordance with established protocols.
  • Support month-end, quarter-end, and year-end collection activities by providing accurate account status, payment forecasts, risk assessments, and supporting documentation.
  • Contribute to collection performance measures such as past-due receivables, aging distribution, promise-to-pay performance, dispute cycle time, collection effectiveness, cash forecast accuracy, and days sales outstanding.
  • Follow applicable financial controls, authorization requirements, records-retention standards, customer confidentiality expectations, and company policies.
  • Use approved generative AI or intelligent automation capabilities to prepare initial drafts of collection communications, account summaries, follow-up notes, and escalation documentation, ensuring all outputs are reviewed for accuracy, appropriateness, confidentiality, and compliance before use.
  • Identify inaccurate recommendations, data-quality issues, unusual patterns, and workflow exceptions generated by automated collections processes and route them to the appropriate process, technology, or data owner for correction.
  • Other duties as assigned.
  • Job description subject to change at any time.
