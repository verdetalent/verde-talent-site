Position Specific Description Join the electrical maintenance team as we restart Duane Arnold Energy Center—a groundbreaking effort to return a decommissioned nuclear plant to service. This position is located in Palo, Iowa. Job Overview The Plant Duane Arnold (PDA) Electrician will be responsible for repairing, adjusting, rearranging, installing and maintaining assigned plant and substation electrical equipment. The Electrician will check, clean and calibrate equipment, controls, instruments and meters to ensure accuracy and reliability.
