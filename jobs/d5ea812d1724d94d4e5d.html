Full description

Can you design IT solutions that make HSE simple and compliant? Do you enjoy working with stakeholders to enable safe, data-driven operations? Are you motivated by influencing critical health and safety outcomes? Then apply now for the IT HSE Architect role at Vestas!

Digital Solutions > Supply Chain, Transport, Procurement, Sustainability and Quality > Architecture & Design

The Digital HSE Management product team works closely with Global and Regional HSE leadership to design and implement digital solutions that enable a risk-based, proactive approach to health, safety, and environmental management, moving from reactive incident handling to prevention and continuous improvement. The team consists of 13 architects who oversee different products that interface with the business.

We enable colleagues across the business to perform their work effectively without needing in-depth knowledge of HSE processes, by ensuring that our systems inherently support compliance with all relevant requirements. The team is part of the Digital Solutions organization within the "Supply Chain, Transport, Procurement, Quality, HSE & Sustainability" Product Area, which delivers digital capabilities that support safe, compliant, and efficient operations across Vestas. As a team, we play a key role in shaping and supporting critical areas related to Health and Safety, where our work has a direct and meaningful impact on the organization.

Responsibilities

As an IT HSE Architect you will be responsible for three overall areas:

Architecture and Solutions Design

  • Define and maintain the HSE target architecture across applications, data, and integrations, translating HSE business capabilities into scalable IT solutions and establishing architecture standards, patterns, and principles
  • Design and govern HSE data and integrations, including definition of data models, data flows, and interfaces (e.g., SAP CPI, identity management, external systems)
  • Enable data-driven insights by supporting analytics for incident trends, root cause analysis, and compliance monitoring
  • Maintain the HSE technology roadmap, ensure alignment with enterprise architecture standards and ITSM processes, and support decision-making through architecture reviews

Business Alignment

  • Partner with the Product Owner and HSE leaders to convert strategy into digital capabilities
  • Ensure IT solutions support operational safety, compliance, and reporting processes
  • Help drive alignment between global, regional, and site-level HSE needs

Delivery Support

  • Guide the product team during implementation
  • Validate solution designs and ensure alignment with target architecture
  • Support incident/problem management from an architecture perspective

Qualifications

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in IT, Software Engineering, or related field
  • 5+ years in IT architecture or solution design
  • Experience with HSE digital platforms such as Sphera or similar HSE management tools.
  • Experience with enterprise platforms such as:
  • SAP (S/4HANA, SuccessFactors)
  • Microsoft Azure / Microsoft 365 ecosystem
  • Experience supporting regulated or safety-critical environments

Competencies

  • Profound stakeholder engagement and management across backgrounds and seniority levels
  • Ability to translate business needs into technical architecture
  • Structured thinking and problem-solving
  • Communication skills for executive and technical audiences
  • Understanding of trade-offs, scalability, and maintainability

In return, we provide a supportive, global culture, ongoing development opportunities, and attractive benefits to help you build a strong and lasting career with us.

About Vestas

Vestas is the energy industry’s global partner on sustainable energy solutions. We are specialised in designing, manufacturing, installing, and servicing wind turbines, both onshore and offshore.

Across the globe, we have installed more wind power than anyone else. We consider ourselves pioneers within the industry, as we continuously aim to design new solutions and technologies to create a more sustainable future for all of us. With more than 185 GW of wind power installed worldwide and 40+ years of experience in wind energy, we have an unmatched track record demonstrating our expertise within the field.

With 30,000 employees globally, we are a diverse team united by a common goal: to power the solution – today, tomorrow, and far into the future.
