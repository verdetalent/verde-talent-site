Full description

As a Systems Developer in Industrial Technology Platform, you will bring your skills in software development and application design into play through technical design, implementation of new capabilities, and the ongoing maturing and maintenance of existing platforms and services. You will work closely with architects, developers, and stakeholders to deliver high-quality software solutions that support the organization's digital transformation and operational excellence.

Infrastructure Services > Industrial Technology Platform > Industrial Engineering and Support

Responsibilities:

  • One of your primary focus areas will be the continued development of our next-generation platform based on: Frontend: Vue.js, TypeScript, and Python & Backend: FastAPI, Kafka, ClickHouse, and related technologies
  • You will contribute to designing, building, testing, deploying, and maintaining scalable, reliable, and secure applications and services.
  • You will help improve software quality through automated testing, CI/CD practices, code reviews, and engineering best practices.
  • You will also support our existing platform based on: Backend: Laravel, PHP, Gearman, Redis, MariaDB, and related technologies
  • While the legacy platform will be gradually replaced over the next several years, ongoing maintenance, support, troubleshooting, and selective enhancements will remain an important responsibility.
  • You will collaborate closely with architects and fellow developers to ensure technical solutions align with architectural standards and business requirements

Qualifications:

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Software Development, Engineering, Computer Science, Information Technology, or related fields.
  • 5–7 years of experience in software development.
  • Software development certifications in relevant technologies, combined with practical experience, may serve as an alternative or supplement to formal education.
  • Experience as a full-stack developer.
  • Experience as a frontend or backend developer combined with network engineering skills is also valuable.
  • Practical experience with: Vue.js, Python,TypeScript, and FastAPI
  • Experience working with modern software development practices, including version control, testing, and agile methodologies

Beneficial Experience:

Experience with one or more of the following technologies and practices:

  • Rust
  • OpenAPI
  • CI/CD pipelines (GitHub)
  • ClickHouse
  • Kafka
  • Kubernetes
  • Docker
  • Git
  • VS Code
  • Scrum
  • Jira
  • Confluence
  • Agentic development tools and AI-assisted development practices

Competencies:

  • You are a supportive colleague, willing to learn, share knowledge, support colleagues, and contribute to team goals.
  • You demonstrate a professional attitude in your work, communication, and interactions with stakeholders and colleagues.
  • You are structured, systematic, and responsible for delivering high-quality solutions.
  • You actively seek opportunities to automate, standardize, and improve development processes.
  • You put great pride into building stable, secure, maintainable, and high-quality products.
  • You are comfortable working across the software development lifecycle, including analysis, design, implementation, testing, debugging, problem-solving, and documentation.
  • You possess solid analytical skills and skill at finding and fixing technical problems and enjoy solving complex technical challenges.
  • You value continuous learning and keep yourself updated on relevant technologies, tools, and engineering practices engineering practices.

What we offer:

  • Work on a globally distributed, high-scale platform
  • Help shape architecture and engineering direction
  • Be part of a fast-growing industrial technology team
  • Clear focus on innovation, automation, and modern engineering practices

About Vestas

Vestas is the energy industry’s global partner on sustainable energy solutions. We are specialised in designing, manufacturing, installing, and servicing wind turbines, both onshore and offshore.

Across the globe, we have installed more wind power than anyone else. We consider ourselves pioneers within the industry, as we continuously aim to design new solutions and technologies to create a more sustainable future for all of us. With more than 185 GW of wind power installed worldwide and 40+ years of experience in wind energy, we have an unmatched track record demonstrating our expertise within the field.

With 30,000 employees globally, we are a diverse team united by a common goal: to power the solution – today, tomorrow, and far into the future.

Vestas promotes a diverse workforce which embraces all social identities and is free of any discrimination. We commit to create and sustain an environment that acknowledges and harvests different experiences, skills, and perspectives. We also aim to give everyone equal access to opportunity.
