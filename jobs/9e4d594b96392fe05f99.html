Full description

Are you passionate about shaping modern cloud and AI-enabled architectures that enable commercial excellence? Do you enjoy connecting business strategy, enterprise platforms, data, and emerging technologies to create scalable digital capabilities?

Digital Solutions & Development > Digital Solutions> Product Architects

At Vestas, we envision a future where digital solutions act as a catalyst for growth, efficiency, and sustainability. In Digital Solutions, we work in a product‑led, agile setup to continuously evolve digital capabilities that support commercial and customer‑facing operations. We are looking for a Cloud & AI Architect to help define and evolve our Commercial and Contract Lifecycle Management architecture. In this role, you will leverage robust Core Enterprise Architecture built on SAP, Salesforce, and Snowflake while designing the Core Commercial Architecture supporting customer engagement, opportunity development, sales execution, and contract lifecycle management. Working closely with Product Architects, Senior Architects, Lead engineers and Enterprise Architects, you will help create the architectural foundations that enable scalable business capabilities, trusted data, seamless integrations, and AI-powered innovation.

Responsibilities

Your focus will be on enabling end-to-end commercial capabilities through modern cloud, data, integration, and AI architecture.

  • Design and evolve the Core Commercial Architecture across commercial and contract lifecycle capabilities.
  • Leverage core platforms (SAP, Salesforce, Snowflake) in combination with Microsoft Azure cloud services to enable product teams to craft valuable user centric products
  • Define architecture principles, standards, and guardrails across applications, integrations, data, and AI solutions.
  • Enable adoption of AI, MCP, and modern integration patterns that securely connect systems, data, and intelligent agents
  • Promote API-first, event-driven, and cloud-native architectures
  • Support modernization, simplification, reuse and composability across the commercial technology landscape

Qualifications

We believe you are an experienced architect with the following profile:

  • Degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, or a related field
  • Significant experience in Product Architecture, Solution Architecture, Cloud Architecture, or Enterprise Architecture
  • Experience supporting commercial, sales, customer engagement, and or contract lifecycle management capabilities
  • Solid understanding of cloud platforms, enterprise applications, integrations, and data architecture
  • Practical knowledge of enterprise platforms such as Salesforce, SAP, Snowflake, and Legal AI solutions, combined with a solid understanding of cloud services including Microsoft Azure and or AWS
  • Knowledge of AI architecture patterns, MCP, and enterprise AI enablement

Competencies

To thrive in this role, it is important that you bring/have:

  • Well-developed stakeholder management and communication skills
  • Ability to balance strategic thinking with pragmatic execution and gaining buy-in without direct authority
  • Collaborative and people-oriented
  • A skilled systems thinker
  • Comfortable navigating complex business and technology landscapes
  • Business-focused with a focus on technology and innovation
  • Curious about cloud, data, and AI technologies

What we offer

You will join a collaborative architecture community where Product Architects, Senior Architects, and Enterprise Architects work together to build the digital landscape. This is an opportunity to influence how Commercial and Contract Lifecycle Management capabilities evolve through cloud-native platforms, trusted data foundations, enterprise-scale integrations, and AI-enabled solutions built on a strong SAP, Salesforce, and Snowflake foundation.

Additional information

Your work location will be Copenhagen, Denmark, where other team members and stakeholders are located. You are expected to work in-office at least 3 days per week. You should expect approximately 10-20 annual travel days in this role.

About Vestas
