Quality Inspector I The Quality Inspector is responsible for testing and inspecting products at various stages of the production process. This role evaluates and compiles inspection and quality data to support decisions that ensure the quality, reliability, and compliance of nacelles, hubs, and drivetrain (DT) components. Responsibilities: This role supports factory quality performance by identifying defects, reducing non‑conformities, and ensuring products meet specifications before release, shipment, or storage. The position works closely with Production, Technical Support, and Quality Engineering to drive improvements in DPU and FPY while maintaining strict adherence to quality processes. Identify and analyze defects impacting Defects Per Unit (DPU) and First Pass Yield (FPY) metrics; collaborate with Production and Technical Support to resolve issues. Inspect materials and products against specifications and known defect criteria. Initiate and document Non‑Conformity (NC) reports for defects and deviations. Provide independent quality approvals for processes not directly involved in personal assembly work. Case‑handle assigned NCs and support factory personnel through initiation and containment. Perform containment actions on non‑conforming parts and products. Coordinate part disposition including scrap or internal rework, with Quality Engineer support. Facilitate non‑standard work events to bring products back into conformance. Coordinate and track rework activities for NC material in production or inventory. Support production lines by identifying potential quality issues and interpreting specifications. Facilitate Form, Fit, and Function (FFF) testing and document results. Verify that all product and process requirements are met prior to release for storage, including: Reviewing inspection journals in QDA Ensuring required process documentation is complete Stamp approvals or rejections in QDA based on inspection results.
