Full description

Are you interested and skilled in electricity, mechanics, and hydraulics? Are you motivated by renewable energy and prefer a physical job in the field? As a Service Technician at Vestas, you'll play a key role in advancing sustainable practices.

Region MED > SBU MED Service > Buzau

If you are looking for a new challenge in which you can contribute to strategy and development of a company and are motivated by renewable energy, then take a closer look at Vestas. Service Vestas Eastern Europe (part of Vestas Mediterranean) is a Sales Business Unit covering Eastern Europe countries including Romania. We are responsible for the sales, installation and service of wind farms throughout these regions. We are the Nº 1 performing business unit within Vestas and aim to make Vestas Mediterranean the best place to work.

Responsibilities

  • Conducting in working safely and with an attention to detail; promotes and always demonstrates safe behavior and practices
  • Performing all mechanical, hydraulic, and electrical component maintenance, repair or replacement of parts to correct malfunctions
  • Secures and monitors the clean and tidy condition of the turbine after job completion
  • Fills out work orders and check list, confirms completed orders including proposals for an optimization of processes
  • Checks tools and spare parts for accuracy, operational capability and completeness prior to starting work
  • Responsible for the equipment of the vehicles with tools, personal protective equipment and spare parts
  • Ensuring on time return of unused and defective materials and tools with correct declaration and correct packaging

Qualifications

  • High school diploma or technical training in the field of electro-technology or mechanics
  • At least 2 years of professional experience
  • Basic knowledge in working with MS-Office
  • Driving license in class B+E

Competencies

  • Good knowledge of written and spoken English
  • High degree of physical flexibility
  • Team working and operational readiness
  • Readiness for travel activities
  • Ability to work at heights

What we offer

We are a diverse group of professionals motivated by our work in wind energy. You will have the opportunity developing a career within a very exciting industry in a global company.

About Vestas

Vestas is the energy industry’s global partner on sustainable energy solutions. We are specialised in designing, manufacturing, installing, and servicing wind turbines, both onshore and offshore.
