Job Description:

Role Overview

The Procurement & Subcontract Specialist at Fluence is a mid-career professional responsible for driving sourcing and subcontracting activities across the sales and pre-execution phases of energy storage projects. With a strong foundation in procurement, this role owns end-to-end sourcing packages, contributing to competitive proposal development, cost accuracy, and supplier strategy.

Working closely with cross-functional teams, the specialist supports pricing development, supplier selection, and procurement assumptions, while ensuring alignment with regional and project requirements. This role also plays a key part in developing supplier relationships and driving efficient, scalable procurement practices.

Key Responsibilities

  • Own and deliver end-to-end sourcing packages in support of project proposals, ensuring timely and high-quality execution
  • Lead sourcing activities for EPC, BOP, and key equipment packages, including RFQ preparation, bid analysis, and supplier selection
  • Influence proposal competitiveness by driving cost accuracy, supplier strategy, and procurement assumptions
  • Manage RFQ processes end-to-end, including scope definition, commercial evaluation, and supplier recommendations
  • Support subcontract negotiations, with progression toward independently leading commercial and contractual discussions
  • Collaborate closely with Sales to support pricing development and proposal submissions
  • Partner with Engineering to ensure supplier proposals align with technical and project requirements
  • Contribute to pricing inputs (SOG) and procurement strategies for proposals and pipeline opportunities
  • Support supplier development, qualification, and relationship management aligned with regional procurement objectives
  • Maintain procurement data, dashboards, and reporting accuracy using SAP and other tools
  • Ensure compliance with Fluence procurement policies, procedures, and governance standards
  • Support demand planning activities through the issuance of purchase orders and letters of intent, as required

Required Qualifications & Experience

  • 4–6 years of experience in procurement, sourcing, or subcontract management within industrial, energy, or engineering environments
  • Proven experience managing RFQs, supplier selection, and sourcing activities end-to-end
  • Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Business Administration, Supply Chain, or a related field
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a structured, detail-oriented approach
  • Excellent communication skills with the ability to engage and influence internal stakeholders and external suppliers

Preferred Qualifications & Experience

  • Experience in energy storage, renewable energy, construction, or BESS projects
  • Exposure to EPC or BOP subcontracting environments
  • Experience with SAP S/4HANA or similar ERP systems
  • Experience supporting pricing, cost estimation, or proposal development activities

Additional Skills

  • Ownership & Accountability: Takes responsibility for delivering sourcing and subcontracting activities independently and to a high standard
  • Collaboration & Influence: Builds strong, effective relationships across cross-functional teams and with external partners
  • Problem Solving: Proactively identifies and resolves procurement and supplier challenges with practical, outcome-driven solutions
  • Resilience: Thrives in a fast-paced, dynamic project environment and maintains composure under pressure
  • Negotiation: Supports and develops capability to lead commercial discussions and contract negotiations
  • Relationship Management: Establishes and maintains productive, long-term supplier and stakeholder partnerships
  • Initiative: Proactively drives sourcing activities and identifies opportunities for improvement
  • Data-Driven: Leverages data and insights to support decision-making and optimize outcomes
  • Time Management: Effectively prioritizes and manages multiple deadlines in a project-driven environment
