By securing essential services efficiently, you will help us achieve our goals of enhancing our presence and improving customer engagement. Your role will foster cross-departmental collaboration and build strong partnerships with external suppliers, upholding the values that drive our success. Drive the phases of a sourcing strategy, including identifying requirements, stakeholder alignment, supplier benchmarking, specifications, supply strategy/method RFx, executing the sourcing process, and formalizing contract agreements.
