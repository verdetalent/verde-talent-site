General Description Responsible for the proper installation, use, and application of equipment utilized in the plant, with a continuous focus on quality, safety, cost, and productivity. Responsibilities Develop and execute preventive and corrective maintenance for production equipment. Establish maintenance frequencies/schedules Provide direct support in equipment selection, installation, and startup/commissioning. Develop technical training programs for building maintenance personnel. Continuously monitor and supervise critical equipment. Perform failure analysis and diagnose problems. Implement automation projects. Analyze performance indicator reports, including: Downtime Preventive maintenance Corrective maintenance Equipment failures Costs Support other service departments in failure analysis. Comply with and enforce all provisions of the TPI Internal Work Regulations. Promote and ensure compliance with TPI’s Mission, Vision, and Values. Maintain a customer-focused approach through risk-based thinking, supporting and continuously improving Management Systems processes (ISO 9001, ISO 14001, and ISO 45001). Qualifications Bachelor’s Degree in Engineering, such as: Mechatronics Engineering Electromechanical Engineering Electronics Engineering Minimum of 3 years of maintenance experience , including: Maintenance of heating and cooling systems. Compressor maintenance. Dust collection system maintenance. Maintenance of high- and low-voltage electrical installations. Maintenance of overhead cranes and mobile equipment. Conversational-level English proficiency. Competencies: Results-oriented. Leadership. Equipment failure analysis and problem diagnosis. Motivated mindset. Effective communication. Ability to read electrical diagrams and blueprints. Emergency response capability. Our commitment to a fair hiring.
