Journeyman Lineman Serviceman - Amarillo, TX

Xcel Energy
Amarillo, TX Full-time Individual Contributor $53.16 per hour
Applying takes you to Xcel Energy's own careers site to complete your application.
Full description

Are you looking for an exciting job where you can put your skills and talents to work at a company you can feel proud to be a part of? Do you want a workplace that will challenge you and offer you opportunities to learn and grow?  A  position at Xcel Energy could be just what you’re looking for.

Xcel Energy is looking for an experienced Distribution Journeyman Lineman Serviceman for the Amarillo, TX location!

Summary

The person filling this position must be self-motivated, work well with others and possess a positive attitude and commitment to work safely, provide quality customer service, create satisfaction and to work productively. In this challenging and rewarding position, the successful candidate must have a well-rounded knowledge of distribution primary feeders, transformer loading and fusing practices. The ideal candidate is thoroughly familiar with the safety regulations of the Company and must be well acquainted with the hazards involved in distribution service work.  This role will perform a variety of lineman duties that involve overhead and underground electric distribution and transmission construction, operations and maintenance activities. Primary responsibilities consist of but are not limited to: installation, maintenance and removal of overhead and underground distribution conductors and equipment. Must be able to climb poles, work on energized conductors and equipment, perform hot stick work on energized high voltage conductors, work in inclement weather, be available for callout work and all other work as outlined in the Journeyman Serviceman position.

Requirements:

  • Must successfully score on the Journeyman Assessment
  • To be considered at the Journeyman level, must possess a Journeyman Lineman Certification that is approved by the Department of Labor.
  • Must have a Class A CDL
  • Must be capable of performing all aspects of electric distribution overhead, underground, construction, maintenance, and troubleshooting.
  • Must be available for emergency call-outs as per a schedule.
  • Must have effective communication skills (verbal and written).
  • Position could possibly require travel for extended time periods
  • Must be physically fit and able to lift objects up to 90 pounds, pass a physical examination and be able to perform strenuous physical labor under all types of conditions.
  • Experience with bucket trucks, digger derricks, trenchers and groundwork with utility line crew required
  • A high awareness of safety and compliance with company safety rules is required at all times
  • Must reside within 20 miles of the Amarillo, TX service center.
  • Selection criteria may include testing and/or assessment.
  • Must pass the Xcel Energy security and drug screening

Preferred Requirement

Must have a well-rounded knowledge of distribution primary feeders, transformer loading and fusing practices. This includes, but is not limited to, meter sets and their connections, the use of hot line tools, protective devices, fuses, lightning arresters, disconnect switches, breakers, grounding sets, transformers and their connections, and troubleshooting.
