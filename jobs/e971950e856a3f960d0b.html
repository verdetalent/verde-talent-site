Requisition ID: 96307 NextEra Analytics offers energy consulting services using industry-leading scientific analysis for planning, siting, forecasting and optimizing all forms of energy projects. Our optimization and analytics platforms integrate open-source technologies to leverage massive, diverse sets of utility operating data. This enables rapid development of operational solutions. Applying expertise in advanced mathematics, data and physical sciences, we solve some of the hardest problems facing the energy industry. Position Specific Description NextEra Analytics is the optimization and analytics team for the world’s largest renewable energy generator. We thrive on solving the toughest problems facing the energy industry and creating innovative forecasting and optimization solutions that lead the way into the next era of energy. This position is responsible for providing product ownership and vision in planning and overseeing the successful execution of various small to medium-sized projects throughout the project life cycle. Individuals will provide project guidance, prioritization, and coordination with various internal teams such as Applied Math, Science, and Engineering & Technology to ensure timely and effective project execution within the Agile development framework. Employees in this role commonly provide status updates and presentations to varying levels of management and collaborate with other business units to ensure projects are not only effective but completed on time and on-budget. The role also includes direct people leadership responsibilities for a team of junior Product Managers, including hiring support, onboarding, mentoring, performance management, professional development, resource planning, and ensuring alignment with product strategy and organizational objectives while fostering a collaborative, high-performing product organization.
