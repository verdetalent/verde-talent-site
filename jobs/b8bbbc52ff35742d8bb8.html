Full description
As part of Vestas Global Finance Shared Service Centre, the team is primarily responsible in providing standardized, rule-based services to the region. This refers to the end-to-end processes of Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable and Travel & Expenses. Works closely with Regional Finance, Treasury and other SSC functional areas to ensure issues and concerns are addressed positively and in accordance within prescribed deadlines. Ensure timely, accurate, and complete billing and invoice distribution in line with contractual terms, local regulations, and global policies. Monitor and resolve billing discrepancies, including pricing issues, master data errors, and system-related failures. Oversee and consolidate non-standard or non-migrated AR billing processes. Support standardization for Global Billing Process Collaborate closely with the relevant stakeholders to resolve issues efficiently. Identify recurring issues and conduct root cause analysis and preventive actions. Support or lead AR projects, process improvements, and automation initiatives. Provide audit support (internal and external), including documentation and walkthroughs. Perform analytical reviews and reporting to ensure KPI targets are met. Consolidate monthly operational decks. Ensure adherence to AR task lists, SLAs, and internal controls framework. Maintain and update Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) as needed.
