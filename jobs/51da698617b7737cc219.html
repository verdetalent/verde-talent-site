At PALMS Insurance, a NextEra Energy company, we specialize in offering customized insurance solutions that address the unique needs of our clients. Our commitment to excellence, reliability, and customer satisfaction sets us apart as a trusted provider in the insurance industry. Are you ready to make a meaningful difference? Position Specific Description The ideal candidate for this position will be able to handle and adjust assumed reinsurance claims that may be complex in nature on behalf of Palms Insurance Company, Palms Specialty Insurance Company and Sabal Specialty Insurance Company. It is expected that the Reinsurance Claims Account Manager will be responsible for the following: Assumed Reinsurance Claims Management & Processing Review and validate assumed reinsurance claims to ensure accuracy and adherence to contract terms and conditions. Coordinate the submission, tracking, and resolution of reinsurance recoveries, including facultative and treaty claims. Analyze claim data and documentation to confirm reinsurance applicability and recovery potential. Maintain accurate records of outstanding recoverable and settlements in the claims management system. Account Management & Communication Act as the primary point of contact for brokers, reinsurers, and internal stakeholders regarding reinsurance claims. Collaborate with underwriting, finance, and legal teams to resolve discrepancies or disputes. Manage and reconcile claim statements and bordereaux with counterparties. Facilitate regular status updates, claim summaries, and audit support for reinsurance partners. Analysis & Reporting. Prepare reports on claims activity, recovery performance, and outstanding balances. Support financial close processes through timely and accurate reinsurance claims data.
