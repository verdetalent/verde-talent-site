Full description

Our team at Bloom Energy embraces the unprecedented opportunity to change the way companies utilize energy. Our technology empowers businesses and communities to responsibly take charge of their energy. Our energy platform has three key value propositions: resiliency, sustainability, and predictability. We provide infrastructure that is flexible for the evolving net zero ecosystem. We have deployed more than 30,000 fuel cell modules since our first commercial shipments in 2009, sending energy platforms to data centers, hospitals, manufacturing facilities, biotechnology facilities, major retail stores, financial institutions, telecom facilities, utilities, and other critical infrastructure customers around the world.

Our mission is to make clean, reliable energy affordable globally. We never stop striving to improve our technology, to expand and improve our company performance, and to develop and support the many talented employees that serve our mission!

We are looking for a Field Service Apprentice -Fixed Termto join our team in one of today’s most exciting technologies. This role will report toField Service Regional Managerand will be based in Santa Teresa, New Mexico.

Role and Responsibilities:

  • Fixed term for approximately 6 months or approximately 1,200 hours from June 15th, 2026, through December 15th, 2026.
  • Learn to become a primary driver for maintaining Bloom Energy servers, ensuring optimal performance and reliability.
  • Learn and perform fuel cell system maintenance, site upkeep, safety protocols, and issue resolution.
  • Demonstrate proficiency in working independently and collaboratively within a dynamic, fast-paced setting.
  • We will train you to gain a comprehensive understanding of diverse electrical and mechanical systems, enabling effective troubleshooting and problem-solving.

Skills and Experience:

  • Enrolled in an electrical apprenticeship program
  • We are looking for someone with strong mechanical and troubleshooting aptitude. Our training program will help you understand fuel cell mechanical systems, piping, plumbing and valve operation
  • This job will expose you to 3phase systems, UPS (back-up power) systems, electrical and mechanical maintenance, electrical generation and distribution systems, power electronics, Automatic Transfer Switch Systems, and microprocessor-based control platforms.
  • Experience with various test and measurement equipment, electrical codes and personnel safety products and procedures is preferred.
  • You will learn about monitoring systems in operation
  • Basic understanding of requirements of working safely
  • Understanding of basic troubleshooting techniques and skills
  • Highly motivated and able to work independently
  • Demonstrates sound judgment
  • A team player who exhibits a high level of integrity and professionalism in all situations
  • Able to communicate effectively in English in both written and verbal form to facilitate duties as required
  • Able to follow procedures and execute sequenced work instructions from either written and/or verbal communications
  • Able to facilitate shift work as needed
  • Able to travel as needed for training

Physical Requirements :

  • Ability to lift 45-50lbs
  • Ability to work with heights
  • Ability to work in outdoor weather conditions
