Full description

Do you want to make an impact in a global, purpose-driven organization within renewable energy?

We are looking for a Senior Manager in Global Procurement to lead a key electrical procurement area—combining people leadership, strategic supplier management, and global collaboration.

Join a dynamic environment where you will shape supplier strategies, lead an international team, and drive high-impact procurement decisions.

Vestas Technology & Operations > Global Procurement > Direct Material Procurement > Electrical (Power Solution)

Vestas Technology & Operations (VTO) is the integrated CTO and COO organization within Vestas, encompassing the full industrial value chain — from Research & Development, Procurement and Manufacturing to Supply Chain and Quality. VTO’s mission is to transform the way Vestas engineers, sources, contracts, manufactures and delivers its products to consistently meet the highest standards for quality, cost efficiency and time-to-market.

Vestas Global Procurement is responsible for overseeing all procurement activities across the organization. This includes leading cross-functional category teams, driving negotiations and contract management, developing end-to-end cost optimization initiatives, shaping category strategies, and fostering strong supplier relationships.

Within this function, we cultivate a collaborative and inclusive environment where respect, accountability, and mutual support are core values—enabling our teams to stay focused, committed, and motivated.

As a Senior Manager Procurement, you will lead a highly ambitious and globally distributed team. You will be overall responsible for activities and engagement with key strategic partners and suppliers of critical electrical equipment, such as converters and transformers, ensuring supplier performance and long-term value creation.

In this role, you will also be an integral member of the Electrical Management Team, contributing to the development and execution of procurement strategies. You will work closely with cross-functional stakeholders, including the solution core team, to drive the strategic direction for the cross functional business deliverables, business priorities, and strengthen Vestas’ position in the market.

Responsibilities

In this role, you will operate in a dynamic and fast-paced environment, balancing short-term execution with long-term strategic direction while leading multiple high-impact initiatives.

Your key responsibilities will include:

  • Define and drive the long-term strategic vision and direction for the Electrical procurement area
  • Lead and inspire a global team of 8 direct reports, ensuring clear direction, performance, and alignment with business objectives
  • Foster both personal and professional growth by coaching, mentoring, and developing team members to reach their full potential
  • Build and maintain close, value-driven relationships with key strategic suppliers, focusing on long-term partnerships and performance optimization
  • Shape and develop the future supply base in close collaboration with commodity teams and key stakeholders
  • Drive alignment across senior leadership (including C-level) and cross-functional teams to ensure effective strategy execution and cohesive supplier engagement

Qualifications

As a Senior Manager, you are expected to lead by example, challenge established ways of working and drive continuous improvement across the organization.

To succeed in this role, you bring:

  • Minimum of 7 years’ experience in strategic procurement, with a strong track record of driving value through sourcing and supplier management
  • Proven experience in leading and developing high-performing teams in a global or matrix organization
  • Bachelor’s degree in Business, Supply Chain Management, Engineering, or a related field
  • Demonstrated experience managing large-scale procurement budgets
  • Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Office tools, particularly Excel and PowerPoint, with the ability to analyze data and present insights effectively
  • Strong track record of engaging and influencing senior stakeholders, including C-level leadership, with excellent communication and relationship-building skills
  • Fluency in English, both written and spoken

Competencies

We are looking for a driven and curious leader who challenges the status quo and brings energy, structure, and collaboration to everything they do.

To succeed in this role, you demonstrate:

  • A proactive mindset with strong ambition, intellectual curiosity, and a continuous desire to learn and improve
  • An inclusive approach that encourages stakeholder input, while effectively prioritizing and implementing ideas through structured, fact-based decision-making
  • The ability to maintain a big-picture perspective, balancing long-term strategy with immediate business needs
  • A structured, data-driven approach to solving complex challenges and driving informed decisions
  • A confident and inspiring leader who is able to engage, motivate, and align diverse teams, while driving results through influence and collaboration
  • A forward-looking mindset with the ability to identify optimization opportunities and unlock value across the organization
  • Excellent communication skills with a proven ability to build trust, manage relationships, and influence stakeholders at all levels

What we offer

We offer an exciting opportunity to join Electrical Global Procurement within a flat, multicultural, and highly innovative organization focused on delivering outstanding results.

At Vestas, success is driven by a deep understanding of our business and our people, combined with the ability to design the right solutions and execute them effectively. We invite you to be part of this mission—while enjoying the journey along the way.

As part of our team, you will have strong opportunities for both professional and personal growth. You will further develop your expertise within procurement, working closely with highly experienced and inspiring colleagues across a global network.

About Vestas

Vestas is the energy industry’s global partner on sustainable energy solutions. We are specialised in designing, manufacturing, installing, and servicing wind turbines, both onshore and offshore.

Across the globe, we have installed more wind power than anyone else. We consider ourselves pioneers within the industry, as we continuously aim to design new solutions and technologies to create a more sustainable future for all of us. With more than 200 GW of wind power installed worldwide and 40+ years of experience in wind energy, we have an unmatched track record demonstrating our expertise within the field.

With 39,000 employees globally, we are a diverse team united by a common goal: to power the solution – today, tomorrow, and far into the future.

Vestas promotes a diverse workforce which embraces all social identities and is free of any discrimination. We commit to create and sustain an environment that acknowledges and harvests different experiences, skills, and perspectives. We also aim to give everyone equal access to opportunity.
