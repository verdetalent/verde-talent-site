Craft Attendant (Jointing) Location: Galashiels (with travel to sites across the Edinburgh & Borders District) Salary: From £30,150 plus excellent benefits Permanent / Full‑time – 37 hours per week Closing Date: 23rd July 2026 Help us create a better future, quicker! At SP Energy Networks, we're investing in the electricity network of the future, ensuring homes, businesses and communities continue to receive a safe, reliable and sustainable supply of power. We're looking for a Craft Attendant (Jointing) to join our Distribution team based in Galashiels supporting activities across our Edinburgh & Borders district. This is a fantastic opportunity for someone who enjoys working outdoors, thrives in a hands-on environment and wants to build a long-term career within the energy industry. Working alongside experienced Cable Jointers, you'll play an important role in delivering essential network operations, supporting maintenance, repairs and new connections that help keep the electricity network running safely and efficiently. What you'll be doing As a Craft Attendant, you'll provide vital support to our Jointing teams across a variety of operational and project activities. Your responsibilities will include: Assisting experienced Cable Jointers with underground cable installation, maintenance and repair activities. Supporting the safe and efficient delivery of work on the electricity distribution network. Undertaking site preparation activities, including excavation, reinstatement and general groundwork duties. Acting as a Safety Attendant in accordance with training, competence and authorisation levels. Ensuring all work is completed safely and in line with company policies, procedures and health and safety requirements. Assisting with the handling, transportation and organisation of tools, materials and equipment. Supporting the setup and maintenance of safe work areas on site.
