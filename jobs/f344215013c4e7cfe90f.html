Do you bring solid experience in finance and transformation initiatives? Are you motivated by roles with high business impact and exposure to senior leadership? Do you thrive in leading complex, high-stakes projects in a global organisation? Then you might be our new Finance Transformation Senior Project Manager. Finance> Finance > Finance Join us on an exciting Finance transformation journey where we are building a modern, globally integrated Finance function. We are creating a solid, standardized backbone supported by a global operating model, enabling consistency, efficiency, and scalability across the business. At the same time, we are elevating the role of Finance by improving business partnering through better data, standardized reporting, and enhanced capabilities. As we reinforce planning and forecasting to support long-term strategy, you will be part of a multi-year transformation that reshapes structures, roles, and ways of working, positioning Finance as a trusted, strategic partner to leadership. This is a unique opportunity to play a key role in building the future of Finance in a fast-changing, global environment. The Finance Transformation Office The Finance Transformation Office will be a team of four professionals responsible for overseeing Vestas’ finance transformation agenda. We ensure that strategic finance initiatives are delivered in a structured, transparent, and value-focused manner. Acting as the central hub, the office enables alignment across Finance, Digital Solutions, and the wider business. We support change adoption and ensure that transformation initiatives deliver measurable business outcomes that align to the long-term direction and objectives of the organisation.
