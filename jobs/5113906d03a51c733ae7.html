Full description

First Solar reserves the right to offer you a role most applicable to your experience and skillset.

Basic Job Functions:

  • Lead CdTe photovoltaic absorber research and development.
  • Develop and deploy new manufacturable technologies and processes to improve solar cell performance and reliability.
  • Lead technical team of engineers and technicians through stages of new technology development from basic feasibility and understanding through process development, definition, and implementation.

Education/Experience:

  • Bachelors in material science, physics, or similar technical discipline.
  • Master’s or Ph.D. is preferred.
  • Minimum 5 years’ experience (3 years’ experience if Masters or 2 years’ experience if Ph.D.) in a Device Development, Process Integration, or Process Development.
  • Semiconductor / Thin Film / Electronics Related Industry Experience.
  • Experience with Thin Film Photovoltaics preferred.
  • People and Project Management experience.
  • Experience in Semiconductor Industry or PV Technology Industry in the following areas is strongly preferred:
  • Thin Film Deposition Processes (vacuum deposition), Thermal Processes & Process Control.
  • Device Integration and characterization.
  • Material Characterization.

Required Skills/Competencies:

  • Proven history of innovation in developing new technologies and processes for high-performance solar cells.
  • Ability to develop, plan and manage complex and multidisciplinary R&D projects, assess technological risk, scopes, and timelines.
  • Experience in thin film metrology development, device characterization and test equipment development with sufficient understanding of optical, electrical, and opto-electronic properties of photovoltaic devices.
  • Fundamental understanding of material and device characterization as well as failure analysis and root cause understanding.
  • Experience in thin film deposition methods and processes.
  • Advanced understanding of process equipment, engineering techniques and practices.
  • Ability to handle complex data, summarize, and communicate results effectively.
  • Demonstrated ability to manage engineers, set-up goals and objectives and motivate others to accomplish desired results.
  • Ability to coordinate activities between various engineering and operations groups. Good verbal and written communication skills are required.
  • Proven competency in statistical process optimization and data analysis, including Design of Experiments (DOE), Analysis of Variance and Process Control implementation and optimization.
  • Experience using statistical software (JMP preferred).

Essential Responsibilities:

  • Direct or matrix manage a team of cross functional engineers. Define specific and quantifiable objectives for each individual team member as well as for the team.
  • Strong written and verbal communications skills to ensure effective co-operation of interdepartmental teams.
  • Responsible for effective communication between teams, foster team building, encourage creativity.
  • Effectively interface with other functional areas in support of development activities.
  • Ensure adherence to program goals and schedules.
  • Prioritize experiments and activities.
  • Integrate material and device characteristics and process data to determine the mechanisms and root causes of failures and resulting film and device properties.
  • Ensure adherence to all safety procedures and housekeeping standards.
  • Develop First Solar IP portfolio by documenting inventions and supporting First Solar IP personnel in writing intellectual property and patents.
  • Other duties as assigned.
  • Job description subject to change at any time.

Reporting Relationships:

  • Reports to Development Director.
  • Will have direct reports.

Travel:

  • 0% - 5% (On occasion/as needed for training, etc.)

Estimated Salary Range:

  • $131,200- $187,000

  • Required to use hands to grasp, lift, handle, carry or feel objects on a frequent basis.
  • 20/40 vision in both eyes together, with or without correction, is required.
  • Must be able to comply with all safety standards and procedures.
  • May reach above shoulder heights and below the waist on a frequent basis.
  • May stoop, kneel, or bend, on an occasional basis.
