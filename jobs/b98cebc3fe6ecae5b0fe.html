Full description
The Parts Sales Senior Manager is a critical member of Service Sales team. The individual in this role is expected to operate with a high degree of ownership and follow-through, managing strategic commercial initiatives, coordinating cross-functional teams, and ensuring consistent execution. The vision statement for the Service Sales team is to "cultivate a collaborative team that creatively partners with our colleagues and customers to positively impact communities and the environment." Our objectives are employee wellness, customer partnership, commercial creativity, and strategic execution. We are a collaborative team working to ensure Vestas continues to adapt to the dynamic challenges of the wind industry and seeks the opportunity to partner with customers to optimize their turbine assets across decades. We value innovation, strong working relationships, and constructive tension, and we believe in the inherent value of diversity in business. Responsibilities: The responsibility of the Parts Sales Senior Manager is to generate revenue and profit margin growth by managing a team of sales representatives and sales support team members who manage sales of Vestas and non-Vestas wind turbine parts to customers located within the US and Canada. The primary objective for the Parts Sales Senior Manager is to meet Vestas' sales objectives through sales management, customer engagement, proposal and quote development, and active selling.
