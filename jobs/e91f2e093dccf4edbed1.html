Job Description:

Role Overview

We are seeking a commercially sharp, fast-paced, and highly strategic Senior Manager of Sales for IPP Clients to lead our growth initiatives among Independent Power Producers and project developers across the UK and Ireland (UK & IE). In this role, you will be responsible for defining our IPP segment strategy, leading a talented team of sales managers, and driving transaction execution for some of the most dynamic energy storage projects in the region.

You will champion a Challenger Sales methodology tailored to the unique, rapid-fire nature of private developers and investors. Your primary focus will be on articulating how Fluence’s battery energy storage systems (BESS) and digital asset-optimization software maximize the lifetime value, merchant performance, and bankability of assets to meet strict margin and order intake targets.

Key Responsibilities

Strategic Leadership & IPP Segment Strategy

  • Define Segment Strategy: Develop and execute a targeted sales strategy for the UK & IE IPP and developer market, identifying high-value pipeline opportunities and positioning Fluence as the partner of choice for fast-track projects.
  • IPP-Focused Challenger Sales: Lead the sales team in applying the Challenger Sales methodology specifically calibrated to disrupt transactional, CAPEX-first buying behaviors common among developers:
  • Teach for Differentiation: Reframe the client’s focus from minimizing upfront CAPEX to maximizing lifetime Net Present Value (NPV) and project IRR. Educate IPPs on how operational metrics—such as superior Round-Trip Efficiency (RTE), optimized degradation curves, and advanced software-driven state-of-charge management—directly translate into higher merchant trading revenue and lower long-term OPEX.
  • Tailor for Resonance: Speak the distinct languages of both the entrepreneurial developer (focused on speed-to-market, grid connection security, and early-mover advantages) and their financial backers (focused on debt sizing, debt service coverage ratios, and meeting strict bankability criteria).
  • Take Control of the Dialogue: Steer commercial discussions toward standardization to compress project timelines. Confidently push back on highly customized, non-standard technical requests that delay deployment, demonstrating how standard Fluence configurations accelerate time-to-market and mitigate construction-phase risks.
  • Market & Grid Analysis: Stay ahead of UK & IE market dynamics, including connection queues, regulatory updates (e.g., Grid Code requirements), and ancillary services evolution, to position Fluence as a strategic advisor.

Client Engagement & Commercial Execution

  • Origination & Relationship Management: Cultivate deep, trust-based relationships with leading IPPs, private equity infrastructure funds, and joint-venture developers active in the UK & IE market.
  • Project Finance & Bankability Navigation: Lead structured, complex negotiations with IPP sponsors and their debt lenders, ensuring Fluence's commercial contracts, performance guarantees, and warranties satisfy strict project-finance criteria.
  • Optimizing the Revenue Stack: Partner with Fluence’s digital/software teams to sell integrated hardware-and-software solutions, showing how our algorithmic trading software enables IPPs to capture maximum volatility in the Balancing Mechanism and wholesale markets.

Team Management & Sales Enablement

  • Lead and Mentor: Manage, coach, and support a high-performing team of sales professionals dedicated to the IPP segment.
  • Build Agility: Equip the team with the tools, financial models, and pricing strategies needed to produce rapid, accurate, and highly competitive bids in response to fast-moving developer timelines.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Coordinate closely with engineering, legal, structured finance, and operations to deliver seamless, bankable project proposals under tight schedules.

Required Qualifications & Experience

  • 8+ years of B2B sales experience in the renewable energy, energy storage, or independent power generation sectors, with a proven history of closing utility-scale projects with IPPs or private developers.
  • High proficiency in project finance structures, including debt-sizing, leverage constraints, equity IRR calculations, and structured revenue agreements.
  • Mastery of the Challenger Sales model (or similar insight-led frameworks), specifically demonstrated in shifting transactional buyers toward long-term value and software-enabled solutions.
  • Deep knowledge of the UK & Ireland energy storage market, grid connection dynamics, and key active IPP developers and infrastructure investors.
  • Proven capability to lead, inspire, and develop a sales team within a fast-scaling, high-pressure business environment.
  • Clear understanding of battery energy storage technology, battery chemistry degradation, and digital energy management/trading software.
