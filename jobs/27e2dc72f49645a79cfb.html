This role is pivotal in advancing the company’s decarbonization goals while ensuring reliability, affordability, and regulatory compliance across multiple operating companies. The position will manage a multidisciplinary team and coordinate with internal and external stakeholders to deliver a large volume of high-impact energy infrastructure investments for a rapidly growing part of the business.
