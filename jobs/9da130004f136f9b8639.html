Full description
First Solar reserves the right to offer you a role most applicable to your experience and skillset. Basic Job Functions: Provide Quality Assurance support to manufacturing departments by performing quality audits of standard work, work in process, finished products, incoming parts, etc. Collect and analyze data for trends, process capability and machine reliability. Work with manufacturing supervisors and engineers to implement corrective actions, employee training and scrap reduction. Education/Experience: 5 years’ or more experience in a manufacturing environment. 2 years’ experience working with an ISO Quality System; including Internal Audits, use of SPC techniques, and JMP Data Analytics. Experience including ASQ Certification (CMI, CPT, CQA) desired. 2-year Technical Degree. 4-year B.S. degree highly desired. Required Skills/Competencies: Detail oriented. Self-motivated. Refer to "Operation/Production" competencies. Proven problem-solving skills, including use of basic quality tools (Pareto analysis, SPC, gage R & R, etc.). Experience with ISO 9001, including Internal Audit Procedures. Experience using micrometers, gage blocks and microscopes. Proven computer skills (Word and Excel required, Access a plus). Proven communications skills. Essential Responsibilities: Complete assignments and projects as directed, with minimal supervision. Inspect incoming materials and outgoing product and provides feedback to Engineering and Manufacturing for corrective and/or preventive action. Maintain Quality Control Procedures and records per Company Policy and ISO Standards. Complete internal audits per a defined schedule including a specified format for documentation of findings. Train personnel on various quality programs. Define and implement corrective/preventive actions and continuous improvement activities.
