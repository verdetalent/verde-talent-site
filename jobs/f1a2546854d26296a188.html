Full description

Vestas is the major leader in wind technology and a driving force in the development of the wind power industry. Vestas' core business comprises the development, manufacture, sale, marketing, and maintenance of Wind Turbines. Come and join us at Vestas!

Service Asia > Service Operation South > Tamilnadu

Customer Service Department handles Post-sales Service Operations to meet the contractual obligations with customers

Responsibilities

  • Follow the high level of safety culture and standards as per company requirements
  • Attend to break down maintenance on call; ensure WTG's and related equipment's are maintained in neat and technically acceptable conditions according to standard engineering practices
  • Document the work done indicating consumption of spares/ consumables, list further actions along with targeted completion dates
  • Report abnormalities and take corrective action in consultation with the Supervisors
  • Keep up to date with all operating/ maintenance manuals, checklists, service bulletins, etc. applicable to the WTG and associated equipment, carry out servicing activities as per the latest certified checklists, manuals and service bulletins
  • Ensure safety and care of all tools, tackles, measuring instruments and spare parts in custody
  • Comply with all HSE / ISO & all other statutory requirements
  • Ensure office and guest house are maintained in good condition and monitor the performance of security personnel/ operators, report non-compliances
  • Assist in the sub-assembly of WTG components at the factory premises whenever required
  • Rectification of Mechanical, Electrical and hydraulic Break Downs of the Wind Turbines
  • Analyzing the performance of the turbines and Preparing performance Reports
  • Major work like Blade, Gearbox, Generator and main shaft replacement added advantage
  • Experience in Unit substation maintenance and rectification of Unit sub-station related breakdown added advantage
  • Knowledge in SAP S/4HANA added advantage
  • Knowledge in basics of Computer, MS-Excel, MS-Word and Power BI

Qualifications

  • Diploma or Degree - Mechanical, Electrical or Electronics
  • Minimum 3 to 6 years working experience in Wind turbine Generator
  • Good communication in English / Tamil is must and Hindi is additional advantage

Competencies

  • Willingness to promote Vestas commitment to diversity, equity, inclusion and belonging
  • Ability to read, comprehend and write in English
  • Ability to climb stairs and ladders 60-150 meters in height
  • Ability to effectively participate in all training courses, including high-angle rescue
  • Able to work in demanding physical and inclement weather conditions
  • Comfortable working remotely with limited supervisory interaction
  • Should be Flexible as the position requires travelling and unplanned overtime may occur
