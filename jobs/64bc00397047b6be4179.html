Full description

The Workplace and Collaboration SME will be part of an agile product team responsible for the deployment and lifecycle management of software/applications. It shall take part in managing laptops and desktops, both physical and virtual. This includes operating, updating, and maintaining end-user PC platforms throughout their lifetime. The role also involves providing 3rd level support to assist other support functions and ensuring high-quality service delivery.

The team is responsible for software packaging and deployment, primarily through Microsoft Endpoint Configuration Manager (MECM) platform but not limited to it. The ideal candidate will bring a broad skill set with a specific focus on application packaging, deployment, and automation, with experience in software lifecycle management and delivery optimization, as well as PSADT technical skills.

Responsibilities

  • Manage end-to-end application packaging lifecycle (intake, packaging, validation, and release)
  • Create, develop and maintain deployment scripts using standardized frameworks (e.g., PSAppDeployToolkit)
  • Repackage and standardize applications (MSI/EXE) according to enterprise guidelines
  • Perform testing, support QA/UAT, and ensure reliability of software deployments
  • Provide high-level diagnosis and resolution of packaging and deployment issues and act as escalation point for L1, L2 and Workplace & Collaboration Engineers
  • Oversees automation development with the current platforms used in the area, including Robopack and Robopatch
  • Ensure applications are compatible with Modern Management solutions (e.g., Intune, MECM.)
  • Deliver high-quality, unattended installations in a Modern Workplace environment
  • Ensure compliance with packaging standards and deployment readiness
  • Support both traditional and cloud-based application delivery models (On-prem and Intune Apps)
  • Oversee migration of on-prem to cloud-based applications
  • Collaborate with stakeholders to ensure application requirements are met
  • Knowledgeable on management for Software Portals. (e.g. Company Portal, SWC. Etc.,)
  • Knowledge in client management platforms (e.g., MECM, Intune, Citrix, AVD)
  • Manage applications in task sequences for OS deployment (OSD) using MECM
  • Develop and maintain PowerShell scripts and continuously improve current scripting logic of packages
  • Collaborate with architects and other SMEs to optimize software management process
  • Manage initiatives to develop software management leading to automation
  • Maintain documentation, user guides, and technical references
  • Mentor team members and contribute to prioritization and planning with the Product Owner

Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in IT, Computer Science, or related field
  • Minimum of 5 years' experience in application packaging and client management
  • Demonstrated experience in process improvement and application package process automation
  • Practical experience in transitioning on-prem to cloud base apps
  • Solid proficiency in MECM, Intune, Windows Operating Systems, Active Directory and Entra ID
  • Experience working with enterprise deployment platforms
  • Advanced PowerShell scripting
  • Familiarity with Agile ways of working
  • Demonstrated skill in analyzing challenges and developing solutions
  • Effective communication and stakeholder engagement skills
  • Ability to work on their own and lead technical initiatives

Competencies

Technical Competencies

  • Application Packaging Tools: PSADT, Smart Package Studio, Robopack.
  • Solid understanding of MSI/MST, EXE, Windows OS, and scripting (PowerShell).
  • Extensive knowledge of packaging standards and enterprise deployment best practices.
  • MECM / Intune: Application deployment, monitoring, and issue resolution fundamentals. Including Task Sequence management and configuration.
  • MECM / Intune: Client management, issue resolution.
  • AVD / Virtual Environment: Basic to intermediate understanding of application delivery in virtual desktop environment
  • AVD/Virtual Environment: Fundamental knowledge on client management and structure of Virtual Desktop Environment.

Professional Competencies

  • Structured thinking and structured problem solving
  • Clear communication and stakeholder coordination
  • Quality and process-oriented mindset
  • Ability to work in a collaborative environment
