Senior Lease and Land Analyst

Role Overview

Title: Senior Lease and Land Analyst

Hours: Full-time; Salaried

Location: Salt Lake City, UT

Benefits Eligible: Yes

Manager: Head of Land

Mission – Why We Exist and Why We Need You

Geothermal energy is one of the worlds largest untapped sources of clean, reliable power. At Zanskar, we are transforming geothermal exploration and development through advanced technology, data science, and disciplined project execution.

The Senior Lease and Land Analyst plays a critical role in securing, managing, and optimizing the company's land and geothermal mineral assets. This position serves as the technical expert for lease administration, title analysis, land records management, contractual compliance, and real property due diligence across Zanskar's exploration and development portfolio. Working closely with Development, Legal, GIS, Permitting, Finance, and Business Development, the Senior Lease and Land Analyst ensures our land assets are development-ready while minimizing commercial and legal risk.

Outcomes – Problems You'll Solve

The Senior Lease & Land Analyst will lead and support all aspects of lease administration and

land management, including:

Administer a portfolio of federal, state, tribal, and private geothermal, mineral, and

surface leases from acquisition through commercial operations.

Prepare, review, negotiate, and administer leases, options, amendments, assignments, ratifications, easements, rights-of-way, surface use agreements, and other land contracts.

Track and ensure compliance with lease obligations, including bonus payments, rentals, royalties, primary terms, extension options, drilling commitments, continuous operations provisions, and contractual notice requirements.

Perform title analysis, ownership verification, and due diligence by reviewing deeds, leases, title opinions, probate records, ownership reports, and county land records.

Coordinate title curative activities with attorneys, landmen, title companies, and regulatory agencies to resolve ownership issues and maintain marketable title.

Support acquisitions, divestitures, joint ventures, financing transactions, and project development by conducting land and lease due diligence.

Maintain accurate land records, ownership databases, GIS mapping, and lease administration systems while ensuring data integrity.

Collaborate with internal teams to secure site control, support permitting activities, manage rights-of-way, and facilitate project development schedules.

Prepare executive reports summarizing lease status, acreage position, contractual obligations, title risks, and project readiness.

Identify commercial, legal, and operational risks affecting land assets and recommend

practical solutions that support business objectives.

Experience

5+ years of progressive experience in lease administration, land administration, title analysis, or land management within the oil & gas, geothermal, mining, or energy industries.

Demonstrated experience managing large and complex lease portfolios across federal, state, tribal, and private lands.

Experience supporting exploration, drilling, development, and commercial operations.

Preferred Certifications

Certified Lease & Title Analyst (CLTA) – NALTA

GIS/ArcGIS or QGIS experience a plus

Location

The position is based out of our headquarters in Salt Lake City, Utah
