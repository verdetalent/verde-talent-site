Role and Scope of Position:

Supervise line crew(s) involved in the installation, maintenance and repairs of the underground and overhead electric distribution system and oversee equipment, materials, and record-keeping.

Essential Functions:

  • Supervises line crew(s) involved in the installation, maintenance and repairs of the underground and overhead electric distribution system
  • Oversees equipment, materials, and record-keeping duties to include completion of time sheets for crew members as well as tailboard documentation.
  • Trains, instructs, and mentors Line-workers to ensure that safety rules and procedures are followed
  • Evaluates the performance of each crew member and initiates additional training or corrective action as may be necessary to bring performance up to standard
  • Recommend promotions, demotions and discipline through proper documentation
  • Coordinates and schedules work assignments
  • Conducts tailboards, and truck and trailer inspections
  • Ensures all work is completed in a timely manner, on schedule, and within budget
  • Provides first response coverage to off-hour emergency trouble calls and investigates, corrects, and clears trouble. In the event of high trouble call volume, ensures proper staffing has been accomplished
  • Accepts standby duty responsibilities throughout the year, which consists of being available via telephone and/or pager during this duty; accepts temporary responsibility for Field Supervisor-Lines and Supervisor-Distribution Lines in the daily operation of the Area Work Center in their absence
  • Works with emergency services, such as fire and police to accomplish a safe environment
  • Enforces Company environmental policies and procedures on each job site
  • Identifies appropriate procedural methods; and make decisions based on analysis of information
  • Works effectively with subordinates; superiors, interfacing departments and vendors
  • Analyzes operational methods and develops improved procedures as needed
  • Interprets Company policies, procedures and practices
  • Performs the duties of a Line-worker I (RG 34.5)

Technical Knowledge/Skill/Education/Licenses/Certifications:

Technical Knowledge/Skill:

  • Knowledge of and the ability to apply electrical theory
  • Complete working understanding of Company line work practices and policies is required.
  • Ability to become a certified rubber glover of 34.5 kV.
  • Employee weight limit for this position is 300 pounds.
  • Must live within 30 minutes of the Area Work Center
  • Requires mechanical aptitude, manual and finger dexterity,
  • Ability to communicate effectively, follow instructions and climb and work on poles and structures.
  • To qualify for the position an individual must meet the requirements associated with standby coverage, and provide such coverage

Education:

  • High School diploma or the equivalent and a combination of education, training, and experience equivalent to a Line-worker I (RG 34.5) is required.

Experience:

  • Minimum of three (3) years as a Line-worker I (RG 34.5).  Supervisory experience is preferred.

Licenses & Certifications:

  • Commercial Driver's license (Class A with Special Trailer Endorsement) and a safe driving record is also required

Working Conditions:

  • Must be available to work emergency restoration assignment as required.
  • Must be available to travel between MA/CT/NH as necessary.
  • Will be required to work overtime when needed
