**Position Summary**

The Work Management Specialist (Project Planner) is responsible for planning and coordinating maintenance and capital project work to ensure safe, efficient, and cost-effective execution. This position develops comprehensive, barrier-free work packages that support successful project implementation while maintaining compliance with company standards, regulatory requirements, and safety expectations.

The successful candidate will work closely with Operations, Maintenance, Engineering, Supply Chain, Contractors, and Project Management to plan, coordinate, and support outage and project activities from initiation through completion.

**Responsibilities**

  • Develop detailed work breakdown structures (WBS) for maintenance and capital projects.
  • Prepare complete, barrier-free work packages including detailed work scope, Health, Safety & Environmental (HSE) plans, Quality plans, Resource plans, Schedules, and Cost estimates.
  • Procure parts and materials required to support project execution.
  • Prepare contract Requests for Proposal (RFPs) and participate in contractor bid evaluations.
  • Obtain quotations and submit requisitions for non-stock parts & materials, equipment, and contractor services.
  • Coordinate with Supply Chain to ensure timely delivery of project materials.
  • Assist in prioritizing preventive and corrective maintenance work orders.
  • Submit equipment Lock Out/Tag Out (LOTO) requests.
  • Develop Job Hazard Analyses (JHAs) and maintenance procedures.
  • Support configuration change activities by identifying spare parts, updating inventory, removing obsolete materials, initiating drawing revisions, and developing preventive maintenance job plans.
  • Participate in planning, scheduling, and project coordination meetings.
  • Communicate regularly with project stakeholders regarding scope, schedule, risks, and execution.
  • Participate in Duke Energy's Safety Observation Program.
  • Perform other related duties as required.
