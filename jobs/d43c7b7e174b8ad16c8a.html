About Redwood Materials

Redwood is localizing a global battery supply chain that seamlessly integrates recovery, reuse, and recycling—keeping critical minerals in circulation and driving the energy transition. Founded in 2017,we’re delivering low-cost and large-scale energy storage and producing battery materials in the U.S. for the first time, all from batteries we already have.

Field Service Technician, Solar and Battery Storage

We are seeking an experienced Field Service Technician to support solar and battery storage systems across multiple field locations. In this role, you will travel regularly to manage work requests, perform equipment inspections, and execute maintenance with minimal oversight in a fast-paced, startup environment. The ideal candidate brings hands-on experience in electro-mechanical troubleshooting, repair, and maintenance of solar panels, battery storage systems, and related equipment. You have strong technical judgment, a high standard for quality, and the ability to prioritize and execute across multiple tasks.

Responsibilities will include:

  • Troubleshoot and repair solar and battery storage systems, including electrical and mechanical components
  • Perform routine maintenance and inspections of solar panels, inverters, and battery storage units
  • Operate specialized tools and equipment for solar and battery system installations and repairs
  • Manage field-based projects and oversee contractors and third-party work
  • Create and implement standard work instructions (SWIs) for field service tasks
  • Complete daily task lists and respond to field service work requests
  • Address general electro-mechanical issues related to solar and battery systems
  • Work cross-functionally with operations teams to support growth and continuous improvement goals

Desired Qualifications:

  • At least 2 years of experience in electro-mechanical troubleshooting and repair, preferably in solar or battery storage systems
  • Strong background in electrical systems and mechanical maintenance
  • Ability to troubleshoot and repair at a high level with a self-motivated, hands-on mindset
  • Physical ability to lift and carry up to 50 lbs. and perform tasks requiring bending and lifting
  • Proficiency in maintaining and using tools and equipment necessary for field service duties
  • A passion for sustainability and making the world a better place!

Physical Requirements:

  • Must be able to lift and carry up to 50 lbs., bend, lift, and walk up and down stairs carrying weight
  • Essential physical requirements include climbing, standing, stooping, and using specialized tools

Working Conditions:

  • Valid driver’s license and ability to travel to field locations; frequent travel required
  • Work in both indoor and outdoor environments with exposure to varying weather, noise, temperature extremes
  • Availability to work weekends, nights, or on- call as needed

The position is full-time. Compensation will be commensurate with experience.
