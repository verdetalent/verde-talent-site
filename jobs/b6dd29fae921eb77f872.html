Position Specific Description

This position is located at our Port Everglades Plant in Fort Lauderdale, Florida and will travel the state of Florida to support the fleet outages. Tools are provided by the company. Days and shifts will vary based on the business unit needs during the outage. Normal schedule (not supporting an outage) is a day shift Monday-Friday. Overtime hours average approximately between 400 hours to 800 hours per year. Employees are offered per diem when traveling outside of their region.  The plant operates 24/7 so individuals will be expected to work and support weekend days as needed.

Job Overview

Performs Instrumentation & Control equipment maintenance, troubleshooting, replacement and repairs activities:

  • Repairs, replaces, troubleshoots, calibrates and maintains power plant Instrumentation & Control equipment and systems
  • Utilizes/operates various types of test instruments.  Uses hand, power, tools/equipment necessary to support a safe and productive work environment
  • Candidates will be required to take and pass the MASS Test and also be Hands-On tested to demonstrate knowledge and craft skills
  • Work Schedule: Day Shift, except when supporting outages.  Travel required for this position
  • Local Candidate Strongly Preferred
  • Relocation Assistance is not provided
  • Solid knowledge and experience in calibrating, maintaining and troubleshooting Instrumentation & Control equipment and systems in power plant, industrial facility, processing plant or Navy environment (minimum of 2 years)
  • Proficiency in I&C skills, knowledge and abilities related to:  Industrial variables' measurement, industrial transmitters (digital and analog), controllers, control loops, final control elements (pneumatic, hydraulic, and electric), Distributed Control Systems, data communications, fiber optics, control drawings and transducers

Desired Qualification:

Troubleshooting Distributed Control Systems and PLC's Installation, troubleshooting and repair of fiber optic conductors

Required Qualifications

  • Pwr Plt Mtce Pos Sel Sys Test passed
  • Valid Driver's License (NON CDL)

Preferred Qualifications

  • None
