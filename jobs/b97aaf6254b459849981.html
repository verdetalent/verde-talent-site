First Solar reserves the right to offer you a role most applicable to your experience and skillset. Basic Job Functions: The HR Generalist I will work with HR management and Operations management to provide mobility, Immigration and Site-specific HR support (US assigned locations only) to enable successful operations across US corporate and Global Manufacturing sites. Provides day-to-day Mobility and Immigration support by serving as the primary point of contact for associates across Company Operational Jurisdictions. This position also has responsibilities in the following functional areas: employee relations, training, onboarding, policy implementation, and employment law compliance. Education/Experience: Minimum 1 to 2 years of experience in Human Resources is preferred. Proven employee relations experience in a manufacturing setting is preferred. Knowledge of HRIS systems, ADP, and Kronos is preferred. Bachelor’s Degree required, major in human resources or business is preferred. A PHR or SHRM-CP certification is preferred. Required Skills/Competencies: Understanding of general human resources policies and procedures. Good knowledge of employment/labor laws. Basic understanding of US work visa types/regulations. Time management & strong organization skills. Excellent verbal, written communication, and people skills. Ethical Practice. Cultural Awareness. Relationship management. Must be self-motivated, with the ability to work independently and in a team environment. Proficient use of all Microsoft Office suite programs and HRIS systems. Essential Responsibilities: Serve as the primary point of contact for global associates regarding immigration and visa-related inquiries. Act as the first point of HR contact for assigned business sites. Support HR leadership with internal reviews, audits, and assessments of immigration data, processes, and policies.
