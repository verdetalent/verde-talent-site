Do you have an established experience within project management with high focus on Health & Safety, Quality, and Time/Planning? Are you team-oriented with an ability to encourage continuous development within the team whilst working in a result-oriented environment? Do you work to find solutions with an ability to stay professional and focused during periods of high activity? Then, Vestas might have the opportunity for you! Region NCE and Global Offshore > Global Offshore > MCE Team lead The Main Component Exchange department is responsible for ensuring a high-quality customer experience when completing main component exchange projects for our customers, supporting service sites in executing special campaigns and internal project support within department. The department is part of the Offshore Service organization in the Vestas NCE Region. You will become part of a team of over 500 professionals working daily to provide offshore services for our trusted customers. This includes everything from strategy work, operational improvement, Health & Safety, technical case handling to delivering the services on the wind turbines offshore. As Deputy Site Manager you will manage daily offshore operations to ensure safe, productive, and cost efficient use of vessel time, resources, and personnel while meeting budget and productivity targets. Quality Lead within the project team. Engage with Feedback loop to Internal and External resource management to support technician development within core team. You will be involved in shift leadership, safety oversight, logistics and documentation.
