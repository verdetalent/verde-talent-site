About Us: Vestas is a trusted name in wind energy, dedicated to advancing sustainable energy solutions. We pride ourselves on our innovative and safety-focused approach to renewable energy. Job Overview: The Mobile Site Manager will be tasked with the management of several service sites across a specific region, aiming to maintain smooth operations, high levels of customer fulfillment, and compliance with safety standards. This role requires significant travel, as you will be assigned to various sites as needed. Key Responsibilities: · Safety and Compliance: Establish and oversee the implementation of safety practices and protocols. Ensure compliance with Vestas’s organizational standards and safety regulations. · Operational Effectiveness: Cultivate a culture of safety, structure, and continuous improvement. Communicate change management effectively to stakeholders. · Team Leadership: Lead and develop a team of Service Technicians. Ensure team members are compliant with safety training and certifications. Build internal and external partnerships. · Customer Relations: Interface with customers, address inquiries, and collaborate with internal departments to resolve issues. Ensure all contractual obligations are met. · Financial Management: Manage site budgets, cost allocations, and operational revenues. Input and ensure accurate data in systems like Salesforce and SAP. · Continuous Improvement: Develop and deliver continuous improvement plans. Implement LEAN practices at the site. · Technical Assistance: Provide technical support to Service Technicians for diagnosing and resolving issues. Ensure team adherence to work instructions and propose improvements. · Travel Requirements: This is a 100% travel position. You may be assigned to any service site within your designated region and occasionally to sites in other regions based on business needs.
