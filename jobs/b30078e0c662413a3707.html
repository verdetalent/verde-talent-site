Fluence (Nasdaq: FLNC) is a global market leader delivering intelligent energy storage and optimization software for renewables and storage. Our solutions and operational services are helping to create a more resilient grid and unlock the full potential of renewable portfolios. For more information, please visit fluenceenergy.com . Job Description: Role Overview This is a highly networked role that requires an ability to work independently and produce results to meet customer deadlines. This role is responsible for optimizing the end-to-end proposal and contract management process, ensuring high-quality, customer-tailored proposals and contracts for clean battery storage solutions are delivered on time and meet all technical and commercial requirements. Key Responsibilities Oversees and standardizes proposal processes across multiple teams and geographies. Sets organizational-level frameworks and tools to ensure customer requirements are always captured and addressed. Evaluates process effectiveness and implements systemic improvements. Ensures consistency and scalability of proposal management across the business. Manage and create multiple compelling proposals to provide equipment sales, turn-key EPC solutions, and LTSA services for energy storage projects. Key responsibilities include understanding RFP / RFQ requirements, and establishing a winning bid strategy, coordination with the engineering, commercial, and legal teams to understand project requirements and risks. Manage and coordinate contract documentation during the negotiation phase of multiple complex opportunities, overseeing the transition from RFP response to contracting.
