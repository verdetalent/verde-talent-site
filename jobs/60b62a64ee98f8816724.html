Position Summary The Strategic Sourcing Organization is seeking a Senior Sourcing Specialist to support the sourcing and contracting of large spend equipment and labor for generation projects including thermal, hydro, and Renewables. The position is in our Major projects and Renewables team and will report to the Director Sourcing, Major Projects. Primary responsibilities are sourcing, contracting and category management of equipment and labor contracts for our new construction gas and hydro projects with additional responsibilities for supporting renewables. This is the third level of the Sourcing classification hierarchy. Employees at this level manage work with minimal supervision. Incumbents are expected to develop advanced skills and to have the ability to work independently, with guidance limited to unusual or complex projects or issues. They identify problems, develop solutions, and take actions to resolve. Responsibilities Plan, develop and execute sourcing strategies for equipment, professional services, construction services and EPC contracts, which ensure reliable and safe deliveries on schedule and at a competitive cost.
