Role Overview for Sr. Service Technician

Wondering what your day-to-day could look like as our new Sr. Service Technician? In this role, you’ll play an integral part of the service team, primarily responsible for commissioning and performing preventative and corrective maintenance on SolBank Storage Power Block (SPB). This role also involves fulfilling obligations under Long Term Service Agreements (LTSA’s) for Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) projects. The successful candidate must be willing to travel and perform tasks at various project sites throughout the US. Experience with BESS or renewable power generation equipment in a service environment is required.

Whilst you’re working in field services, your work will directly impact our mission to create a cleaner, brighter future. We’re looking for someone who’s ready to roll up their sleeves, share their ideas, and make a real difference—every single day.

What You’ll Be Doing as a Sr. Service Technician

  • Commission CSI Energy Storage’s SolBank Storage Power Block
  • Perform preventative and corrective maintenance of SPBs to adhere to warranty requirements
  • Manage obligations for BESS equipment under LTSA’s, which may include:
  • Energy Storage Cabinets (ESC’s)
  • Power Conversion Stations (PCS)
  • Ring Main Units (RMU’s) / Switchgear
  • Transformers
  • SCADA and networking devices
  • Provide onsite diagnostic, troubleshooting, and hands-on support to maintain equipment and meet availability and capacity guarantees
  • Oversee and supervise sub-contractors on site when required
  • Assist in developing standard operating procedures and processes
  • Complete work order documentation in a timely manner
  • Ensure accuracy of spare parts inventory
  • Perform proper Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) and switching procedures as needed
  • Support client training during commissioning
  • Adhere to all health and safety rules and policies
  • Complete other duties and projects as assigned
  • Team up with talented colleagues and partners to achieve project goals.
  • Champion our standards for quality, safety, and sustainability.
  • Help us keep improving—your ideas are always welcome!

Want To Join? Here Is What We Are Looking For

  • Qualified Electrical Technician
  • Minimum 5 years of relevant work experience in a service environment, preferably in renewable energy
  • OSHA 30 Certified (OSHA 10 certification is accepted if OSHA 30 is obtained soon after hire)
  • Previous training and knowledge of inverters
  • SCADA experience is beneficial
  • Knowledge of general and national codes, medium voltage power systems, and Energy Storage system applications is an asset
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills
  • Demonstrated proficiency with MS Office Applications (Word, Excel)
  • Previous experience with a CMMS software program is preferred
  • Valid driver’s license with a clean driving record
  • First Aid Trained
  • Ability to travel to regional locations as needed (over 50% of the time)
  • Ability to work successfully both independently and within a team
  • Demonstrated professional appearance and conduct
  • Great communication, teamwork, and problem-solving abilities.
