SSC-Contract Management

Responsibilities

  • Oversee the complete contract lifecycle operations across case management, shared mailboxes, SAP & Salesforce workflows
  • Ensure timely handling of tickets, emails, follow ups, escalations, and closure of cases in alignment with SLAs
  • Lead day to day team operations including workload allocation, queue monitoring, backlog management, and first time right quality review
  • Collaborate with Sales, Legal, Finance, and other cross functional teams to resolve queries, exceptions, and documentation gaps
  • Drive excellence initiatives by standardizing processes, improving accuracy, and optimizing turnaround times
  • Maintain governance, compliance, and documentation standards for contract creation, amendments, renewals, terminations, and audit readiness
  • Ensure new team member readiness by driving comprehensive onboarding, skill ramp up, process training, and ongoing capability assessment to enable smooth integration and productivity
  • Conduct regular performance reviews, skill development, and training to build team capability and operational resilience
  • Act as the escalation point for complex contract issues, customer specific requirements, and cross team coordination challenges
  • Ensure all cases are logged, tracked, and reported accurately with strong focus on data quality, metrics visibility, and operational transparency
  • Prepare and present weekly/monthly dashboards on SLA performance, case volumes, backlog status, quality metrics, and continuous improvement progress
  • Support audit activities, RCA investigations, and risk mitigation actions for process gaps and compliance deviations

Qualifications

  • Graduate or Post graduate degree in Business, Commerce, or related field with minimum 7-10 years of experience in contract management or commercial operations
  • Minimum 1-2 years of experience leading teams or supervising operational processes
  • Fluent in English is a must
  • Experience in customer facing roles or cross functional coordination will be an advantage
  • Good MS Office knowledge, especially Excel and PowerPoint
  • Experience with Salesforce, SAP, Ariba, or similar platforms will be an asset

Competencies

  • Ability to independently manage end to end contract processes and moderate complexity cases
  • Strong analytical, coordination, and problem-solving skills
  • Proficiency in using SAP, Salesforce and case management (functional level)
  • High accuracy and attention to detail in contract review and documentation
  • Effective communication and stakeholder management across cross functional teams
  • Strong team leadership skills with ability to guide, coach, and support team members
