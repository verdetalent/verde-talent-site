Basic Job Scope:

When you work at First Solar, you will be challenged every day and provided real world experience. Expected Length of Internship:

Education/Experience:

  • Junior/Senior student with previous internship experience
  • First Solar encourages all interns to register their internship with their university.

Required Skills/Competencies:

  • Experience with a strong marketing analytics mindset and interest in data, AI/LLMs, digital marketing, AI and AI agent creation, and social media platforms.
  • Ability to treat AI output as a first draft to be checked and sharpened, not a finished answer

Basic Internship Scope (Project):

  • Build AI-assisted competitor-analysis workflows that produce a first-pass, human-reviewed read to inform marketing strategy
  • Build and maintain social and media performance dashboards (e.g., Sprout Social) to analyze campaign effectiveness, web analytics, and engagement trends
  • Use AI/LLMs to define and document audience personas — customers, communities, policy stakeholders — to support content strategy
  • Evaluate content performance against those personas and recommend improvements
  • Measure and document the capacity gained and time saved by the AI workflows you build, so the role proves its own return
