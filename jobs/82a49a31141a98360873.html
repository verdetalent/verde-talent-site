Basic Job Functions: • The person in this role is responsible for conducting routine analysis following defined methods in support of recycling, wastewater, raw material production, procurement, and refinement. • Day-to-day responsibilities include sampling and sample preparation, operation and maintenance of analytical instrumentation as assigned, data management / reporting and maintenance of a clean laboratory environment. • This person will be comfortable working under the direction of laboratory management and will be conscious of safety and data quality. Education/Experience: • Associate degree in chemistry or closely related field required. • BA or BS in chemistry or closely related field preferred. • Coursework in analytical chemistry, polymers, and inorganic or solid-state chemistry preferred. • At least 3 years’ experience in an analytical laboratory environment or academic equivalent experience required. • Experience in an environment involving polymer analysis, thermal analysis, and wet chemistry preferred. Required Skills/Competencies: • Proven competency in chemical analysis; ICP analysis, and polymer characterization preferred. • Ability to prepare samples, standards, and conduct analyses. • Ability to learn the operation, calibration, and preventative maintenance of metrology equipment. • Skilled in wet and solid sample preparation methodology. • Will handle and use toxic as well as flammable materials. • Intermediate knowledge of standard software (MS Office suite, JMP). • Ability to use analytical skills to read charts and graphs to find trends. • Good communication skills required. • Ability to multi-task.
