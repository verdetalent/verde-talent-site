Logistics Team Member

Shift: Night Shift: Monday - Thursday 3:45 pm - 4:45 am with a $3/hour differential for pay.

What does a Logistics Team Member do?

  • Maintain a safe work environment by following all safety rules and procedures; actively participate in 5S activities.
  • Receive, store, and distribute materials, tools, and products based on internal customer needs.
  • Read schedules and work orders to determine items to move or distribute; ensure FIFO practices.
  • Sort and place materials on racks, shelves, or bins; assemble and prepare customer orders for shipment.
  • Accurately record material movements and maintain inventory records using computer systems.
  • Safely operate heavy equipment and vehicles to transport items within the plant or between locations.
  • Handle hazardous materials and waste in compliance with all state and federal regulations, including documentation and transport.
  • Contribute to team goals and adapt to process changes; perform other duties as assigned.

What skills/abilities must I have to qualify?

  • High school diploma or GED required; 1–3 years of logistics, warehousing, or material handling experience preferred.
  • Ability to communicate effectively in English (reading, writing, speaking).
  • Intermediate computer skills; proficiency with Microsoft programs and inventory systems preferred.
  • Ability to perform basic math (add, subtract, multiply, divide).
  • Must be able to lift up to 30 lbs, climb stairs, and wear required PPE.
  • Regular and reliable attendance; ability to safely operate heavy equipment.

What you’ll receive:

  • Benefits coverage within your first month
  • State-of-the-art Training Program
  • Paid Time Off (PTO)
  • Excellent 401(k) plan
  • Annual bonus

Salary: $23.50-$25.50 per hour + $3/hour differential for pay
