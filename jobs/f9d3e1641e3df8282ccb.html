Employee & Labor Relations Specialist The Employee & Labor Relations Specialist serves as a trusted advisor to leaders and employees, helping create a fair, consistent, and legally compliant workplace. In a manufacturing environment where safety, productivity, and consistency are paramount, this role manages employee relations issues, supports nonunion labor strategies, and ensures clear, respectful workplace practices. The specialist partners closely with People & Culture Business Partners and operations leadership to resolve concerns, improve work environments, and build better employee relations Responsibilities: Develop employee relations strategies to support proactive communication, fair practices, strong leadership, and a respectful culture to create an environment where employees are heard, valued, and safe Partner with supervisors and managers to coach on effective employee management, documentation, and conflict resolution. Analyze trends in complaints, absenteeism, turnover, and corrective actions; recommend and deploy timely interventions Working closely with legal, c onduct investigations related to policy violations, performance concerns, misconduct, and other workplace issues. Support company culture by reinforcing values, communication expectations, and consistent employment practices. Support vulnerability assessments and proactive retention/engagement strategies including stay interviews, exit interviews, and environmental scans to guide strategic improvements Ensure compliance with federal, state, and local labor laws (FLSA, FMLA, ADA, EEOC, NLRA, etc.).
