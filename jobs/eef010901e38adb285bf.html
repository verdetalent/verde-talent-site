Salesforce Consultant – Agentforce

ScottishPower HQ, Glasgow

Salary up to £74,000 + up to 15% bonus, single cover private medical & other excellent benefits

Closing date- Friday 31st of July 2026

Help us create a better future, quicker!

The Applications Consultant is responsible for designing, implementing, and governing Salesforce Agentforce solutions that deliver measurable business value, increased workforce productivity, enhanced customer engagement, and operational efficiency across SPEN.

Working within the Centre of Excellence, the role will lead the adoption of Salesforce Agentforce, Einstein AI, Data Cloud, and related Salesforce platform capabilities. The post holder will work closely with business stakeholders, Salesforce architects, product owners, and implementation partners to identify opportunities for autonomous agents, conversational AI, workflow automation, and intelligent service delivery.

The role will ensure Agentforce solutions align with enterprise architecture, security, governance, and responsible AI principles while establishing reusable frameworks, standards, and best practices for Salesforce AI adoption.

As this role supports Critical National Infrastructure business processes, this will be an operational mainly office-based, role where working hours may change to suit the needs of our critical services.

What you’ll be doing

  • Lead the definition and execution of the Salesforce Agentforce roadmap
  • Identify and prioritise use cases for autonomous agents across Customer Operations, Network Operations, Asset Management, Field Services and Corporate Functions
  • Establish governance, security, and operational standards for Agentforce implementations.
  • Providing clear AI design direction to Delivery Teams. Improving the knowledge of Value Stream through mentoring, workshops, and other skill transfer methods
  • Early engagement on large projects and programmes; Taking strategic direction from the Architecture & Strategy team and defining detailed AI Solutions for the business. Assisting business in early scope definition, feasibility analysis, and business case development
  • Evaluate emerging AI technologies and provide recommendations on their suitability
  • Lead AI proof-of-concept and pilot initiatives through to production deployment
  • Identify opportunities where AI can improve operational efficiency, safety, customer service, and asset management
  • Support Agentforce implementation through Application and Product Roadmaps
  • Lead the configuration and deployment of autonomous Salesforce agents
  • Define agent actions, topics, instructions, guardrails, and escalation paths
  • Ensure agents operate securely and effectively within define business processes
  • Support testing, validation and monitoring of agent performance and business outcomes
  • Experience in design, delivery and evaluation of AI requirements and development of appropriate Global and Local solutions working with the Transformation Architecture & Strategy Team and other Technology Teams
  • Define scalable patterns for agent orchestration, workflow automation and human-in-the-loop decision making
  • Maintain awareness of Salesforce market trends and technology developments
  • Validation of all financial cost assumptions regarding the Agentforce solutions
  • Identify opportunities for Agentforce deployment across key business functions

What you’ll bring

  • Significant experience of AI solution development in an organisation of similar scope and scale to SP Energy Networks, with previous Solutions Application Design experience
  • Significant experience delivering AI, Data Analytics, Machine Learning, or Intelligent Automation solutions both internally and with external vendors
  • Significant experience with Salesforce platform technologies including Agentforce, Einstein AI, Data Cloud, Service Cloud and Salesforce Flow
  • Experience designing and implementing autonomous agents using Salesforce Agentforce
  • Understanding of Salesforce Trust Layer, Prompt Builder and Agent Builder
  • Experience integrating Salesforce AI capabilities with enterprise systems through APIs and event-driven architectures
  • Understanding of LLMs, prompt engineering, RAG architectures, and agentic AI frameworks
  • Highly developed problem solving and delivery skills with the ability to analyse complex issues, recommend appropriate solutions and manage projects to delivery these
  • Experience working with data platforms, analytics, and AI model deployment
  • Understanding of AI governance, security, ethics, and regulatory considerations
  • Ability to identify and prioritise high-value AI opportunities
  • Excellent communication skills, with an ability to distil technical concepts to business focussed audiences
  • Strong technology consulting and stakeholder management skills
  • Experience translating business problems into AI solutions
  • Ability to develop AI business cases and value realisation metrics

Salesforce specific experience

  • Salesforce certified professional with multiple cloud implementations
  • Experience designing enterprise customer and operational workflows on Salesforce
  • Experience with complex Salesforce integrations
  • Experience delivering AI-enabled Service Cloud and Field Service solutions
  • Knowledge of Salesforce release management and DevOps practices
  • Understanding of Salesforce Well-Architected Framework principles

Minimum Criteria

  • 5 years plus of progressive experience in software engineering/enterprise architecture/technology leadership across multiple products, systems, and/or platforms
  • Demonstrable experience in AI, Advanced Analytics, Intelligent Automation or Data Science related roles
  • Experience designing and implementing AI-enabled business solutions
  • Strong understanding of enterprise AI architecture and governance
  • Knowledge and practical experience of:
  • Salesforce Agentforce
  • Salesforce Einstein AI
  • Einstein Copilot
  • Agent Builder
  • Prompt Builder
  • Salesforce Data Cloud
  • Service Cloud

Why SP Energy Networks

SP Energy Networks is part of the Iberdrola Group, one of the world’s largest integrated utility companies and a world leader in wind energy. We keep electricity flowing to homes and businesses through Central and Southern Scotland, North Wales and in the Northwest of England. We operate over 4000km of cables and lines that make-up the transmission network – connecting infrastructure like wind farms into the electricity system. It’s a role that puts us right at the heart of Scotland’s ambition to be Net Zero by 2045.

And we’re taking it very seriously. We’re investing >£10 billion into our transmission network, directly supporting the rapid growth needed in renewable energy. With diverse opportunities across our businesses and a commitment to invest in our own internal talent, ScottishPower can offer people real career opportunities that meet personal and professional goals, in a global organisation.

Inclusion, diversity, and a social purpose are at the heart of everything we do. Together with our values, they bring us together into a stronger, more sustainable business with direct links to the communities we serve. It takes all kinds of people to build a large-scale business like ours, so whatever your background, you’ll fit right in.
