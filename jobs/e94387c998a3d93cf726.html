**Specialist** | **Full-Time**  | **Supply Chain and Logistics**

Department

The Construction & Transport Procurement team is responsible for the strategic sourcing of all goods and services for the delivery of our wind energy solutions to our customers.

Through early engagement of our supply chain partners, we secure the best value engineered solution for the development and execution of our EPC projects. With a cross functional team of stakeholders, we strategically develop partnerships to enable sustainable growth and drive value for our end customers.You will be part of a highly dynamic and diverse team, that is driven by Vestas Values (Passion, Collaboration, Accountability and Simplicity) and is excited about the prospects of renewables in the region and our purpose to fight climate change. We offer a rewarding, purpose-based career in an international environment.

Responsibilities

  • Lead the sourcing process throughout the bidding process, ensuring safety, quality, delivery and competitiveness of the supply base
  • Drive package negotiations and develop and implement value-adding commercial strategies- Manage the contract closing process, including document drafting and stakeholder approvals via the ERP system- Build supply market intelligence and ensure that the right supply base is available to enable our sustainable growth strategy
  • Ensure that all cost out and value creation opportunities are identified, and that relevant risk assessments are completed and acted upon to reduce risk.

Qualifications

  • Minimum of a bachelor's degree in engineering, business, law or finance
  • 8+ years of procurement, commercial or project management experience, ideally within the energy industry or the construction industry

Competencies

  • Expert in contract drafting and negotiation
  • Strong knowledge of ANZ construction market- Good interpersonal and stakeholder management skills- Advanced MS Office skills including data analysis
  • Well-rounded ERP (SAP/Ariba) user
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills
  • Able to manage complex supplier relationships
  • Well organized and disciplined approach
  • Initiative and creativity in finding solutions

About Vestas

Vestas is the energy industry’s global partner on sustainable energy solutions. We are specialised in designing, manufacturing, installing, and servicing wind turbines, both onshore and offshore.

Across the globe, we have installed more wind power than anyone else. We consider ourselves pioneers within the industry, as we continuously aim to design new solutions and technologies to create a more sustainable future for all of us. With more than 200 GW of wind power installed worldwide and 40+ years of experience in wind energy, we have an unmatched track record demonstrating our expertise within the field.

With 39,000 employees globally, we are a diverse team united by a common goal: to power the solution – today, tomorrow, and far into the future.
