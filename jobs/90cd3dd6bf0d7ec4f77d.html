Position Specific Description The Sr Workforce Planning Consultant under the supervision of the Manager, Workforce Planning, will be responsible for the following. Data Analysis & Reporting Develop, maintain, and automate dashboards and reports related to contractor workforce metrics. Analyze contractor headcount, spend, tenure, bill rates, supplier performance, and time-to-fill metrics. Provide trend analysis, forecasting, and scenario modeling to support workforce planning. Program Performance & Optimization Monitor program KPIs such as fill rates, cycle times, compliance rates, and attrition. Support supplier scorecard development and performance analysis. Conduct spend analysis across departments, suppliers, and job categories. Evaluate rate benchmarking and market competitiveness. Data Governance & Compliance Ensure data accuracy and integrity across systems. Support audits and compliance reviews related to contractor tenure, classification, and policy adherence. Maintain documentation of data definitions and reporting standards. Job Overview Employees in this job develop short and long range strategic plans for effective recruitment, placement, and development of employees. This role works with operational personnel to establish and maintain effective workforce planning initiatives for the organizations.
