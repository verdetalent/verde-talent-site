Full description

Bloom Energy faces an unprecedented opportunity to change the world and the way energy is generated and delivered.  Our mission is to make clean, reliable energy affordable globally.  Bloom’s Energy Server delivers highly reliable and resilient, always-on electric power that is clean, cost-effective, and ideal for micro grid applications.  We are helping our customers power their operations without disruption and combustion.

We are looking for a Business Intelligence Senior Manager to join our team in one of today’s most exciting technologies.  This role would report to the Director of Service Business Intelligence and be based in Mumbai, India.

The Business Intelligence Senior Manager will lead and develop a team of developers, analysts and data architects to perform complex business analysis, build data analytics and automation into our service strategy. This will include tracking fleet performance, working with forecasting models, and development of automated planning for parts and maintenance. This position will also be responsible for enhanced analytics and visualization capabilities for Senior Level reporting requirements.

Requirements:

  • 7–10 years in Business Intelligence, operational analytics, or data analytics
  • Team leadership and analyst mentorship experience
  • Experience in business analysis and data interpretation
  • Experience with data warehousing technologies (such as AWS, GCP, Azure, SQL, NoSQL)
  • Strong SQL and Python knowledge
  • Good knowledge of statistics and identifying patterns and insights
  • Experience with data visualization tools like Tableau or Amazon QuickSight
  • Experience in energy or industrial equipment sectors is a plus
  • Familiarity with SLAs, dispatch, CM/PM frameworks is a plus
  • Exposure to Python/R or predictive modeling is a plus
  • Excellent communication and presentation skills!

Responsibilities:

  • Building and managing a high performing business intelligence team
  • Leading the Service Business Intelligence execution of delivering reports, analysis and insights
  • Prioritizing and managing analytics demands of internal and external teams
  • Establishing best practices for dashboard design, data modeling, QA, and documentation
  • Driving a culture of accuracy, speed, and continuous improvement
  • Responsible for maintaining KPIs, performing analysis and developing and automating self-service reports and dashboards
  • Responsible for working on complex business problems, scenario analysis, performance modeling, and presenting data-driven recommendations
  • Solving challenging data and architectural problems using cutting edge technology to improve data pipelines with IT and Data Engineering teams
  • Cross functional collaboration with multiple business and technical teams to support QBRs and strategic planning

Graduate Education in:

  • Bachelor’s Degree in Analytics, Computer / Data Science or equivalent; MBA a plus

About Bloom Energy:

At Bloom Energy, we support the move toward a 100% renewable future and our fuel-flexible technology offers one of the most resilient electricity solutions for a world facing unacceptable levels of power disruptions. Our resilient platform has proven itself by powering through hurricanes, earthquakes, forest fires, extreme heat and utility failures. Unlike backup generators, our fuel cells creates no harmful local air pollutants. At the same time, Bloom is at the forefront of the transition to renewable fuels like hydrogen and biogas with new hydrogen power generation and electrolyzer solutions.  Our customers include but are not limited to; manufacturing, data centers, healthcare, retail, low-income housing, colleges, and more!  For more information, visit www.bloomenergy.com.
