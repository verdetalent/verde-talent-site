Full description

Are you motivated by creating clarity in complex environments and turning strategy into execution?

Do you enjoy building the structures, processes, and governance that enable teams to perform at their best?

As a Product Manager, you will play a critical role in strengthening the product management discipline across Vestas, creating the frameworks, insights, and operating rhythm that help our product organization make better decisions, move faster, and deliver greater customer value.

Modern product organizations increasingly rely on strong product operating models, governance, roadmap management, and decision frameworks to connect strategy with execution and business outcomes.

Vestas Technology & Operations > Product Line Onshore > Product Management ONS

Product Line Onshore is the strategic driving force behind VTO’s product success, with end-to-end accountability for competitiveness, customer value, and business outcomes. Acting as the voice of the customer within VTO and the voice of VTO towards customers, the function ensures that market needs, customer insights, and business priorities are translated into a compelling product vision and roadmap.

It steers the product portfolio, investment decisions, budgets, and key cross-functional initiatives to maximize value creation and long-term performance. In essence, the Product Line function aligns the organization around clear priorities, enabling efficient execution, innovation, and sustainable growth while delivering superior customer value.

Responsibilities

As a Product Manager, you will help define and maintain the operating framework for our product management organization. You will drive performance transparency, coordinate annual product planning cycles, facilitate portfolio governance, and establish the tools, templates, and processes that enable consistent and effective product management across the business.

Working across the value chain, you will connect market insights, product strategy, business performance, and development priorities to support fact-based decision-making and long-term portfolio success.

  • Own and continuously improve the product management operating framework, including governance processes, performance reporting, planning cycles, and decision-making structures
  • Coordinate the annual product planning process, ensuring alignment between market needs, business priorities, product roadmaps, and development activities
  • Develop and maintain standardized business case methodologies, templates, value frameworks, and supporting tools to enable consistent evaluation of investment opportunities and strategic initiatives
  • Drive visibility of product and portfolio performance through robust reporting, KPI tracking, and management insights that support senior leadership decision-making
  • Facilitate the development and maintenance of market, product, and portfolio roadmaps, ensuring clear prioritization and alignment across key stakeholders

Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Engineering, Business, Economics, or a related field
  • Relevant experience in renewable energy, electric utilities, industrial manufacturing, management consulting, strategy, product management, portfolio management, or business performance management
  • Experience developing business cases, investment evaluations, financial analysis, or strategic planning materials
  • Strong analytical capabilities with the ability to transform data into actionable business insights
  • Experience coordinating complex initiatives across multiple stakeholders and functions
  • Excellent communication and presentation skills with the ability to structure information for senior leadership audiences

Competencies

  • Strategic thinker with a strong customer and market orientation
  • Demonstrated ownership mentality with a focus on delivering measurable business results
  • Ability to lead through influence, creating alignment and momentum without formal authority
  • Strong decision-making capability, balancing data, business judgement, and customer value
  • Collaborative and resilient leader who thrives in complexity and drives progress through ambiguity

What we offer

This is a unique opportunity to influence and elevate product management practices within one of the world's leading renewable energy companies. You will gain direct exposure to strategic portfolio decisions while partnering with senior leaders across product, technology, commercial, and operational teams.

The role offers the chance to establish and enhance the frameworks, governance models, and ways of working that drive long-term business growth and deliver sustainable customer value. Working in a global, highly collaborative environment, you will be encouraged to demonstrate initiative, take ownership, and contribute to a culture of continuous improvement.

Guided by our core values of Collaboration, Simplicity, Accountability, and Passion, we foster an inclusive workplace where diverse perspectives are embraced and innovation thrives through collective expertise and shared ambition.

About Vestas

Vestas is the energy industry’s global partner on sustainable energy solutions. We are specialised in designing, manufacturing, installing, and servicing wind turbines, both onshore and offshore.

Across the globe, we have installed more wind power than anyone else. We consider ourselves pioneers within the industry, as we continuously aim to design new solutions and technologies to create a more sustainable future for all of us. With more than 200 GW of wind power installed worldwide and 40+ years of experience in wind energy, we have an unmatched track record demonstrating our expertise within the field.

With 39,000 employees globally, we are a diverse team united by a common goal: to power the solution – today, tomorrow, and far into the future.
