**Team Leader | Full-Time  | Field Operations**

Join Vestas and Help Build the Future of Renewable Energy

At Vestas, we are a leading force in wind energy, committed to advancing the transition toward a sustainable future. We are looking for a Supervisor to join our team in Lussac Les Châteaux and play a key role in ensuring operational excellence, team performance, and safety on site.

Your Role

As a Supervisor, you will be responsible for leading and coordinating a team of technicians and operators while ensuring high-quality service delivery, compliance with safety standards, and operational efficiency.

You will act as a key link between field teams, internal stakeholders, and customers, ensuring smooth execution of maintenance activities and continuous improvement of site performance.

Key Responsibilities

Lead and coordinate daily operations of the service team

Ensure adherence to Vestas processes, safety standards, and quality requirements

Monitor team performance and promote continuous improvement

Support the development and training of team members, including safety initiatives

Manage workforce planning and ensure resource availability

Oversee reporting, time tracking, and task execution accuracy

Collaborate with internal teams, customers, and external partners

Ensure compliance with local regulations and company policies

What We’re Looking For

Demonstrated background leading teams in operational or industrial settings

Solid technical understanding of mechanical systems or wind turbine operations

A safety-first mindset and commitment to high standards

Clear and effective verbal and written communication abilities

Comfortable working in a changing environment and prioritizing tasks efficiently

Methodical approach and problem-solving abilities

Proficiency with digital tools (MS Office; SAP or similar systems)

Well-developed capability to manage personal responsibilities, accommodate changing requirements, and maintain order

Why Join Vestas?

Be part of a global leader in renewable energy

Work in an engaging and purpose-driven environment

Access to continuous training and development opportunities

Grow your leadership skills within an international organization

About Vestas

Vestas is the energy industry’s global partner on sustainable energy solutions. We are specialised in designing, manufacturing, installing, and servicing wind turbines, both onshore and offshore.

Across the globe, we have installed more wind power than anyone else. We consider ourselves pioneers within the industry, as we continuously aim to design new solutions and technologies to create a more sustainable future for all of us. With more than 185 GW of wind power installed worldwide and 40+ years of experience in wind energy, we have an unmatched track record demonstrating our expertise within the field.

With 30,000 employees globally, we are a diverse team united by a common goal: to power the solution – today, tomorrow, and far into the future.
