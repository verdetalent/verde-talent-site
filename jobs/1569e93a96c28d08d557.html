Service Administrative Support team (SAS) is part of the Global Capacity Center (GCC), and is responsible for supporting the commercial administrative process in MED region.

Responsibilities

  • Interface with Service Sales Teams in the MED region
  • Ensure an efficient flow of information
  • Support the commercial cycle through the SAP system
  • Recovery Management
  • Prepare business reports based on the Service performance
  • Enhance the data quality of the commercial system

Qualifications

  • Administrative/finance background / a Degree
  • Fluent in English, oral and written communication (Mandatory)
  • Be initiative-driven, highly motivated, and processes-oriented
  • Skilled IT user, including Microsoft Office
  • Intermediate Excel user

Competencies

  • Works well with others in an international setting
  • Good understanding of business process improvements
  • Good interpersonal cooperation skills and a customer mindset attitude
  • Good communication
  • Believe in and comply to our values of Collaboration, Simplicity and Accountability
