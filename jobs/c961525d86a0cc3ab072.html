Service, Construction Academy and Tools Department is part of the Services Organization in Latin America. Our focuses on providing solutions in the areas of materials and components supply, repair loop and tools to maximize the efficiency of the wind turbine fleet serviced in the region Responsibilities: Manage WH - Tools stock; Ensure that tool transfer requests are processed in a timely manner; Manage material separation team; Coordinate maintenance of all tools in the region; Coordinate inspections of WH tools and sites in the region. Qualifications: A higher degree in engineering (mechanical, electrical, production or similar) is desirable; Technical course (mechanics, electrical, metallurgy or similar). At least 3 years of experience with maintenance work; Experience working with renewable energy is desirable. Experience with SAP desirable. For internal candidates, you have been in the same role and/or at Vestas for at least one year when you apply.
