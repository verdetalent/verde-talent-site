Job Description

As a Maintenance Helper, you will provide janitorial services, help maintain the warehouse, assist O&M Techs with maintenance activities and other activities at the plant as needed. The position requires demonstrated ability to work in a team environment with proven interpersonal skills and professionalism in dealing with management, co-workers and vendors. Must be able to handle multiple assignments and be a self-starter.

Responsibilities:

  • Assist O&M Techs performing maintenance on plant equipment
  • Work within and ensure strict adherence to all safety and environmental procedures
  • Assists with stocking, maintaining warehouse consumable and parts inventory and cleans and keeps warehouse orderly and professional
  • Operate plant trucks to make deliveries and procure supplies and equipment from local vendors as needed
  • Operate plant forklift to load, unload, move and store equipment, parts and supplies safely following established procedures
  • Operate filter press and other water treatment systems as qualified and directed by Operations
  • Perform minor and routine repairs and preventative maintenance on plant vehicles and other equipment as qualified
  • Clean plant equipment to maintain station professional appearance using a variety of cleaning supplies including pressure washer
  • Perform all janitorial cleaning services for the plant
  • Assist with quarterly inventory process as needed
  • Outage support with material handling, boxing/crate fabrication for parts install and shipping
  • Assists with special events set up as needed
  • Possibility of overtime

Requirements

  • High school diploma or equivalent
  • Valid driver’s license and acceptable driving record
  • Prior experience operating in an industrial setting
  • Eligible to work in the United States without the need for employer visa sponsorship now or in the future

​

Preferred Requirements

  • Forklift Operator experience
  • Computer proficiency with Microsoft Office software.
