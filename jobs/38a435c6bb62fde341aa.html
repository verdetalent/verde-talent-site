Eversource Energy
Dorchester, MA Full-time Individual Contributor $47.26/hr

Full description

Eversource will not offer immigration-related sponsorship for this position (e.g., H-1B, O-1, J-1, TN, E-3, etc.).  Applicants requiring visa sponsorship to start employment with Eversource will not be considered.

Eversource is looking for a talented and motivated Diesel/Automotive technician to join our Fleet team. We offer competitive pay, training, paid CDL training, 401K, vacation, and a great work life balance culture!

Training Location: Wareham, MA or Dorchester, MA.  (training period is 6 months)

(permanent location determined after training period)

Department: Fleet Maintenance

Starting Pay Rate: $47.26/hr.   Upgraded to $57.61/hr. after completion of 6-month training period. Shift differential where applicable.

Schedule:  Various Shifts

Technician Duties and Responsibilities

  • Must be able to trouble shoot, perform inspections and make repairs on chassis, gas/diesel engines, drive trains, rear axles, steering mechanisms, hydraulic/air braking systems, suspensions, cooling systems, wheels, tires, and repair associated construction equipment.
  • Perform scheduled and unscheduled repairs and preventive maintenance as assigned.
  • Safely handle standard garage materials, chemicals, and maintain a clean & safe work area.
  • Accurately document work using Chevin maintenance program
  • Able to work independently either in the assigned shop or at other locations within the limits of the classification.

QUALIFICATIONS:

  • CDL Driver’s and Inspectors license preferred.  Must be able to obtain within six months.
  • High School diploma or equivalent
  • Completion of a postsecondary program in automotive service technology a plus
  • Must be able obtain valid health certificate.
  • Experience with electronic diagnostic software and instrument systems preferred.
  • Must possess a working knowledge of and the ability to use a tablet or a desktop computer.
  • Must have the ability to diagnose problems in automotive/truck equipment and to make the necessary repairs to return the equipment to safe and operable condition.
  • Must be familiar with the use and safe operation of torches and gas welding equipment.
