With one of the nation’s most fuel-efficient, cost-effective power generation fleets and industry-leading reliability, we’re redefining what’s possible in energy. Want to be part of something powerful? Join our outstanding team and help shape the future of energy. Position Specific Description Everyday our employees help us deliver safe, affordable, and reliable service to our more than 4.9 million customers. They are helping shape our company and how our customers see us. This position will report to a Regional Plant General Manager and will be responsible for driving best in class Safety and environmental performance, responsible for all aspects of operations and maintenance to maximize plant reliability and development of high-performance teams to support 24/7 operations. Dependent on candidate qualifications and business need, the successful candidate will be based out of one of our Florida power plants. The successful candidate for this position shall be able to manage all aspects of site business and operations, achieving all production and business targets while maintaining an injury-free work site. Combined cycle power plant operations experience required. Preferred Qualifications: Strong knowledge and experience within power plant operations, maintenance and troubleshooting Six Sigma Certification; Yellow Belt or higher Bachelor's in engineering Job Overview Leaders in this role oversee a team at a generation facility. Individuals are accountable for all aspects of the management and performance of both the physical and human assets in their assigned facility.
