Full description

Do you thrive at the intersection of finance, strategy, and leadership? Do you enjoy guiding business decisions, testing assumptions, and converting financial analysis into practical next steps?

Finance > Power Solution Finance > R&D Finance

Based in Aarhus, the R&D Finance team is a strategic finance partner to Vestas' R&D organization, enabling informed decision-making through financial business partnering, performance management, and investment transparency.

Working closely with R&D leaders and specialists, the team provides financial insights, challenges assumptions, and supports prioritization across the R&D portfolio to maximize value creation and ensure efficient allocation of resources.

As a Finance Business Partner, you will support a Senior Vice President area and be an integrated part of the leadership team you partner with. Through financial planning, forecasting, performance management, and business case evaluations, you will help shape priorities, drive transparency, and enable informed decisions across Vestas' product development portfolio.

Responsibilities

As a Finance Business Partner, you can expect to undertake the following activities:

  • Act as a trusted financial business partner for R&D senior stakeholders across the value chain to ensure financially sound decision making
  • Deliver future-oriented financial insights that support timely decisions instead of focusing only on past reporting
  • Promote sound financial governance and higher-quality decisions across product development programs and Program Board forums
  • Ensure transparency around project performance, value creation, spending, forecasting, and key performance indicators
  • Use financial analysis and business knowledge to evaluate assumptions, priorities, and investment decisions
  • Oversee the creation and ongoing refinement of business cases that inform product and portfolio decisions

Qualifications

We expect that you can reflect yourself in the following criteria:

  • Master's degree in Finance, Economics, Business Administration, or a related field
  • 5+ years of experience within Finance Business Partnering, Performance Management, FP&A, Strategy or Consulting
  • Demonstrated skill in shaping decisions and collaborating effectively with senior stakeholders and key decision-makers
  • Experience supporting complex projects, portfolios, or product development organizations
  • Advanced capabilities in financial modeling, business case preparation, and analytical work

Competencies

Furthermore, we hope that you can nod in recognition of the following competencies:

  • You build trust and credibility with stakeholders at all organizational levels
  • You regularly question assumptions and encourage constructive discussion
  • You accept responsibility and see actions through to completion
  • You can translate data into clear business recommendations
  • You perform effectively in intricate, evolving settings
  • You are adept at combining analytical abilities with effective communication and stakeholder management

What we offer

We provide an engaging role with plenty of room for development in a motivating work environment. You will join an international environment that prioritizes development and collaboration among colleagues.

About Vestas

Vestas is the energy industry’s global partner on sustainable energy solutions. We are specialised in designing, manufacturing, installing, and servicing wind turbines, both onshore and offshore.

Across the globe, we have installed more wind power than anyone else. We consider ourselves pioneers within the industry, as we continuously aim to design new solutions and technologies to create a more sustainable future for all of us. With more than 200 GW of wind power installed worldwide and 40+ years of experience in wind energy, we have an unmatched track record demonstrating our expertise within the field.

With 39,000 employees globally, we are a diverse team united by a common goal: to power the solution – today, tomorrow, and far into the future.

Vestas promotes a diverse workforce which embraces all social identities and is free of any discrimination. We commit to create and sustain an environment that acknowledges and harvests different experiences, skills, and perspectives. We also aim to give everyone equal access to opportunity.
