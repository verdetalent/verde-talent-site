Looking for meaning at work ?

Join Vestas and contribute to achieving a de-carbonized future.

Boost your career in an international and innovative renewable energy company in a group with more than 30,000 employees worldwide.

EVP MED / Region MED / Area Site Management Rennes

Your mission will be to optimize our client’s wind farms during their life cycle, ensuring the maximum capacity of the wind turbines

Responsibilities:

You will have a key role in ensuring the satisfaction of our customers over the long term. Working in pairs, you will report to the Area Supervisor and will be in charge of:

Carry out the curative maintenance of the turbines, in order to optimize the performance of the turbines to the benefit of our clients, following the Quality, Safety and Environment standards.

Ensure a smooth operation of our turbines and control the operation of the different elements of the wind turbine at all stages of intervention

Carry out preventive maintenance of the turbines according to current rules or work instructions, (complete refurbishment of turbines). You will be reporting your interventions.

Qualifications:

BTEC Higher National Diploma, BTS/DUT industrial or Electrical / Mechanical Engineering certified by the BZEE certification if possible

Ideally 2 to 5 years of experience in industrial maintenance or electricity, with knowledge of high voltage

Knowledge of technical English (written/spoken) and familiar with the Office Pack.

Competencies:

Enthusiastic contact with customers regarding completed and upcoming service and repairs Motivated, autonomous, results-oriented, innovative, mobility required because trips are planned for the activity

Good communication, team spirit

Solid commitment to the concept of safety in general and safety in the work environment, ability to work at height.

We offer:

An exciting job in a significant and international environment, with 40 years of experience.

Career perspectives: 4 levels of technicians: technician, expert technician, senior technician, and Leader Technician

A secure environment that will protect you while working in the field, as well as the best specific protection equipment on the market thanks to strict security rules

Executive status, additional days off (12 to 15 days per year, depending on the year), meals taken care off by Vestas, incentive bonus and profit-sharing schemes, benefits from the advantages of the works council (gift vouchers)
