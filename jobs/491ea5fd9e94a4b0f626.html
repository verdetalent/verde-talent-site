Project Coordinator (Delivery)

Location: Telferton, Edinburgh (with travel across the district as required)

Salary: £38,760 plus excellent benefits and career progression opportunities

Closing Date: 13th September 2026

Help us create a better future, quicker

At SP Energy Networks, we're playing a vital role in delivering the electricity network that will power a cleaner, greener future. As part of the Iberdrola Group, we're investing billions in our network infrastructure to support Net Zero ambitions and connect homes, businesses and communities to reliable, sustainable energy.

We're looking for a Project Coordinator (Delivery) to join our Edinburgh and Borders team. This is an exciting opportunity to play a key role in delivering operational, investment and customer projects across the electricity distribution network.

Working closely with customers, stakeholders, contractors and operational teams, you'll help coordinate the successful delivery of a diverse portfolio of projects, ensuring safety, customer service, regulatory compliance and business performance remain at the heart of everything you do.

If you're an organised and proactive individual with project delivery experience and a passion for making a difference, we'd love to hear from you.

What you'll be doing

  • Supporting the delivery of operational, investment and customer projects across the distribution network.
  • Managing project activities to ensure delivery against financial, regulatory and business objectives.
  • Developing and coordinating project plans that minimise disruption to customers and local communities.
  • Building strong relationships with customers, contractors, local authorities and key stakeholders to support successful project outcomes.
  • Supporting the achievement of key performance targets including Customer Minutes Lost (CML), Customer Interruptions (CI), Customer Satisfaction, Guaranteed Standards and Time to Connect.
  • Coordinating contractor activities to ensure works are delivered safely, efficiently and to the required quality standards.
  • Managing project and contract administration activities throughout the project lifecycle.
  • Supporting the delivery of maintenance, reliability and network security programmes.
  • Analysing performance data and feedback to identify opportunities for continuous improvement.
  • Applying sound engineering and commercial judgement to resolve challenges and support decision-making.
  • Promoting a strong safety culture and ensuring compliance with all health, safety and environmental requirements.
  • Supporting the delivery of capital investment projects that enhance network performance, resilience and customer service.

What you'll bring

  • Experience coordinating or delivering projects within utilities, infrastructure, engineering, construction or a similar industry.
  • Strong planning and organisational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.
  • Customer-focused approach with the ability to build positive relationships at all levels.
  • Commercial awareness and an understanding of balancing customer, operational and business needs.
  • Ability to analyse information and identify practical solutions to challenges.
  • Technical or operational experience within an engineering environment would be advantageous.
  • Knowledge of regulatory or performance-driven environments would be beneficial.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications and IT systems.

Minimum Criteria

  • Craft qualification, apprenticeship, S/NVQ or equivalent experience.
  • Experience in a project delivery or project coordination role.
  • Relevant operational, construction or infrastructure experience.
  • Strong IT and computer literacy skills.

Why SP Energy Networks

SP Energy Networks is part of the Iberdrola Group, one of the world’s largest integrated utility companies and a world leader in wind energy. We keep electricity flowing to homes and businesses through Central and Southern Scotland, North Wales and in the North West of England. We operate over 4000km of cables and lines that make-up the transmission network – connecting infrastructure like wind farms into the electricity system. It’s a role that puts us right at the heart of Scotland’s ambition to be Net Zero by 2044. And we’re taking it very seriously.

With diverse opportunities across our businesses and a commitment to invest in our own internal talent, ScottishPower can offer people real career opportunities that meet personal and professional goals, in a global organisation.

Inclusion, diversity, and a social purpose are at the heart of everything we do. Together with our values, they bring us together into a stronger, more sustainable business with direct links to the communities we serve. It takes all kinds of people to build a large-scale business like ours, so whatever your background, you’ll fit right in.
