Full description

Fluence (Nasdaq: FLNC) is a global market leader delivering intelligent energy storage and optimization software for renewables and storage. Our solutions and operational services are helping to create a more resilient grid and unlock the full potential of renewable portfolios. With gigawatts of successful implementations across nearly 50 markets, we are transforming the way we power our world for a more sustainable future. For more information, please visit fluenceenergy.com.

Job Description:

The Business Development Representative is responsible for generating and qualifying new business opportunities, developing early-stage customer relationships, supporting opportunity progression, and contributing to Fluence’s commercial growth strategy. The role works closely with Sales, Marketing, Product, Account Management, and other cross-functional teams to identify prospective customers, develop pipeline opportunities, and strengthen Fluence’s position within the energy storage and renewable energy markets.

Business Development & Lead Generation

  • Identify, engage, and qualify prospective customers across target markets and customer segments.
  • Execute outbound prospecting activities and manage inbound leads to generate qualified sales opportunities.
  • Collaborate with Sales, Marketing, and Growth teams to develop and execute strategic outreach campaigns.
  • Establish and nurture relationships with key industry stakeholders, decision-makers, developers, utilities, EPC partners, and other customers.
  • Represent Fluence at industry conferences, trade shows, customer events, and networking opportunities.

Opportunity Qualification & Pipeline Management

  • Conduct discovery meetings to understand customer needs, project requirements, procurement processes, timelines, and decision-making structures.
  • Assess opportunities based on project scope, commercial viability, readiness, and strategic fit.
  • Maintain accurate and up-to-date customer and opportunity records within Salesforce.
  • Prepare opportunity assessments and contribute to internal discussions regarding opportunity prioritization and resource allocation.
  • Support the development of indicative pricing, preliminary proposals, and high-level scopes of work in collaboration with cross-functional teams.
  • Ensure seamless handover of qualified opportunities to Account Management teams while maintaining continuity of information.

Relationship Management & Market Intelligence

  • Deliver compelling introductory presentations and value propositions that position Fluence as a trusted energy storage solutions partner.
  • Support strategic customer engagement initiatives alongside Account Managers.
  • Maintain regular communication with prospects to advance opportunities through the sales cycle.
  • Gather and share market intelligence on customer trends, competitor activity, policy developments, and market dynamics impacting investment decisions.
  • Provide insights and feedback to Product, Marketing, and Strategy teams to strengthen go-to-market initiatives and messaging.

Cross-Functional Collaboration

  • Partner closely with Sales, Marketing, Product, Delivery, Legal, Finance, and Technical teams to support business objectives and ensure governance compliance.
  • Participate in bid planning, opportunity reviews, and sales strategy discussions.
  • Build relationships with channel partners, industry associations, and key ecosystem stakeholders.
  • Contribute to the continuous improvement of business development processes, sales methodologies, and supporting tools.

Qualifications & Experience

  • Proven experience in Business Development, Sales Development, Lead Generation, Account Development, or a similar commercial role.
  • Strong ability to identify, qualify, and develop business opportunities.
  • Excellent communication, presentation, networking, and relationship-building skills.
  • Experience working with CRM platforms, preferably Salesforce.
  • Strong organizational and stakeholder management skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities effectively.
  • Commercially focused with strong analytical and problem-solving capabilities.
  • Ability to thrive in a collaborative, fast-paced, and cross-functional environment.
  • Knowledge of the energy, renewable energy, battery energy storage systems (BESS), utilities, or infrastructure sectors is highly desirable.
  • Willingness to travel as needed to support customer engagements and industry events.

Education & Professional Development

  • Bachelor’s degree in business, Marketing, Engineering, Energy Management, Economics, or a related field.
  • Equivalent industry experience in business development, sales, renewable energy, energy storage, or related sectors may be considered in lieu of formal qualifications.
  • Training or certifications in sales methodologies, business development, or CRM systems are advantageous.
  • Demonstrated commitment to ongoing professional development within renewable energy, energy storage technologies, and commercial sales practices.
