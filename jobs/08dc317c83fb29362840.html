Full description

As part of Vestas Global Finance Shared Service Centre, the role holds the primary responsibility of delivering standardized, rule-based services to the region, specifically encompassing the full spectrum of Intercompany Reconciliation processes. The A2R Analyst II works with the Team Leader and/or Manager in overseeing and offering assistance in the broader context of Intercompany Reconciliation operations.

SSC > Finance SSC > Controlling and Reporting

Responsibilities:

  • Act as a subject matter expert (SME) in Intercompany (ICO) processes and serve as the 1st level point of escalation for daily production issues and concerns
  • Manage the end-to-end Intercompany reconciliation processes to ensure accuracy and consistency
  • Investigate and resolve complex intercompany reconciliation issues, collaborating with stakeholders as needed
  • Oversee the maintenance of organized records and documentation related to intercompany transactions and reconciliation
  • Provide guidance and mentorship to junior reconciliation staff to support their professional development
  • Participate in continuous improvement and optimization efforts for Finance and Accounting procedures related to Intercompany Reconciliation
  • Responsible for consolidating and monitoring non-standard processes and Intercompany Reconciliation activities that have not yet been migrated to the Shared Services Center (SSC)
  • Support or participate in ICO reconciliation projects, overseeing their effective implementation
  • Manage the ICO Process/Project Pillar, ensuring its effective management and execution
  • Manage the resolution of errors within the overall Intercompany reconciliation function to maintain accuracy and integrity
  • Promptly engage and collaborate with Regional Finance, Treasury, Global Sourcing, and other business stakeholders to swiftly address operational issues and challenges with high quality
  • Promptly escalate issues to supervisors or managers when intervention or clarification is required from senior leadership
  • Review, consolidate, and present Intercompany operational reports and monthly decks, ensuring data accuracy
  • Conduct analytical reviews to guarantee the achievement of Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) and Process Performance Indicators (PPIs) for the Intercompany reconciliation function
  • Monthly review and validate KPI results
  • Monitor the back-up system and update TL if there are some challenges with the back-up system
  • Review and finalize Intercompany reconciliation SOX documentation, ensuring its accurate and timely submission in SAP GRC
  • Investigate, communicate, and resolve control issues, working to prevent their recurrence, and escalate identified risks as necessary
  • Perform peer-to-peer reviews of Intercompany reconciliation SOX documentation to ensure compliance
  • Provide internal and external audit support as requested, ensuring compliance with audit requirements
  • Monitor and ensure the timely completion of tasks according to the Intercompany reconciliation Task list
  • Participate in Intercompany reconciliation monthly operational calls with stakeholders to maintain effective communication
  • Ensure that Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) are reviewed and updated on a quarterly basis to reflect current processes
  • Provide support during the monthly accounting close process and address critical business partner issues
  • Contribute to effective process improvement and standardization initiatives, aligning with Vestas Global policies
  • Develop training materials aligned with the Intercompany curriculum and conduct training sessions within the reconciliation team and other ICO functions

Qualifications:

  • Possession of a bachelor's degree in accountancy or a related accounting field.
  • Demonstrated experience of at least three to four (3-4) years in Intercompany accounting.
  • Familiarity with SAP and proficiency in MS Office tools.
  • Candidates should be able to solve problems effectively, make informed decisions, and communicate ideas well in presentations.
  • Effective English communication skills, both written and verbal.
  • Ability to work flexible schedules, including holidays and various shifts as needed.
  • Capacity to work effectively with minimal supervision.
  • Exhibit a professional attitude and attitude while maintaining the ability to collaborate effectively with diverse personalities.

Competencies:

  • Open to working on holidays as needed and available for any shift assignment
  • With a professional attitude and demeanor
  • Can blend well to different kind of personalities
  • Ability to lead and work collaboratively with cross-functional teams
  • Proficiency in Intercompany accounting processes and reconciliation procedures
  • Demonstrated analytical and problem-solving abilities to identify and resolve complex issues
  • Effective communication skills to collaborate with various stakeholders effectively
  • Ability to oversee and manage reconciliation projects from inception to completion
  • Meticulous attention to detail to ensure data accuracy and compliance with documentation
  • In-depth understanding of accounting principles and practices, especially in the context of intercompany transactions
  • Capacity to handle evolving reconciliation requirements and address complex reconciliation challenges
  • A mindset for continuous improvement and optimization of processes
  • Ability to assess and manage risks associated with Intercompany reconciliation
  • Collaborative mindset to work effectively within a team and across departments
  • Good time management skills to prioritize tasks and meet deadlines
  • Maintain a positive and focused approach to reconciliation tasks and challenges
