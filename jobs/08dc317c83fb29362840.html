Full description
As part of Vestas Global Finance Shared Service Centre, the role holds the primary responsibility of delivering standardized, rule-based services to the region, specifically encompassing the full spectrum of Intercompany Reconciliation processes. The A2R Analyst II works with the Team Leader and/or Manager in overseeing and offering assistance in the broader context of Intercompany Reconciliation operations.
