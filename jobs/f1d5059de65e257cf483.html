A DAY IN THE LIFE

  • Leading safety and quality initiatives at a site level
  • Developing, reviewing, and managing owner contracts and subcontracts
  • Managing costs and scheduling labor, materials, tools, equipment, and subcontractors
  • Constant communication with the owner/client about progress and conflict management
  • Traveling to the jobsite (minimum 50% required travel)

WHAT YOU’LL NEED

  • Experience managing self-perform construction projects with a minimum budget of $30M
  • Exceptional customer and client relation skills
  • Knowledge of schedule and budget creation, control, and monitoring
  • Contract and subcontract development and management experience
  • Valid driver’s license with an acceptable motor vehicle driving record (MVR)

WHAT WILL SET YOU APART

  • Financial forecasting experience (estimates to and at completion, cash, and cost flow)
  • A proven record of safety program management and implementation
  • Knowledge of Standard Word Instructions (SWIs), and controlling/implementing sitewide quality programs effectiveness

WHO ARE WE?

With more than 115 years of construction experience, the company has played a vital role in building America’s critical infrastructure, ranging from railroads and highways to dams and mines. Since 1997, Blattner has focused on renewable energy solutions and is responsible for some of the most significant solar, wind and storage projects in the U.S. Today, Blattner has installed more than 25% of America’s utility-scale wind, solar and energy storage capacity. Blattner Company is the parent company of Blattner Energy and D.H. Blattner & Sons, of which all are members of the Quanta Services family of companies. Company headquarters are in Avon, Minnesota.
