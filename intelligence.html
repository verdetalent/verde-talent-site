How this is built. We track 80 companies and read their job pages every day; 44 have US roles open right now. Pay is what those postings advertise, not offers made or salaries paid. The workforce map is the U.S. Energy & Employment Report's county data (2026 edition), rolled up by state, because BLS withholds those figures for 28 states to protect employer confidentiality; planned capacity is the EIA generator inventory (july 2026), solar, wind and storage only. Pay vs market divides each advertised salary by the market median for that same role, so it is not moved by seniority mix, and needs 8+ postings across 3+ roles. Government wage and workforce figures are BLS OEWS (2025) and QCEW (2026Q1). Rebuilt every morning; last built September 21, 2026.
