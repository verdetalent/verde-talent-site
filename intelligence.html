How this is built. Job figures come from the career sites of the employers we track, read daily; pay is what those postings advertise, not offers made or salaries paid. Workforce size and government pay figures are BLS Occupational Employment & Wage Statistics (2025); industry concentration is BLS QCEW (2026Q1), which is also the workforce layer on the map - people employed in solar, wind and storage generation and in battery manufacturing. The roles layer is our own coverage: the career sites we read, not every employer in the country. Planned capacity is the EIA generator inventory (july 2026). Tightness is only shown where BLS publishes that exact occupation and at least 200 people hold it in the state - BLS suppresses small cells, so some states show no figure rather than a misleading one.
