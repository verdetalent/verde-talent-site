Our story

The clean energy industry is growing faster than almost any other part of the U.S. economy — but the hiring infrastructure for it hasn't caught up. Job seekers with hard-won energy credentials are stuck applying on generic boards built around oil & gas and tech. Employers hiring for solar, wind, storage, and grid roles are stuck posting into the same crowded feeds as every other industry.
Verde Talent is a brand-new hiring platform built to fix that gap — job postings pulled directly from clean energy employers' own career pages, organized around the roles and sectors that actually make up this industry, paired with real labor-market data (compensation, hiring trends, fill-difficulty) sourced from public data including the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics.
Right now, that's a solo effort: one person building the product, running the job crawler, and talking to every company and candidate directly. It's early days — the goal is to grow this into the default hiring layer for the energy transition.

Our values
Planet first
Every decision we make runs through a simple filter: does this help more clean energy get built? We're not neutral on climate — we're explicitly on the side of the transition.
Transparency
We show salary ranges on every job and publish our own hiring and market data in the open, including in our Intelligence reports. As this grows, that stays true — no black boxes.
Accessibility
Clean energy jobs shouldn't only be findable by people with the right LinkedIn network. Verde Talent is free for job seekers, always, and designed to surface opportunities regardless of background.
